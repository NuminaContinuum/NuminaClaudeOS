# /yin-yang-questionnaire — The Yin & Yang (Neidan) questionnaire

One skill for the inner-alchemy assessment. It runs in stages and knows where the user is:

- **Q1 — Quick placement** (3-5 min). Places the user at their current stage on the 5-stage path of refining essence → energy → spirit → void. Confidence after Q1: **rough**.
- **Q2 — Full stage profile** (10-12 min). Resolves a state per stage and runs over-rating detection (the somatic checks). Confidence after Q2: **deeper**.
- **Q3 — Settled reading** (20-30 min). A long, open-ended reading settling the full profile, the transition the user is at, and the practice ahead. Confidence after Q3: **settled**.

The canonical content for all 5 stages (essence, yin/yang qualities, markers, gift, pitfall, over-rating diagnostics, evidence signals) lives in `yin-yang-framework.md`, in this skill's folder. Both this questionnaire and `/maps yin-yang` draw from it — adapt the tone to where the user actually is; do not quote it verbatim.

The map itself is generated by `/maps yin-yang`. This skill feeds it structured readings and offers to refresh it afterward.

**Core principle — where you are, not who you are.** Carry this through every reading: a deeply held Foundation is a real stage with its own gift, never a lower self. The map shows presence at the current stage, not a race to the summit.

**The 5 stages (3 phases):** Laying the Foundation (築基) · Refining Essence into Energy (煉精化氣) · Refining Energy into Spirit (煉氣化神) · Refining Spirit, Returning to Emptiness (煉神還虛) · Merging with the Dao (煉虛合道). Phases: Foundation → Refinement → Return. Stages are progressive but a glimpse of a later stage does not mean an earlier one is complete.

**Questions stay in plain experiential language.** No Chinese terms, no stage names in the answer options. The classical terms (characters + pinyin + translation) appear only on the result screen, translated names primary, exactly as the other tradition maps handle their source vocabulary.

---

## Step 0 — Figure out where the user is

Read `context-library/maps/yin-yang.md` if it exists and check which stages have run (`## Q1 Reading`, `## Q2 Reading`). Route:

- **Nothing done yet** → run Q1.
- **Q1 done, Q2 not** → offer Q2: *"You've got the quick placement. Ready to go deeper? The next reflection (about ten minutes) shows the whole path — which stages are alive in you, what's beginning to open, and whether the stage you'd name matches the energy you can actually feel."* If they decline, stop gracefully.
- **Q1 and Q2 done, Q3 not** → offer Q3: *"You've seen the full profile. The long reading (20-30 minutes) settles it in your own words — what your practice actually looks like, the transition you're at, and the next step. Want to do it now, or another time?"* If they decline, stop gracefully.
- **All three done** → offer to re-run a stage for a fresh read, or refresh the map from everything since.

Never push toward deeper stages. Q2 and Q3 are standalone-capable if the user asks for them directly. Honour an explicit stage request.

## Q1 — Quick placement

### Q1 Step 1 — Open

Present this intro, then begin immediately.

> *Where are you on the path of inner alchemy?*
>
> *The Taoist path maps a transformation: refining your essence into energy, energy into spirit, and spirit into the open emptiness from which yin and yang first divided. Five stages, from laying the foundation through the gradual reconciliation of every opposite into the undivided Dao.*
>
> *This reads where your practice and energy actually are right now — not where you hope they are, and not who you are. A foundation deeply held is its own accomplishment.*
>
> *Eight short questions. Pick the closest fit.*

### Q1 Step 2 — Present the questions

One at a time. Wait for each answer. Accept the letter only. **No commentary between questions.** Option order is shuffled per question and stage names are hidden, so the result can't be gamed. **The parenthetical tag is for your scoring only — never show it to the user.**

**1. When you turn inward in practice these days, the typical experience is closest to:**
(a) My body and mind are restless. The work is settling down, regulating the breath, building a steady baseline *(Foundation)*
(b) I can feel energy moving — warmth, current, aliveness in the body. The work is circulating and refining it *(Refining Essence→Energy)*
(c) The energy has settled into a deep stillness. The breath is barely there. Something luminous is gathering *(Refining Energy→Spirit)*
(d) The sense of a separate "me" thins out. There's a vast spaciousness, an emptiness, an opening *(Refining Spirit→Emptiness)*
(e) There's no separation to maintain. Inner and outer, self and world, move as one *(Merging with the Dao)*

**2. Your relationship to your own energy — the felt aliveness in the body:**
(a) I can feel it distinctly, and I'm learning to move it, circulate it, refine it *(Refining Essence→Energy)*
(b) It's stabilised into something quieter and more spirit-like. Less "energy," more presence *(Refining Energy→Spirit)*
(c) It's dissolving into spaciousness. The energy and the emptiness are no longer separate *(Refining Spirit→Emptiness)*
(d) There's nothing to manage. It moves itself *(Merging with the Dao)*
(e) Mostly I'm trying to stop leaking it — conserving, resting, not depleting myself *(Foundation)*

**3. The breath in your practice:**
(a) Slow and deep. Sometimes I feel it connect with a current that circulates through the body *(Refining Essence→Energy)*
(b) So subtle it almost stops. Breathing seems to happen on its own, faintly, from somewhere deep *(Refining Energy→Spirit)*
(c) Barely present. I'm more aware of space than of breathing *(Refining Spirit→Emptiness)*
(d) Not something I notice as separate at all *(Merging with the Dao)*
(e) Irregular — something I'm actively regulating and lengthening *(Foundation)*

**4. What "yin and yang" feels like in your inner life right now:**
(a) Beginning to circulate together. I can feel opposites starting to communicate *(Refining Essence→Energy)*
(b) Merging. There's a felt union, a settledness where the opposites meet and quiet *(Refining Energy→Spirit)*
(c) Dissolving. The very distinction between them is thinning into one openness *(Refining Spirit→Emptiness)*
(d) Gone as a distinction. Only the undivided remains *(Merging with the Dao)*
(e) Scattered. My energies feel pulled in different directions, and I'm gathering them *(Foundation)*

**5. The biggest edge in your practice right now:**
(a) Working with the energy — opening the channels, circulating, not forcing *(Refining Essence→Energy)*
(b) Stabilising the stillness — letting energy settle into spirit without grasping *(Refining Energy→Spirit)*
(c) Letting go of even the subtle self — releasing into emptiness *(Refining Spirit→Emptiness)*
(d) There isn't an edge to name. It rests *(Merging with the Dao)*
(e) Consistency and groundedness — showing up, calming down, not depleting *(Foundation)*

**6. When you sit in stillness, what happens:**
(a) Stillness comes, and within it I feel energy and warmth begin to move *(Refining Essence→Energy)*
(b) A deep, luminous quiet opens. Time and the body recede *(Refining Energy→Spirit)*
(c) The "one who sits" gets very faint. There's mostly open space *(Refining Spirit→Emptiness)*
(d) There's no one sitting and nothing to attain. It's simply so *(Merging with the Dao)*
(e) My mind is busy. Stillness is brief and effortful *(Foundation)*

**7. The role of effort in your practice:**
(a) Active, skilled effort — guiding and circulating energy *(Refining Essence→Energy)*
(b) Effort softening — more allowing the settling than making it happen *(Refining Energy→Spirit)*
(c) Almost no effort — mostly letting go, releasing into space *(Refining Spirit→Emptiness)*
(d) No effort. Nothing is being done *(Merging with the Dao)*
(e) Constant, foundational effort — disciplining the body, the breath, the routine *(Foundation)*

**8. If you described where you are to a teacher, you'd say:**
(a) "I'm working with energy. The channels are opening, the circulation is beginning" *(Refining Essence→Energy)*
(b) "Energy is refining into spirit. The stillness is deep and the breath has nearly stopped" *(Refining Energy→Spirit)*
(c) "Spirit is returning to emptiness. The separate self is dissolving into space" *(Refining Spirit→Emptiness)*
(d) "There's nothing to refine. Yin and yang have returned to the One" *(Merging with the Dao)*
(e) "I'm laying the foundation — regulating body, breath, and mind, building a base" *(Foundation)*

### Q1 Step 3 — Score inline

Tally **stage content tags** (not letter positions) across Q1-Q8. The dominant stage is the user's current placement.

**Tie handling:** if two stages tie, resolve to the **earlier** stage (this is deliberate — inner alchemy inflates easily, so the conservative placement is the honest one). If the tie spans non-adjacent stages, present both readings and let the user pick the closer fit.

**Soft-calibration nudge (Q1 only, no auto-downgrade):** if the dominant stage is Refining Energy→Spirit or later but Q3 (breath) was *not* answered (b)/(c)/(d) (i.e. the breath is still active and regulated), add a gentle note suggesting an earlier stage may fit and recommending Q2 for a closer read.

### Q1 Step 4 — Render the result

**ASCII vertical ascent first**, current stage lit, a soft halo (◌) on the adjacent stage(s) — one back, one forward. The taiji motif transforms up the path. Adapt to the actual result:

```
   ⊙  Merging with the Dao        (empty circle — wújí)
   ◐  Refining Spirit → Emptiness
   ●  Refining Energy → Spirit     ← you
   ◌  Refining Essence → Energy
   ·  Laying the Foundation

   ●  current    ◌  adjacent    Return ▸ Refinement ▸ Foundation
```

Then the stage name (translated, primary) with its Chinese gloss + pinyin (secondary), and a 2-3 sentence reading grounded in the user's actual answers — the lived experience, the gift, the typical pitfall. Held as hypothesis. Draw on `yin-yang-framework.md` but adapt to what they said; do not restate generic content.

Example microcopy (Laying the Foundation):
> *You're **Laying the Foundation** (築基, Zhújī). The work right now is regulation — body, breath, and mind settling into a steady base, and the slow sealing of energy that usually leaks away. The gift here is groundedness. The pitfall is impatience: rushing toward circulating energy before the vessel can hold it. A foundation deeply held is not a lower stage. It's the ground everything else stands on.*

End with the confidence label and a CTA:

> *This is a rough placement — a first location, not a verdict. Q2 reveals which stages are alive in you, what's emerging, and whether the stage you'd name matches the energy you can actually feel. Confidence: rough.*

If the soft-calibration nudge fired, add:
> *One note: you placed at a later stage, but your breath is still active and regulated rather than nearly ceased. In inner alchemy the breath grows very subtle as energy refines into spirit, so an earlier stage may be the more honest fit. Q2 will look at this closely.*

### Q1 Step 5 — Write the Q1 reading


If the file does not exist, create it with this section and a note that the full map is generated by `/maps yin-yang`.

### Q1 Step 6 — Offer to refresh the map

After writing the Q1 reading, check whether a fuller Yin & Yang map already exists (content beyond the reading sections). If it does:

> *"You already have a Yin & Yang map. Want me to refresh it with this placement to raise its confidence? I'll merge what you just answered with what's already there from your notes and conversations."*

If yes, run the refresh (same logic as `/maps yin-yang`), merging the Q1 reading with existing harvested notes and prior readings. If no map exists yet, skip silently.

## Q3 — Long reading (20-30 min)

**Gate:** aware of Q1/Q2 (surface the latest reading if present); standalone-capable otherwise (Section 2 is the baseline). The longest read: a **settled** state per stage woven with the user's own words about what their practice and energy actually feel like. 22 questions across eight sections — open-ended (~45% of the signal), multiple-choice, and Likert. One at a time, no commentary between. **Tags are for your scoring only — never show them.** Shuffle option order. Voice input welcome on the open-ended questions; the user can pause and resume.

### Q3 Section 4 — Adaptive deep-dive (3 open-ended)

From the running profile select 3 prompts: **1 on the Current stage**, **1 on the most-active marker** (the felt circulation, the subtle breath, or over-rating doubt), **1 on the Emerging stage** (or a second Familiar stage).

- **Refining Energy into Spirit** — *"Describe the deep stillness. What is it like when energy settles into something quieter and more spirit-like? What does your breath do at its subtlest? - **Refining Spirit, Returning to Emptiness** — *"Describe the spaciousness. What is it like when the separate self thins out? What remains when 'you' recede? How does this differ from blanking out or spacing off? What does it ask of you when you return to ordinary life?"*
- **Merging with the Dao** — *"Describe your practice at its most undivided. What is it like when there's no separation to maintain, when yin and yang are simply one? How long does it last, how reliably does it arise, and what's the difference between an authentic taste of unity and a beautiful idea of it?"*
- **Over-rating self-inquiry** (if calibration flags triggered) — *"You've named a stage that may be ahead of where your practice currently lives. This is the most common pattern in inner-alchemy self-assessment — the later stages are vivid and the mind reaches for them. Take a breath. What in your actual felt experience genuinely supports the higher placement? What might be borrowed from what you've read, or a glimpse mistaken for a home? ### Q3 Step 8 — Resolve and render

- Combine Section 2 (state per stage), Section 3 Q9 (most diagnostic) + Q10 (over-rating check), Section 4 open-ended (deep texture → HIGH confidence on covered stages), Section 5 (orbit marker Q14 + transition Q15), Section 6 (emerging signal + map-identification), Section 7 calibration (depth × lineage caps the max-plausible stage). ## Tone reminders

- Wise, sincere, grounded. Witness before wisdom. These stages are easy to inflate — stay honest.
- No em dashes. No "unlock," "transform," "breakthrough," "enlightenment."
- Numina lexicon overlaps with neidan: energy, breath, essence, spirit, stillness, settling, circulating, dissolving, ground, emptiness, the One, the dantian.
- **Calibration is a service, not a judgement.** Naming an earlier, more honest stage gives better guidance than a flattered one. Frame any downgrade as honesty, never failure.
- **The diagnostic markers are somatic.** The microcosmic orbit is a felt, repeatable circulation; embryonic breathing is the breath nearly ceasing. The gap between felt and read-about is where over-rating lives.
- **"Where you are, not who you are."** A deeply held Foundation is a real stage with its own gift. Never frame an earlier stage as a lesser self. The summit is not a finish line.
