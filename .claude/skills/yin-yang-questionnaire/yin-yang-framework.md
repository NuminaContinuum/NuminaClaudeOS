# Yin & Yang (Neidan) framework — canonical content (NUM-183)

The reference the Yin & Yang Map and `/yin-yang-questionnaire` draw from. **Use as reference, not script.** The map output should match where the user's practice and energy actually are, in their language — never quote these layers verbatim.

Frame for audiences drawn to energy work, breathwork, inner alchemy, and contemplative practice. Keep the classical Chinese terms out of question copy; they appear on result screens and in maps with translation, translated names primary.

**Core principle — where you are, not who you are.** This map reads a *current position* on a path of transformation, not a fixed identity. A practitioner can be deeply, rightly in Foundation after years of practice. That is not a failure state. The map shows presence and aliveness at the current stage, never a race to the summit.

---

## The path

Neidan (內丹, "inner alchemy") frames development as refining the three treasures — **essence (精 jīng) → energy (氣 qì) → spirit (神 shén)** — and merging yin and yang until they dissolve into the undivided Dao. Five stages, grouped into three phases. Rendered as a vertical ascent with the **taiji** as the recurring motif: turbulent and split at the base, harmonising through the middle, dissolving into an empty circle (wújí / ensō) at the summit. The three dantians (lower, middle, upper) anchor the path.

| # | Phase | Stage | Chinese | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Foundation | Laying the Foundation | 築基 *Zhújī* | — |
| 2 | Refinement | Refining Essence into Energy | 煉精化氣 *Liàn Jīng Huà Qì* | first gate 初關 |
| 3 | Refinement | Refining Energy into Spirit | 煉氣化神 *Liàn Qì Huà Shén* | middle gate 中關 |
| 4 | Return | Refining Spirit, Returning to Emptiness | 煉神還虛 *Liàn Shén Huán Xū* | upper gate 上關 |
| 5 | Return | Merging with the Dao | 煉虛合道 *Liàn Xū Hé Dào* | — |

Phase grouping for the side-rail: **Foundation → Refinement → Return**.

## 1. Laying the Foundation — 築基 *Zhújī*

- **Essence of the work:** Regulate body, breath, and mind. Seal the leaking of essence (jing) that drains away through stress, scattered attention, depletion. Gather scattered yin & yang into a steady base.
- **Yin quality:** Rest, conservation, settling, receptivity to the body's signals.
- **Yang quality:** Discipline, routine, the active effort of regulation.
- **Lived markers:** Restless body and mind settling; breath being consciously lengthened and smoothed; building a consistent daily practice; conserving rather than depleting.
- **Gift:** Groundedness. A vessel that can hold what comes next.
- **Pitfall:** Impatience — rushing toward circulating energy before the foundation can hold it.
- **Integration signal:** The base begins to hold on its own; steadiness arrives without constant effort.

## 2. Refining Essence into Energy — 煉精化氣 *Liàn Jīng Huà Qì* (first gate 初關)

- **Essence of the work:** Convert jing to qi. Feel energy distinctly and learn to circulate it; open the microcosmic orbit (小周天, the small heavenly circulation).
- **Yin quality:** Following the energy, allowing the current rather than forcing it.
- **Yang quality:** Guiding, circulating, the skilled direction of energy.
- **Lived markers:** Warmth, current, aliveness palpable in the body; energy moving along a path (up the back, down the front); breath slow, deep, connected to the circulation.
- **Gift:** A living, felt relationship with one's own energy.
- **Pitfall:** Forcing the circulation; mistaking imagination of the orbit for the felt, repeatable experience of it.
- **Integration signal:** The circulation becomes familiar and returnable; energy begins to quiet toward stillness.

## 3. Refining Energy into Spirit — 煉氣化神 *Liàn Qì Huà Shén* (middle gate 中關)

- **Essence of the work:** Convert qi to shen. Let energy settle into a quieter, more spirit-like presence. The "immortal embryo" conceived from the union of yin & yang.
- **Yin quality:** Deep stillness, the breath nearly ceasing, inward gathering.
- **Yang quality:** Luminous presence, the inner light, the gathering of spirit.
- **Lived markers:** Energy settled into deep, luminous quiet; embryonic breathing (胎息 *tāi xī*) — the breath so subtle it almost stops; time and body receding; a felt union of opposites.
- **Gift:** Stable inner stillness; presence that no longer depends on managing energy.
- **Pitfall:** Mistaking a vivid stillness for a stable attainment. A glimpse is not a home.
- **Integration signal:** Spaciousness begins to open at the edges; the self begins to thin.

## 4. Refining Spirit, Returning to Emptiness — 煉神還虛 *Liàn Shén Huán Xū* (upper gate 上關)

- **Essence of the work:** Convert shen to void. The separate self thins; spirit releases into open spaciousness. The yang-spirit (陽神 *yáng shén*) emerges.
- **Yin quality:** Dissolution, surrender, releasing even the subtle self.
- **Yang quality:** The yang-spirit unbound from form; spaciousness as presence.
- **Lived markers:** The sense of "me" thinning; vast spaciousness and openness; the one who practises growing faint; awareness more of space than of breath.
- **Gift:** Freedom from identification; spirit no longer bound to form.
- **Pitfall:** Confusing emptiness with blanking out or dissociation; spiritual bypass.
- **Integration signal:** The boundary between inner and outer begins to dissolve.

## 5. Merging with the Dao — 煉虛合道 *Liàn Xū Hé Dào*

- **Essence of the work:** Void merges with Dao. Yin & yang fully reconciled into the One (taiji → wújí). The return complete.
- **Yin quality:** Total receptivity; nothing to manage.
- **Yang quality:** Effortless unity; the undivided expressing itself.
- **Lived markers:** No separation to maintain; inner and outer, self and world, moving as one; no one practising and nothing to attain.
- **Gift:** Undivided wholeness; the source lived rather than sought.
- **Pitfall:** A beautiful idea of unity mistaken for an authentic, stable taste of it. This stage is rare.
- **Integration signal:** Lived continuity rather than peak states.

## Intensity / placement (from evidence)

- The map places the user at a **current stage** with an intensity bar, plus a faint **edge** glow on the adjacent stage that is beginning to activate.
- Be conservative. Most committed practitioners live for years in **Foundation** and the **first gate**. Only place at Return-phase stages with strong, repeated, *somatic* evidence — not a single evocative entry.

## Over-rating diagnostics — the somatic spine

Inner-alchemy self-assessment inflates easily, because the later stages are vivid and well-documented. The markers that matter are **felt, not conceptual**. When reading a map or scoring a questionnaire, weight these behavioural checks above any self-rating:

1. **Microcosmic-orbit check** — Stage 2+ requires an *actual felt, repeatable circulation* (小周天), not the idea of one. No felt circulation → not yet Stage 2.
2. **Breath-cessation check** — Stage 3+ classically involves embryonic breathing (胎息), the breath nearly ceasing. An active, regulated breath → not yet Stage 3.
3. **Practice depth × lineage** — inner alchemy is traditionally transmitted. A self-directed practitioner of a few months describing the yang-spirit emerging is almost certainly over-rating. Depth never inflates the placement — it only caps over-claims. A 10-year practitioner can be rightly, deeply in Foundation.

**Calibration is a service, not a judgement.** Naming an earlier, more honest stage gives the user accurate guidance. The gates are crossed in the body, over time, and starting from where the energy actually is matters more than where one hoped to be.
