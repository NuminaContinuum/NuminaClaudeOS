# /chakra-questionnaire — The Chakra map questionnaire

One skill for the whole Chakra assessment. It runs in stages and knows where the user is:

- **Q1 — Quick read** (3-5 min). Locates *where* the work is: which 1-2 centres are calling for attention. Confidence after Q1: **rough**.
- **Q2 — Deeper dive** (10-12 min). Goes from self-report to pattern and embodiment: resolves *direction* (deficient vs excessive) for the active centres, reads somatic and relational patterns. Confidence after Q2: **medium**.
- **Q3 — Settled reading** (20-30 min). A long, open-ended per-chakra reading woven with the user's own words, resolving a settled state for each centre. Confidence after Q3: **settled**.

The map itself is generated by `/maps chakras`. This skill feeds it structured readings and offers to refresh it after each stage.

---

## Step 0 — Figure out where the user is

Read `context-library/maps/chakras.md` if it exists and check which stages have run:

- A `## Q1 Reading` section means Q1 is done.
- A `## Q2 Reading` section means Q2 is done.

Then route:

- **Nothing done yet** → run Q1.
- **Q1 done, Q2 not** → offer Q2: *"You've done the quick chakra read. Ready to go deeper? The next reflection takes about ten minutes and sharpens the map — it works out whether each active centre is running low or running hot, and reads how it's showing up in your body."* If they decline, stop gracefully.
- **Q1 and Q2 done, Q3 not** → offer Q3: *"You've done the quick read and the deeper dive. The long reading (20-30 minutes) is the most complete — it goes centre by centre in your own words and settles the picture. Want to do it now, or another time?"* If they decline, stop gracefully.
- **All three done** → *"You've completed all three chakra readings. Want to re-run one for a fresh snapshot, refresh the map from everything since, or leave it?"* Respect the answer.

Q3 is also standalone-capable — if the user wants the long reading without having done Q1/Q2, run it; its Section 2 scan provides the baseline.

If the user explicitly asks for a specific stage, honour that.

## Q1 — Quick chakra read

### Q1 Step 1 — Open

Present this intro, then begin immediately. No preamble beyond this.

> *Where in the body is your work right now?*
>
> *The chakra map locates where energy is moving — or stuck — in your life. Seven centres, each tied to a different kind of life force.*
>
> *Eight short questions. Pick the closest fit. There are no right answers.*

### Q1 Step 2 — Present the questions

Present each question one at a time. Wait for the user's answer before showing the next. Accept the letter only. **No commentary between questions.** Preserve the meditative quality of the sequence.

Option order is shuffled per question and the chakra names are hidden, so a user who knows the chakra system can't game the result by picking a position. **The parenthetical tag after each option is for your scoring only — never show it to the user.** Present just the description.

**Q1. The most accurate description of your everyday baseline:**

(a) Unspoken truths sitting in my throat *(Throat)*
(b) Foggy, can't see clearly ahead *(Third Eye)*
(c) Disconnected from anything larger *(Crown)*
(d) Anxious about money, safety, or basic survival *(Root)*
(e) Emotionally numb, or emotionally flooded *(Sacral)*
(f) Drained, or pushing too hard *(Solar Plexus)*
(g) Lonely, or losing myself in others *(Heart)*

**Q2. The body part that's been speaking to you most:**

(a) Hips, lower belly, sexual organs *(Sacral)*
(b) Stomach, gut, solar plexus *(Solar Plexus)*
(c) Chest, shoulders, upper back *(Heart)*
(d) Throat, jaw, neck *(Throat)*
(e) Head, eyes, brow *(Third Eye)*
(f) Crown of the head, or "outside" the body *(Crown)*
(g) Lower back, legs, feet *(Root)*

**Q3. What's hardest in relationships right now:**

(a) Letting love in, or out, without losing myself *(Heart)*
(b) Saying what I actually mean *(Throat)*
(c) Seeing the other person clearly, not my projection *(Third Eye)*
(d) Feeling part of something larger together *(Crown)*
(e) Trusting people enough to feel safe *(Root)*
(f) Letting myself feel pleasure or intimacy *(Sacral)*
(g) Asserting myself without dominating *(Solar Plexus)*

**Q4. The kind of work your inner life is asking for:**

(a) Trusting my intuition and inner sight *(Third Eye)*
(b) Reconnecting to meaning beyond the daily *(Crown)*
(c) Stability, grounding, settling somewhere *(Root)*
(d) Reclaiming pleasure, creativity, or feeling *(Sacral)*
(e) Stepping into my power without aggression *(Solar Plexus)*
(f) Healing my heart, or opening it again *(Heart)*
(g) Speaking what I've kept silent *(Throat)*

**Q5. The thing you most often run from:**

(a) Awe, or surrender to mystery *(Crown)*
(b) Insecurity. Will I be okay? *(Root)*
(c) Feelings. They overwhelm me, or escape me *(Sacral)*
(d) Conflict. I either fight or fold *(Solar Plexus)*
(e) Vulnerability. Loving or being loved *(Heart)*
(f) Saying no, or saying too much *(Throat)*
(g) Quiet knowing. I doubt what I sense *(Third Eye)*

**Q6. The dream or daydream that keeps recurring:**

(a) Standing tall, being seen for who I am *(Solar Plexus)*
(b) Deep love, reconciliation, forgiveness *(Heart)*
(c) Speaking publicly, being heard, singing *(Throat)*
(d) Visions, symbols, prescient dreams *(Third Eye)*
(e) Dissolving into something larger *(Crown)*
(f) A safe home, secure footing *(Root)*
(g) Wild creativity, sensual freedom *(Sacral)*

**Q7. Which feels furthest out of reach right now:**

(a) Clarity about what's true *(Third Eye)*
(b) A sense that I am safe *(Root)*
(c) Joy and aliveness in my body *(Sacral)*
(d) A sense of meaning *(Crown)*
(e) Trust in my own agency *(Solar Plexus)*
(f) Feeling truly loved, or truly loving *(Heart)*
(g) My own voice *(Throat)*

**Q8. If a wise friend listened to your life right now, they'd probably say:**

(a) "Let yourself feel." *(Sacral)*
(b) "Look up. Remember the larger story." *(Crown)*
(c) "Take your power back." *(Solar Plexus)*
(d) "Speak what you've been holding." *(Throat)*
(e) "You need to find your ground." *(Root)*
(f) "Trust what you already know." *(Third Eye)*
(g) "Open your heart again, gently." *(Heart)*

### Q1 Step 3 — Score inline

After Q8, tally **chakra content tags** (not letter positions) across the 8 answers. The option letters do not map to a fixed chakra, so score by the parenthetical tag on each chosen option.

- Chakra with the most mentions = **primary focus**
- Any chakra within 1-2 votes of the leader = **secondary focus** (include it)
- Do not resolve direction (deficient vs. excessive) — that is Q2

### Q1 Step 4 — Render the result

Show the result in the terminal. **ASCII overview first**, top 1-2 chakras marked, others quiet. Use the user's actual tally:

```
    7  Crown (Sahasrara)          ○  quiet
    6  Third Eye (Ajna)           ○  quiet
    5  Throat (Vishuddha)         ○  quiet
    4  Heart (Anahata)            ●  calling for attention
    3  Solar Plexus (Manipura)    ○  quiet
    2  Sacral (Svadhisthana)      ◉  also present
    1  Root (Muladhara)           ○  quiet

    ●  primary focus    ◉  secondary focus    ○  quiet
```

Then a brief reading (2-3 sentences). Use their actual answers to name the themes — do not just restate the chakra's generic territory. Held as hypothesis, not verdict.

End with the confidence label and CTA toward Q2:

> *This is a rough reading — a first location, not a diagnosis. When you're ready, the deeper reflection (Q2) works out whether each active centre is running low or running hot, and how it's living in your body.*

### Q1 Step 5 — Write the Q1 reading

Prepend a Q1 reading note below the file header of `context-library/maps/chakras.md`:

```markdown
## Q1 Reading — YYYY-MM-DD

**Confidence:** rough
**Primary focus:** [Chakra name]
**Secondary focus:** [Chakra name, or "none"]

[The 2-3 sentence reading]

---
```

If the file does not exist, create it with this section and a note that the full map is generated by `/maps chakras`.

### Q1 Step 6 — Offer to refresh the map (NUM-115)

After writing the Q1 reading, check whether a fuller Chakra map already exists (i.e. `chakras.md` has map content beyond the reading sections — a `## Visual overview`, prose, or prior `/maps` output).

If it does, offer it as a natural next step, not an interruption:

> *"You already have a Chakra map. Want me to refresh it with this reading to raise its confidence? I'll merge what you just answered with what's already there from your notes and conversations."*

If they say yes, run the chakras refresh (same logic as `/maps chakras`), merging the new Q1 reading with existing harvested notes and prior readings. If no map exists yet, skip silently — the reading is saved and `/maps chakras` will pick it up later.

## Q2 — Deeper dive: direction, somatics, embodiment

**Gate:** Q2 needs Q1 first. If `chakras.md` has no `## Q1 Reading`, say: *"Q2 builds on the quick read. Let's start with Q1 — it takes about five minutes."* and run Q1 instead.

Q2 deepens the centres Q1 surfaced. It moves from self-report toward pattern recognition and embodiment, and resolves **direction** for each active centre: is it **deficient** (shut down, blocked, under-energised) or **excessive** (over-active, compensating, running hot)?

### Q2 Step 1 — Open

> *Let's go deeper into the centres that came up — [name the primary and secondary from Q1].*
>
> *This time it's less about locating and more about texture: how each centre is actually behaving, where it lives in your body, and the patterns it drives in your life. About ten minutes.*

### Q2 Step 2 — Direction for each active centre

For **each** chakra flagged primary or secondary in Q1, ask this pair. Present one centre at a time; name the centre so the user knows what they're answering about.

**D. For [centre] — which is closer to true?**

(a) It feels **shut down or blocked** — I avoid this territory, go numb, or can't access it
(b) It feels **over-active or compensating** — I overdo it, grip, or it runs my behaviour
(c) It swings between both
(d) It feels open and flowing — no strong charge either way

**S. Where do you feel [centre] in your body, and what does it do there?**
(Free text — a sentence or two. Accept whatever they give, including "nothing I can locate.")

### Q2 Step 3 — Pattern and embodiment (all users)

Three questions, one at a time. Free text welcome; offer the lettered options as scaffolding.

**P1. A recurring emotional or relational pattern you'd most like to understand:**
(Free text. If they're stuck, prompt with: a conflict that keeps repeating, a feeling that keeps returning, a situation you keep landing in.)

**P2. The life area where you feel most stuck right now — and the one where you feel most alive:**
(Free text. Two short answers.)

**P3. When you sit quietly and scan your body, what asks for attention?**

(a) A place of tension, holding, or pain
(b) A place of numbness or absence
(c) A place of warmth, openness, or aliveness
(d) Nothing distinct yet — the signal is faint

### Q2 Step 4 — Render the deepened reading

For each active centre, state its **direction** (deficient / excessive / mixed / open) with one line of rationale drawn from the user's actual Q2 answers, then weave in the somatic and pattern material. Keep it held-as-hypothesis.

Update the ASCII overview to annotate direction on the active centres:

```
    4  Heart (Anahata)            ●  active — deficient (guarded, hard to let love in)
    2  Sacral (Svadhisthana)      ◉  present — excessive (intensity as escape)
```

Close with one question worth sitting with, grounded in what actually came up. Confidence label: **medium**.

### Q2 Step 5 — Write the Q2 reading

Prepend a `## Q2 Reading — YYYY-MM-DD` section to `chakras.md` (above the Q1 reading) with: **Confidence: medium**, the direction per active centre, a 3-4 sentence synthesis, and a short bullet list of the somatic/pattern notes captured.

### Q2 Step 6 — Offer to refresh the map (NUM-115)

Same as Q1 Step 6: if a fuller Chakra map exists, offer to refresh it, merging Q2 (direction + somatics + patterns) with Q1 and any harvested notes. The merged map should raise the confidence shown in its header.

## Q3 — Settled reading (20-30 min)

The longest read: a settled state for each of the 7 centres (balanced / deficient / excessive / mixed), woven with the user's own words. 22 questions in seven sections — a mix of open-ended (answer in their own words, ~40% of the signal), multiple-choice, and Likert (1-5). Present one at a time, no commentary between. **Parenthetical tags are for your scoring only — never show them.** Shuffle multiple-choice option order. The terminal handles pause/resume naturally, so there's no separate save step.

If Q1/Q2 exist, open by surfacing the latest reading. If not, that's fine — Section 2 is a built-in baseline scan.

### Q3 Section 1 — Settling in (2 open-ended)

**1.** *Take a breath. In your own words: where in your body or your life is energy moving most right now? Where is it stuck, or moving too fast?*
**2.** *If you had to name what your body is asking of you right now, what would you say?*

(Read dominant-chakra themes from their language — safety, feeling, power, love, voice, vision, meaning — to cross-reference with Section 2.)

### Q3 Section 2 — Quick scan of all 7 centres (7 multiple-choice)

For each, the three options map to *(balanced)* / *(deficient)* / *(excessive)* — shuffle their order, hide the tags.

**3. Safety, body, basic survival:** Grounded enough, not perfect but okay *(balanced)* / Anxious, ungrounded, not at home in my body *(deficient)* / Gripping, hoarding, controlling *(excessive)*
**4. Emotion, pleasure, creativity:** Flowing *(balanced)* / Numb, shut down, disconnected *(deficient)* / Flooded, compulsive, hooked *(excessive)*
**5. Power, will, agency:** Trustworthy, I follow through without dominating *(balanced)* / Passive, small, easily overridden *(deficient)* / Driven, controlling, pushing through people *(excessive)*
**6. Love, grief, connection:** Open, both giving and receiving feel possible *(balanced)* / Walled off, isolated *(deficient)* / Losing myself in others, codependent *(excessive)*
**7. Voice, truth, expression:** Speaking what's true when it matters *(balanced)* / Silent, biting my tongue *(deficient)* / Over-talking, talking through people, oversharing *(excessive)*
**8. Vision, intuition, perception:** I trust what I sense and stay grounded with it *(balanced)* / Foggy, I doubt my inner sight *(deficient)* / Lost in fantasy, ungrounded patterns *(excessive)*
**9. Meaning, mystery, transcendence:** Quiet presence, connected without performing it *(balanced)* / Absent, life feels mostly survival *(deficient)* / Inflated, bypassing ordinary life for "spiritual" detachment *(excessive)*

### Q3 Section 3 — Body, breath, sensation (2 MC + 1 open-ended)

**10. Where in your body do you feel most alive right now?** Lower body — legs, hips, belly *(Root/Sacral)* / Mid-body — solar plexus, chest *(Solar/Heart)* / Upper body — throat, head *(Throat/Third Eye/Crown)* / Whole body, evenly *(balanced)* / Nowhere, I don't really feel my body *(Root deficient + system-wide)*
**11. Where do you feel tension, pain, or numbness?** Lower back/legs/feet *(Root)* / Hips/lower belly/sexual organs *(Sacral)* / Stomach/gut *(Solar)* / Chest/heart/upper back *(Heart)* / Throat/jaw/neck/shoulders *(Throat)* / Head/eyes/sinuses *(Third Eye)* / Top of head, dissociation *(Crown)*
**12.** *Open-ended: Describe that tension or sensation in detail. What does it feel like? What does it remind you of? When did you first notice it?*

### Q3 Section 4 — Adaptive deep-dive (3 open-ended)

From the running profile, pick the **top 2 active chakras** and select 3 prompts from this library (1 clear leader → 2 prompts on it + 1 on the second; 2 close → 1 each + 1 on a third/related centre):

- **Root** — *"Tell me about home and safety. Where you live, where you've lived, where you feel safe and where you don't. What does your body do when you feel unsafe?"*
- **Sacral** — *"Describe your relationship with pleasure, creativity, and sensuality right now. What's flowing? What's blocked? What's compulsive?"*
- **Solar Plexus** — *"Describe a recent moment when you felt fully in your power, and one when you felt overrun or invisible. What's the gap telling you?"*
- **Heart** — *"Tell me about love in your life right now. What's open, what's guarded, what's grieving? Where does it hurt to look?"*
- **Throat** — *"What are you not saying that wants to be said? Or where are you saying too much? What do you fear if you spoke the truest thing?"*
- **Third Eye** — *"Describe a recent moment of clear seeing, and one when your perception felt off. What do you trust about your inner sight, and what do you doubt?"*
- **Crown** — *"Describe your relationship to meaning right now. What gives your life weight beyond the daily? Where does mystery live for you, if anywhere?"*

### Q3 Section 5 — Relationships and voice (2 MC + 1 Likert)

**16. In your closest relationships:** I bring myself fully, I receive others fully *(Heart balanced)* / I hold back, I struggle to let people in *(Heart deficient)* / I lose myself, I can't find my edges *(Heart excessive)* / I disappear or get aggressive in conflict *(Solar mixed)*
**17. When you speak to people who matter:** I tell the truth gently *(Throat balanced)* / I keep what's true to myself *(Throat deficient)* / I talk a lot, dominate, overshare *(Throat excessive)* / I rehearse what I'll say and never say it *(Throat + Solar deficient)*
**18. Likert (1-5):** *I feel safe being seen as I actually am.* (low → Heart + Solar + Throat leaning deficient)

### Q3 Section 6 — Vision and meaning (2 Likert + 1 open-ended)

**19. Likert (1-5):** *I trust what I see and sense, even when I can't explain it.* (high → Third Eye balanced)
**20. Likert (1-5):** *My life feels like part of something larger than me.* (high → Crown balanced)
**21.** *Open-ended: What do you long for that you don't yet have words for?* (longing language reveals the most under-fed centre)

### Q3 Section 7 — Closing reflection (1 open-ended)

**22.** *If your body could speak directly to you right now, in one sentence, what would it say?* — use this as a direct quote at the top of the reading ("Your body said: …").

### Q3 Step 8 — Resolve and render

- Combine: Section 2 (+2 to the keyed state per chakra), Section 3 (tension/numbness → deficient nudge; even aliveness → balanced), Section 4 open-ended (deep texture → HIGH confidence on those centres), Sections 5-6 nudges. Open-ended carries ~40% of the weight.
- Final state per chakra: balanced / deficient / excessive / mixed; HIGH confidence on deepened chakras, MEDIUM on the rest.
- Render: ASCII 7-centre column styled by state (reuse the Q1 legend, annotate direction), the closing body quote at the top, a 2-4 sentence reading per centre woven with the user's phrases, and a **"Where your work is"** section calling out 1-2 centres with concrete practice (e.g. *"Throat deficient: try voice journalling, or speak one true thing each day to someone who matters"*). Confidence: **settled**.

### Q3 Step 9 — Write the Q3 reading and close

- Prepend a `## Q3 Reading — YYYY-MM-DD` section (above prior readings) to `chakras.md` with: **Confidence: settled**, the state per chakra, the closing quote, and the per-centre summary.
- Offer to refresh the Chakra map (merging Q3 with prior readings + harvested notes; raises the map's confidence header).
- Seed 2-3 reflection prompts as conversation starters, focused on the active centres.

## Tone reminders

- Wise, sincere, grounded. Witness before wisdom.
- No em dashes. No "unlock," "transform," "breakthrough," "holistic."
- Numina lexicon: ground, witness, threshold, pattern, centre.
- A reading is an invitation to pay attention, not a label to carry.
- The user knows their own body better than any questionnaire. Hold lightly.
- Q2's "deficient / excessive" language is descriptive, not a verdict. Offer it as a question the body can confirm or correct.
