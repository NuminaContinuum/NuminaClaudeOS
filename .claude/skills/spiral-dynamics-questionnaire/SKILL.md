# /spiral-dynamics-questionnaire — The Spiral Dynamics map questionnaire

One skill for the Spiral Dynamics assessment, run in stages:

- **Q1 — Center of gravity** (3-5 min). Finds the dominant band, then resolves to a specific vMeme. Confidence after Q1: **rough**.
- **Q2 — Deeper read** (8-10 min): full level profile — trailing, emerging, dormant, and shadow levels. Confidence after Q2: **deeper**.
- **Q3 — Long reading** (20-30 min): a settled level profile with open-ended depth, woven with the user's own words. Confidence after Q3: **settled**.

The map is generated by `/maps spiral-dynamics`. This skill feeds it structured readings and offers to refresh it afterward.

## The model — 5 bands → 8 vMemes

| Band | vMemes |
|---|---|
| Survival & Tribe | Beige (Survival), Purple (Tribal) |
| Power & Order | Red (Power), Blue (Order) |
| Achievement | Orange (Achievement) |
| Community | Green (Community) |
| Integral | Yellow (Integrative), Turquoise (Whole-seeing) |

Held as a weighted distribution with a center of gravity. **Never imply higher = better.** Each level is healthy when it fits the conditions, unhealthy when it doesn't. Levels are perspectives a person takes, not categories a person *is*.

---

## Step 0 — Figure out where the user is

Read `context-library/maps/spiral-dynamics.md` if it exists and check which stages have run (`## Q1 Reading`, `## Q2 Reading`). Then route:

- **Nothing done** → run Q1.
- **Q1 done, Q2 not** → offer Q2: *"Q1 found your center of gravity. Ready to go deeper? The next reflection takes about ten minutes and shows the full picture — what's still active underneath, what's emerging, and what you might be pushing away."* If they decline, stop gracefully.
- **Q1 and Q2 done, Q3 not** → offer Q3: *"You've found your center of gravity and the full profile. The long reading (20-30 minutes) is the most complete — mostly in your own words, settling where you sit on the whole spiral and the threshold you're crossing. Want to do it now, or another time?"* If they decline, stop gracefully.
- **All three done** → offer to re-run a stage or refresh the map from everything since.

Q3 is standalone-capable — its Section 2 scan provides the baseline if Q1/Q2 weren't taken.

Honour an explicit request.

## Q1 — Center of gravity

### Q1 Step 1 — Open

> *Where is your center of gravity on the spiral?*
>
> *The spiral maps how human values evolve: survival, tribe, power, order, achievement, community, and on. Most people live primarily at one level, with older levels still active underneath and a next one quietly emerging.*
>
> *Eight short questions. Pick the closest fit. At the end you'll see where you sit, roughly.*

### Q1 Step 2 — Present the questions

One at a time. Wait for each answer. Accept the letter only. **No commentary between questions.** Option order is shuffled and band names are hidden. **The parenthetical tag is for your scoring only — never show it to the user.**

**1. What drives you most days?**

(a) Meeting basic needs. Survival, safety, getting through, belonging to my own *(Survival & Tribe)*
(b) Power and respect. Standing tall, winning, or living by the right code *(Power & Order)*
(c) Achievement. Building, climbing, doing work that counts *(Achievement)*
(d) Connection. Being part of something whole with others *(Community)*
(e) Working with complexity. Sensing patterns across systems and lives *(Integral)*

**2. Your relationship to rules and authority:**

(a) I follow rules when they serve outcomes. I rewrite them when they don't *(Achievement)*
(b) I use rules where useful, suspend them where they aren't. Whatever serves the whole *(Integral)*
(c) Either I claim my own authority and take what's mine, or I serve a higher order *(Power & Order)*
(d) I follow what keeps me safe, or what my people have always done *(Survival & Tribe)*
(e) I question hierarchies. I want flat, fair, consensual structures *(Community)*

**3. What feels most true to you?**

(a) Every voice has a piece of the truth *(Community)*
(b) What evidence and reason show works *(Achievement)*
(c) What my body and my tribe tell me. What I've inherited *(Survival & Tribe)*
(d) Different truths fit different levels. The whole spiral is real *(Integral)*
(e) The one right answer. My faith, my code, or my own raw will *(Power & Order)*

**4. Your idea of "a life well lived":**

(a) In service to something larger than me. The whole is what I tend *(Integral)*
(b) Safe, fed, surrounded by my own *(Survival & Tribe)*
(c) In community. Loved and loving. Equitable, kind, alive *(Community)*
(d) Honored. Either righteous within my code, or feared and respected *(Power & Order)*
(e) Successful. I built something. I'm seen for it *(Achievement)*

**5. What you most distrust:**

(a) Either chaos and disorder, or oppression and tyranny, depending which side I'm on *(Power & Order)*
(b) Hierarchies, exclusion, anyone who puts themselves above others *(Community)*
(c) Anyone certain they have the one right view *(Integral)*
(d) Inefficiency. Sentimentality that blocks results *(Achievement)*
(e) Strangers, risk, the unfamiliar *(Survival & Tribe)*

**6. The kind of work you're called to right now:**

(a) Building, achieving, sharpening my craft *(Achievement)*
(b) Getting basics in place. Stability, safety, belonging *(Survival & Tribe)*
(c) Sensing the whole. Holding paradox. Acting from a longer view *(Integral)*
(d) Healing, including, listening across difference *(Community)*
(e) Either claiming my power, or finding a stronger framework to live inside *(Power & Order)*

**7. Conflict, for you:**

(a) I want everyone heard. Process matters. No one left behind *(Community)*
(b) I move between styles depending on what the situation actually needs *(Integral)*
(c) I fight to win, or I invoke a higher authority to settle it *(Power & Order)*
(d) I avoid it. It's dangerous *(Survival & Tribe)*
(e) I negotiate. Find the deal that produces the better outcome *(Achievement)*

**8. If you imagine the world in 100 years, what do you most want to see?**

(a) My people safe and intact *(Survival & Tribe)*
(b) Progress. Cures, abundance, advancement *(Achievement)*
(c) Equity, ecological repair, every voice valued *(Community)*
(d) The right order restored, or my side prevailing *(Power & Order)*
(e) A civilization aware of itself. Many levels held together *(Integral)*

### Q1 Step 3 — Score the band

Tally **band content tags** (not letters) across Q1-Q8. The dominant band is the center-of-gravity band. **Tie handling:** if two bands tie, fire the tiebreaker for both and let the user pick the closer fit.

### Q1 Step 4 — Tiebreaker (resolve the vMeme)

Fire the band's branched tiebreaker. Achievement and Community resolve directly (no tiebreaker). Options are shuffled; tags are builder-only.

**Survival & Tribe band:**
(a) I live inside a felt sense of ancestors, ritual, kinship, and place *(Purple — Tribal)*
(b) My world is small and present. Day to day survival, my body, my immediate kin *(Beige — Survival)*

**Power & Order band:**
(a) I live inside a higher order. Faith, code, discipline, sacrifice for the right way *(Blue — Order)*
(b) My energy is raw and self-claiming. I take what's mine. I'm my own authority *(Red — Power)*

**Achievement band:** resolves directly to **Orange — Achievement** (no tiebreaker).

**Community band:** resolves directly to **Green — Community** (no tiebreaker).

**Integral band:**
(a) I feel the living whole. Spiritual coherence, planetary mind, cosmic order *(Turquoise — Whole-seeing)*
(b) I see and use the whole spiral. I'm flexible, systemic, value each level for what it does *(Yellow — Integrative)*

### Q1 Step 5 — Render the result

**ASCII spiral/ladder first**, with the resolved vMeme marked and a soft halo (◌) over adjacent levels in the same band:

```
   Turquoise  ○
   Yellow     ●   ← your center of gravity
   Green      ◌
   Orange     ○
   Blue       ○
   Red        ○
   Purple     ○
   Beige      ○

   ●  center of gravity   ◌  adjacent / emerging   ○  quiet
```

Then the level name and a 2-3 sentence reading grounded in the user's actual answers — that vMeme's worldview, its gifts, its shadow. Non-hierarchical. Confidence: **rough**.

Example microcopy:
> *Right now, your center of gravity is **Green**. You live in a worldview that holds harmony, plurality, and care for the whole. The shadow side: getting stuck in process, struggling to act decisively, fearing the use of power.*

End with CTA toward Q2:
> *This finds the dominant level only. Q2 shows what's still active underneath and what's emerging above. Confidence: rough.*

### Q1 Step 6 — Write the Q1 reading

Prepend to `context-library/maps/spiral-dynamics.md`:

```markdown
## Q1 Reading — YYYY-MM-DD

**Confidence:** rough
**Center of gravity:** [vMeme name, e.g. Green — Community]
**Adjacent level:** [adjacent vMeme, or "none"]

[The 2-3 sentence reading]

---
```

If the file does not exist, create it with this section and a note that the full map is generated by `/maps spiral-dynamics`.

### Q1 Step 7 — Offer to refresh the map

If a fuller Spiral Dynamics map already exists, offer to refresh it, merging Q1 with prior readings + harvested notes. If no map exists yet, skip silently.

## Q2 — Full level profile (8-10 min)

**Gate:** Q2 needs Q1. If `spiral-dynamics.md` has no `## Q1 Reading`, run Q1 first. If Q1 exists, open by surfacing it: *"Q1 placed you around [level]. Let's see what else is moving in you."*

Q2 resolves the **full profile**: which level is dominant, which are still active underneath (trailing), which is emerging, which are dormant, and which (if any) is in shadow (actively rejected). 16 questions in three sections. One at a time, no commentary between. **State labels in parentheses are for your scoring only — never show them to the user.** Option order is shuffled.

### Q2 Section 1 — Level scan (8 questions, one per vMeme)

**1. Survival, basic needs, the body's instinctual signals:**
(a) This is daily. I'm in it *(Beige dominant)*
(b) I've been here. It's close. I can drop into it *(Beige active)*
(c) I sense it pulling at me. Something is asking me to come back to basics *(Beige emerging)*
(d) Not really in play *(Beige dormant)*
(e) I push against it. I don't want to live there *(Beige shadow)*

**2. Belonging through kinship, ritual, ancestors, magical thinking, your tribe:**
(a) This is where I live *(Purple dominant)*
(b) Still close. My family, my tribe, my rituals matter *(Purple active)*
(c) I'm being pulled toward it. I want to find my people, my ritual, my roots *(Purple emerging)*
(d) Not in play *(Purple dormant)*
(e) I push against it. Superstition or clannishness repels me *(Purple shadow)*

**3. Raw power, self-claim, conquest, "I take what's mine":**
(a) This is where I live. I'm the source of my own authority *(Red dominant)*
(b) Still operational. I can claim what's mine when I need to *(Red active)*
(c) I'm being pulled toward it. I need to find my fire *(Red emerging)*
(d) Not in play *(Red dormant)*
(e) I push against it. Aggression and dominance scare me, in me or others *(Red shadow)*

**4. Order, discipline, faith or code, sacrifice for the right way:**
(a) This is where I live. There's one right order and I'm in service to it *(Blue dominant)*
(b) Still operational. I have a code I live by, even loosely *(Blue active)*
(c) I'm being pulled toward it. I want structure, discipline, something to commit to *(Blue emerging)*
(d) Not in play *(Blue dormant)*
(e) I push against it. Dogma and "one right way" repel me *(Blue shadow)*

**5. Strategic achievement, optimization, building, climbing, results:**
(a) This is where I live. I build, achieve, optimize *(Orange dominant)*
(b) Still operational. I use it where it serves me *(Orange active)*
(c) I'm being pulled toward it. I want to build something measurable *(Orange emerging)*
(d) Not in play *(Orange dormant)*
(e) I push against it. Striving and material success repel me *(Orange shadow)*

**6. Community, plurality, ecology, equity, listening across difference:**
(a) This is where I live. The "we" matters most *(Green dominant)*
(b) Still operational. I care, I include, I listen *(Green active)*
(c) I'm being pulled toward it. I want to belong to a larger we *(Green emerging)*
(d) Not in play *(Green dormant)*
(e) I push against it. Soft consensus and infinite process exhaust me *(Green shadow)*

**7. Systemic, integrative, flexible, value-each-level, "use what works":**
(a) This is where I live. I move fluidly between levels and styles *(Yellow dominant)*
(b) Still operational. I can take a systemic view when I need to *(Yellow active)*
(c) I'm being pulled toward it. I want a way of being that holds paradox *(Yellow emerging)*
(d) Not in play yet *(Yellow dormant)*
(e) I push against it. Detached big-picture thinking feels cold to me *(Yellow shadow)*

**8. Whole-seeing, planetary mind, spiritual coherence, the living spiral itself:**
(a) This is where I live. I sense the whole as alive *(Turquoise dominant)*
(b) Still operational. I drop into it in moments of stillness *(Turquoise active)*
(c) I'm being pulled toward it. I want to feel the larger living whole *(Turquoise emerging)*
(d) Not in play yet *(Turquoise dormant)*
(e) I push against it. Cosmic spirituality feels false or escapist to me *(Turquoise shadow)*

(a) Survival. I think about it because I have to *(Beige)*
(b) Status. It buys respect or position *(Red)*
(c) Security and right living. I steward it carefully *(Blue)*
(d) A tool I use to build outcomes I care about *(Orange)*
(e) Something to share, redistribute, or use ethically *(Green)*

**10. Conflict:**

**11. When you feel most alive, it's usually:**

### Q2 Section 3 — Cross-cutting Likert (4 items, 1-5)

Ask each as "1 = not at all true, 5 = very true."

**13.** *I notice old ways of operating still running underneath me.* (high → multi-level trailing; low → single-level dominance)
**14.** *Something larger than my current way of being is pulling at me.* (high → emerging signal; locate via Section 1)
**15.** *There's a level on the spiral I actively reject.* (high → confirms shadow)
**16.** *Different situations bring out different parts of me, operating from different levels.* (high → Yellow tendency or broad active profile)

### Q2 Step 5 — Resolve and render

- From Section 1, assign each of the 8 levels one state: dominant / active / emerging / dormant / shadow. Typical shape: 1 dominant, 1-2 active, 0-1 emerging, 0-1 shadow.
- Section 2 probes add +1 nudges; where they agree with the scan, mark confidence HIGH, else MEDIUM (scan wins). Likert items validate shape.
- Render the ASCII spiral with all 8 levels styled by state, e.g.:

```
   Turquoise  ·  dormant
   Yellow     ◌  emerging
   Green      ●  dominant
   Orange     ◌  active
   Blue       ·  shadow
   Red        ·  dormant
   Purple     ·  dormant
   Beige      ·  dormant

   ●  dominant   ◌  active / emerging   ·  dormant / shadow (shadow noted in prose)
```

- Give a 1-2 sentence reading per active level, then a summary line naming the dominant, the trailing, the emerging, any shadow, and the growth threshold (e.g. "the Green-to-Yellow threshold"). Non-hierarchical. Confidence label: **deeper**.

### Q2 Step 6 — Write the Q2 reading

Prepend a `## Q2 Reading — YYYY-MM-DD` section (above the Q1 reading) to `spiral-dynamics.md` with: **Confidence: deeper**, the state per level, the summary line, and a short bullet list of the per-active-level readings.

### Q2 Step 7 — Offer to refresh the map

If a fuller Spiral Dynamics map exists, offer to refresh it, merging Q2 with Q1 and harvested notes; the merged map should raise the confidence in its header.

## Q3 — Long reading (20-30 min)

The longest read: a settled full level profile woven with the user's own words. 23 questions across seven sections — open-ended (~40% of the signal), multiple-choice, and Likert (1-5). One at a time, no commentary between. **State tags are for your scoring only — never show them.** Shuffle multiple-choice option order. If Q1/Q2 exist, surface the latest reading; standalone-capable otherwise (Section 2 is the baseline). Non-hierarchical throughout — describe the lived worldview before naming the level.

### Q3 Section 1 — Settling in (2 open-ended)

**1.** *Take a breath. In your own words: how do you make sense of the world right now? What does life ask of you, and what do you give back?*
**2.** *If you had to describe the worldview you live inside most days, in plain language, what would you say?*

### Q3 Section 2 — Quick scan of all 8 levels (8 multiple-choice)

For each, options map to *(dominant)* / *(active)* / *(emerging)* / *(dormant)* / *(shadow)* — shuffle, hide tags.

**3. Survival, basic needs, the body's instinctual signals:** Daily, I live in it *(Beige dominant)* / Close, I can drop into it *(active)* / Pulling at me, asking me back to basics *(emerging)* / Not in play *(dormant)* / I push against it *(shadow)*
**4. Kinship, ritual, ancestors, belonging to your people:** Where I live *(Purple dominant)* / Close, my tribe/roots/rituals matter *(active)* / Pulling at me, I want to find my people *(emerging)* / Not in play *(dormant)* / I push against it *(shadow)*
**5. Raw power, self-claim, conquest, fire:** Where I live *(Red dominant)* / Operational, I can claim what's mine *(active)* / Pulling at me, I need to find my fire *(emerging)* / Not in play *(dormant)* / I push against it *(shadow)*
**6. Order, discipline, faith or code, sacrifice for the right way:** Where I live *(Blue dominant)* / Operational, I have a code I live by *(active)* / Pulling at me, I want structure to commit to *(emerging)* / Not in play *(dormant)* / I push against it *(shadow)*
**7. Strategic achievement, optimization, building, climbing:** Where I live *(Orange dominant)* / Operational, I use it when it serves me *(active)* / Pulling at me, I want to build something measurable *(emerging)* / Not in play *(dormant)* / I push against it *(shadow)*
**8. Community, plurality, equity, listening across difference:** Where I live *(Green dominant)* / Operational, I care/include/listen *(active)* / Pulling at me, I want a larger we *(emerging)* / Not in play *(dormant)* / I push against it *(shadow)*
**9. Systemic, integrative, flexible, holding paradox:** Where I live *(Yellow dominant)* / Operational, I can take a systemic view *(active)* / Pulling at me, I want a way that holds paradox *(emerging)* / Not in play yet *(dormant)* / I push against it *(shadow)*
**10. Whole-seeing, the living spiral, planetary mind:** Where I live *(Turquoise dominant)* / Operational, I drop into it in stillness *(active)* / Pulling at me, I want the felt sense of the whole *(emerging)* / Not in play yet *(dormant)* / I push against it *(shadow)*

### Q3 Section 3 — Lived experience (2 MC + 1 open-ended)

**11. The domain of your life that feels most alive right now:** Body/survival/safety *(Beige)* / Family/tradition/ritual *(Purple)* / Self-assertion/power *(Red)* / Faith/discipline/code *(Blue)* / Building/achieving *(Orange)* / Connection/equity *(Green)* / Integrating/systems/paradox *(Yellow)* / Whole-seeing/spiritual coherence *(Turquoise)*
**12. The domain that feels most stuck, charged, or contested:** (same 8 options) — flags the level you're wrestling with (often shadow, sometimes the next growth edge)
**13.** *Open-ended: Describe a recent moment when you felt most yourself. What was happening? What was alive in you?*

### Q3 Section 4 — Adaptive deep-dive (3 open-ended)

From the running profile select 3 prompts: **1 on the dominant level**, **1 on the most emotionally charged trailing-or-shadow level** (whatever they reacted to most strongly in Q12 / Section 2), **1 on the emerging level** (or another trailing if none emerged).

- **Beige (Survival)** — *"Tell me about a time you were in pure survival mode. What did it teach you? What part of that is still in you?"*
- **Purple (Tribal)** — *"Tell me about your people, by blood, choice, or spirit. Where do you belong? Where do ritual, ancestry, or the magical live in your life, if at all?"*
- **Red (Power)** — *"Describe your relationship to your own fire. When have you claimed your power, and when have you given it away or pushed too hard with it?"*
- **Blue (Order)** — *"What code do you live by? Where do you sacrifice for what you believe is right? Where has structure held you up, and where has it caged you?"*
- **Orange (Achievement)** — *"Describe what you've built, or want to build. What is achievement worth to you, and what has it cost?"*
- **Green (Community)** — *"Tell me about the 'we' you belong to. Where do you listen across difference well, and where do you get stuck in process or consensus?"*
- **Yellow (Integrative)** — *"Describe a moment when you saw the whole pattern of a situation clearly. How do you move between levels in yourself and in others?"*
- **Turquoise (Whole-seeing)** — *"Describe your felt sense of the living whole. Where does it show up in your life, and how do you hold it without bypassing the ordinary?"*

### Q3 Section 5 — Transitions and edges (2 MC + 1 Likert)

**17. The transition you're inside right now:** Survival → belonging *(Beige→Purple)* / Kinship → self-claim *(Purple→Red)* / Raw power → disciplined order *(Red→Blue)* / Orthodoxy → strategic possibility *(Blue→Orange)* / Individual achievement → communal care *(Orange→Green)* / Inclusive process → integrative action *(Green→Yellow)* / Systemic seeing → felt wholeness *(Yellow→Turquoise)* / Not in a clear transition *(none)*
**18. What you most fear losing as you grow into your next level:** Safety and certainty *(Beige/Blue)* / Belonging to my people *(Purple)* / My edge and my fire *(Red)* / My moral certainty *(Blue)* / My achievements and reputation *(Orange)* / My community and my goodness *(Green)* / My ability to see clearly *(Yellow)*
**19. Likert (1-5):** *I can hold paradox, multiple truths that contradict each other, without needing to resolve it.* (high → Yellow active)

### Q3 Section 6 — Emerging and longing (2 Likert + 1 open-ended)

**20. Likert (1-5):** *Something larger than my current way of being is pulling at me.* (high → strong emerging signal)
**21. Likert (1-5):** *I feel part of a living whole that exists beyond me.* (high → Turquoise active)
**22.** *Open-ended: What do you long for that you don't yet have words for?* (longing language reveals the level most asking to emerge)

### Q3 Section 7 — Closing reflection (1 open-ended)

**23.** *If your spiral could speak to you right now, in one sentence, what would it say about where you are and where you're going?* — use as a direct quote at the top of the reading ("Your spiral said: …").

### Q3 Step 8 — Resolve and render

- Combine Section 2 (state per level), Section 3 (charged-domain Q12 flags shadow/edge), Section 4 open-ended (deep texture → HIGH confidence on those levels), Section 5 (named transition + what's feared lost), Section 6 (emerging signal). Open-ended carries ~40%.
- Final state per level: dominant / active / emerging / dormant / shadow; HIGH on deepened levels.
- Render: the closing **"What your spiral said"** quote at the top; the ASCII spiral with all 8 levels styled by state (same logic as Q2); a 2-4 sentence reading per active level woven with the user's phrases; **"The threshold you're at"** named (e.g. *Green to Yellow*) with concrete practice; **"What you fear losing in the crossing"** reflected back from Q18. Non-hierarchical. Confidence: **settled**.

### Q3 Step 9 — Write the Q3 reading and close

- Prepend a `## Q3 Reading — YYYY-MM-DD` section (above prior readings) to `spiral-dynamics.md` with: **Confidence: settled**, the state per level, the threshold, the spiral quote, and the per-active-level summary.
- Offer to refresh the Spiral Dynamics map (merging Q3 with prior readings + harvested notes; raises confidence).
- Seed 2-3 reflection prompts focused on the active levels and the threshold.

## Tone reminders

- Wise, sincere, grounded. Witness before wisdom.
- No em dashes. No "unlock," "transform," "breakthrough."
- Numina lexicon: ground, witness, threshold, pattern, centre, terrain, spiral.
- Hold the model lightly. Levels are perspectives, not a ranking of people. Never imply higher = better in absolute terms.
