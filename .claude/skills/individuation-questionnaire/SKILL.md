# /individuation-questionnaire — The Individuation map questionnaire

One skill for the Jung Individuation assessment, run in stages:

- **Q1 — Quick placement** (3-5 min). Locates the **primary stage** of individuation most active right now. Confidence after Q1: **rough**.
- **Q2 — Deeper profile** (8-10 min): full stage profile — trailing, emerging, dormant, and shadow stages. Confidence after Q2: **deeper**.
- **Q3 — Long reflection** (20-30 min): a settled stage profile with open-ended depth, woven with the user's own words. Confidence after Q3: **settled**.

The map is generated by `/maps individuation`. This skill feeds it structured readings and offers to refresh it afterward.

## The 6 stages

Unconscious Wholeness → Persona → Shadow Work → Anima/Animus Work → Wise Elder → The Self. Developmental and **spiralling**: earlier stages stay operational, later stages are often emerging. Q1 places the primary stage only. The Self is a living centre, not a static endpoint.

---

## Step 0 — Figure out where the user is

Read `context-library/maps/individuation.md` if it exists and check which stages have run (`## Q1 Reading`, `## Q2 Reading`). Then route:

- **Nothing done** → run Q1.
- **Q1 done, Q2 not** → offer Q2: *"Q1 placed your primary stage. Ready to go deeper? The next reflection takes about ten minutes and shows the full picture — what's still active from earlier stages, what's emerging, and what you might be pushing away."* If they decline, stop gracefully.
- **Q1 and Q2 done, Q3 not** → offer Q3: *"You've placed your stage and seen the full profile. The long reading (20-30 minutes) is the most complete — mostly in your own words, with the inner figures, the threshold you're at, and what the deepest part of you has to say. Want to do it now, or another time?"* If they decline, stop gracefully.
- **All three done** → offer to re-run a stage for a fresh snapshot or refresh the map from everything since.

Q3 is standalone-capable — its Section 2 scan provides the baseline if Q1/Q2 weren't taken.

Honour an explicit stage request.

## Q1 — Quick placement

### Q1 Step 1 — Open

> *Where are you in the work of becoming yourself?*
>
> *Carl Jung described the lifelong arc of individuation: from an undifferentiated wholeness, through the mask we build to meet the world, the meeting with the parts of us we'd rather not see, the inner partner we've projected onto others, the inner elder, and finally a lived wholeness that includes everything.*
>
> *Eight short questions. Pick the closest fit. At the end you'll see where you are on the arc, roughly.*

### Q1 Step 2 — Present the questions

One at a time. Wait for each answer. Accept the letter only. **No commentary between questions.** Option order is shuffled and stage names are hidden, so the result can't be gamed. **The parenthetical tag is for your scoring only — never show it to the user.**

**1. When you wake up these days, the first feeling is closest to:**

(a) A longing for a kind of love or aliveness I can't quite name *(Anima/Animus)*
(b) A felt presence guiding me. The world feels meaningful *(Wise Elder)*
(c) A spacious, settled aliveness. Inner and outer feel of one piece *(The Self)*
(d) Things feel whole. The world is mostly as it should be *(Unconscious Wholeness)*
(e) Time to get going. There's a lot to do and people are counting on me *(Persona)*
(f) A weight of something I've been refusing to look at *(Shadow Work)*

**2. The dream that returns to you most often is:**

(a) Being in front of an audience, naked or unprepared. A role I'm failing or succeeding at *(Persona)*
(b) Pursued by, or attacked by, a dark figure I can't quite face *(Shadow Work)*
(c) A captivating or terrifying figure carrying the energy I most lack *(Anima/Animus)*
(d) A wise old man or wise old woman speaking to me *(Wise Elder)*
(e) Mandalas, circles, sacred geometry, the union of opposites *(The Self)*
(f) Open landscapes, paradise, untroubled places *(Unconscious Wholeness)*

**3. The question your inner life is asking most insistently right now:**

(a) What does it mean to be whole? *(The Self)*
(b) There is no question. Things just are *(Unconscious Wholeness)*
(c) How do I succeed at the role I've taken on? *(Persona)*
(d) Who am I beneath the surface? What have I refused to look at? *(Shadow Work)*
(e) What is missing in me, and where does it live in others? *(Anima/Animus)*
(f) What is the deeper wisdom of this moment trying to teach me? *(Wise Elder)*

**4. What you most often put onto other people that is really yours:**

(a) My judgments. The qualities I refuse in myself, I condemn in them *(Shadow Work)*
(b) My longing. The qualities I lack, I find irresistible in them *(Anima/Animus)*
(c) My wish for guidance. I keep looking for the teacher who has the answer *(Wise Elder)*
(d) Less and less. I usually catch myself doing it *(The Self)*
(e) Nothing that I notice. People are as they appear *(Unconscious Wholeness)*
(f) My ideals. The version of who I should be *(Persona)*

**5. The figure you've been most preoccupied with lately:**

(a) A teacher, elder, guide. Someone who seems to know *(Wise Elder)*
(b) No external figure. The work has turned inward *(The Self)*
(c) No particular figure. Life is full enough as it is *(Unconscious Wholeness)*
(d) A role model. Someone whose external life I want to emulate *(Persona)*
(e) A person who repels me, or whom I can't stop judging *(Shadow Work)*
(f) A figure who fascinates or haunts me, who carries a quality I lack *(Anima/Animus)*

**6. When you think about the version of you that's "real":**

(a) It's whoever I am, day to day. No hidden version *(Unconscious Wholeness)*
(b) It's who I am at work and with the people who rely on me. What I do is who I am *(Persona)*
(c) There's a hidden me I'm slowly meeting. The "real" me is more complex than I thought *(Shadow Work)*
(d) The real me includes qualities I've avoided. I'm growing into them *(Anima/Animus)*
(e) The real me carries a wisdom I'm only beginning to live from *(Wise Elder)*
(f) There's no real me apart from the wholeness I'm gradually inhabiting *(The Self)*

**7. The kind of work your inner life is asking for:**

(a) Looking honestly at what I've avoided. Owning what I've disowned *(Shadow Work)*
(b) Resting in wholeness. Letting it live me as much as I live it *(The Self)*
(c) Building, accomplishing, becoming someone in the world *(Persona)*
(d) Integrating the qualities I usually project. Becoming inwardly fuller *(Anima/Animus)*
(e) Nothing specific. Just to live my life *(Unconscious Wholeness)*
(f) Listening to the inner voice of wisdom. Letting it teach me *(Wise Elder)*

**8. If your life is a road, what is slowing you down on it right now:**

(a) I'm waiting to be shown the next step, and it hasn't come yet *(Wise Elder)*
(b) Nothing. The road is fine as it is *(Unconscious Wholeness)*
(c) Someone, or something, I keep chasing that stays out of reach *(Anima/Animus)*
(d) Something behind me I keep not turning round to face *(Shadow Work)*
(e) Nothing needs to hurry. I've stopped needing to arrive *(The Self)*
(f) I'm not far enough along. Others are ahead of me *(Persona)*

### Q1 Step 3 — Score inline

Tally **stage content tags** (not letters) across Q1-Q8. The dominant stage is the user's primary stage.

**Tie handling:** if two **adjacent** stages tie (adjacent in the arc order above), place the user at the **later** stage (the work has moved forward). If two **non-adjacent** stages tie, present both readings and let the user pick which fits.

### Q1 Step 4 — Render the result

**ASCII arc first**, primary stage lit, a soft halo (◌) on the adjacent stage(s) — one back, one forward:

```
  Unconscious  →  Persona  →  Shadow  →  Anima/Animus  →  Wise Elder  →  The Self
   Wholeness                    ●            ◌
```

Then the stage name and a 2-3 sentence reading grounded in the user's actual answers, naming that stage's themes, gift, and typical challenge. Held as hypothesis. Use the softened lexicon: "the disowned" alongside "the shadow," "the inner partner" / "contra-pole" alongside "anima/animus," "the inner elder" alongside "the wise old man/woman." Avoid gender-binary framing of anima/animus.

Example microcopy:
> *You're in the work of the **Shadow**. You've started meeting the parts of yourself you'd refused to see. The gift here is honesty. The danger is getting stuck condemning what you've found instead of welcoming it home.*

End with confidence label and CTA toward Q2:
> *This places your primary stage only. Q2 reveals what's still active from earlier stages and what's emerging next. Confidence: rough.*

### Q1 Step 5 — Write the Q1 reading

Prepend to `context-library/maps/individuation.md`:

```markdown
## Q1 Reading — YYYY-MM-DD

**Confidence:** rough
**Primary stage:** [Stage name]
**Adjacent stage(s):** [one back / one forward, or "none"]

[The 2-3 sentence reading]

```

If the file does not exist, create it with this section and a note that the full map is generated by `/maps individuation`.

### Q1 Step 6 — Offer to refresh the map

If a fuller Individuation map already exists, offer to refresh it, merging Q1 with prior readings + harvested notes (NUM-115/116 pattern). If no map exists yet, skip silently.

## Q2 — Full stage profile (8-10 min)

**Gate:** Q2 needs Q1. If `individuation.md` has no `## Q1 Reading`, run Q1 first. If Q1 exists, open by surfacing it: *"Q1 placed you in [stage]. Let's see what else is moving in you and what's emerging."*

Q2 resolves the **full profile**: which stage is primary, which earlier stages are still active (trailing), which is emerging, which is dormant, and which (if any) is in shadow (actively avoided). 14 questions in three sections. Present one at a time, no commentary between. **State labels in parentheses are for your scoring only — never show them to the user.** Option order is shuffled.

### Q2 Section 1 — Stage scan (6 questions, one per stage)

**1. Unconscious Wholeness — simply living, at ease, before any pull to look inward:**
(a) This is where I live. I haven't really felt the call to deeper inquiry *(primary)*
(b) Active. I can return to a felt sense of simple wholeness when I rest *(active)*
(c) Pulling at me. I'm being asked to return to a simpler state under all the work *(emerging)*
(d) Not in play. The work has moved past it *(dormant)*
(e) I avoid it. I distrust any felt sense of "just being." It feels like denial *(shadow)*

**2. Persona — building and living the social self:**
(a) Active. I tend my role in the world, but it doesn't define me anymore *(active)*
(b) I avoid it. Performing or fitting myself to a role feels false *(shadow)*
(c) This is where I live. My work, my role, my reputation are the centre of my life *(primary)*
(d) Pulling at me. I'm being asked to build something visible in the world *(emerging)*
(e) Dormant. I've moved beyond identifying with role *(dormant)*

**3. Shadow Work — meeting and owning what you've disowned:**
(a) Pulling at me. I'm just starting to glimpse what I've been avoiding *(emerging)*
(b) Dormant. Not on my plate right now *(dormant)*
(c) I avoid it. Going there feels too dark, too destabilising *(shadow)*
(d) This is where I live. I'm in the middle of meeting parts of me I'd refused to see *(primary)*
(e) Active. I've done some of this work and I return to it when something surfaces *(active)*

**4. Anima/Animus — engaging the qualities you usually project onto an idealised other:**
(a) I avoid it. The "inner partner" feels woo or off-limits to me *(shadow)*
(b) This is where I live. I'm integrating qualities I've spent years projecting outward *(primary)*
(c) Active. I've done some of this. The inner partner is no longer fully outside me *(active)*
(d) Dormant. Not in play right now *(dormant)*
(e) Pulling at me. I'm noticing a longing for qualities I see in others but lack in myself *(emerging)*

**5. Wise Elder — listening to the inner sage:**
(a) Dormant. No inner elder is asking for my attention *(dormant)*
(b) Pulling at me. I'm starting to sense an inner voice of wisdom *(emerging)*
(c) Active. I have moments of contact with an inner guide *(active)*
(d) I avoid it. I distrust any "wise voice within." It sounds inflated to me *(shadow)*
(e) This is where I live. An inner wisdom is teaching me. I'm careful not to identify with it *(primary)*

**6. The Self — the lived realisation of wholeness:**
(a) Active. I touch wholeness in moments. It's not yet constant *(active)*
(b) Dormant. I don't think about this *(dormant)*
(c) Pulling at me. I sense a wholeness on the horizon, asking me toward it *(emerging)*
(d) This is where I live. Wholeness is the ground I orient to, even when I lose it *(primary)*
(e) I avoid it. "Wholeness" sounds like spiritual bypass or self-deception *(shadow)*

### Q2 Section 2 — Archetypal probes (4 questions)

Each adds a +1 nudge to the stage it implies, confirming or refining the scan.

**7. Where your mind goes as you fall asleep, lately:** *(Q1 already asks about dreams, so this does not.)*
(a) Tomorrow. What I have to get done, and how it will be judged *(Persona)*
(b) Something I said or did that I'd rather not look at *(Shadow Work)*
(c) Someone I can't stop thinking about *(Anima/Animus)*
(d) A question I'm waiting on, hoping the answer comes from deeper than thought *(Wise Elder)*
(e) A quiet sense that the day, good and bad, belongs together *(The Self)*
(f) Nowhere much. I just fall asleep *(Unconscious Wholeness)*
(g) It's different every night *(no nudge)*

**8. What triggers your strongest emotional reactions in others?**
(a) Their flaws. I have a list of people I judge harshly *(Shadow Work)*
(b) Their qualities I lack. Their fire, depth, beauty, freedom *(Anima/Animus)*
(c) Their wisdom. I'm hungry for elders, teachers, guides *(Wise Elder)*
(d) Their authenticity. The ones who seem fully themselves *(The Self)*
(e) Their success. The ones who've built what I haven't *(Persona)*
(f) Honestly, not much. Other people are mostly fine to me *(Unconscious Wholeness)*

**9. Where you are in the developmental arc, on a felt level:**
(a) Early adulthood. Building, becoming, claiming territory *(Persona)*
(b) The midlife crossing. The structures I built no longer fit. Something is asking to be undone *(Shadow Work / Anima-Animus)*
(c) Later adulthood. The work is integration, depth, harvest *(Wise Elder / The Self)*
(d) Before any of this. I haven't felt the call *(Unconscious Wholeness)*
(e) None of these fits how it feels *(no nudge)*

**10. The inner figure who's most present in moments of stillness:**
(a) None in particular *(Unconscious Wholeness)*
(b) An idealised version of me. Who I want to be in the world *(Persona)*
(c) A figure I'd rather not meet. Something I've been turning from *(Shadow Work)*
(d) An inner beloved, an inner partner, a figure who fascinates me *(Anima/Animus)*
(e) An inner elder, a wise voice *(Wise Elder)*
(f) A spacious, holding presence that is also me *(The Self)*

### Q2 Section 3 — Cross-cutting Likert (4 items, 1-5)

Ask each as "1 = not at all true, 5 = very true." Each rating speaks for the stages named after it and counts like a probe: a 5 adds +1 to each of those stages, a 4 adds +½, a 3 adds nothing, a 2 takes ½ away and a 1 takes 1 away.

**11.** *I notice my projections in real time and can sometimes take them back.* *(Shadow Work, Anima/Animus)*
**12.** *The figures in my dreams or imagination feel like teachers, not just dreams.* *(Wise Elder)*
**13.** *I'm being asked to grow into qualities I've avoided or only admired in others.* *(Anima/Animus)*
**14.** *There is a wholeness in me that isn't dependent on circumstance.* *(The Self)*

### Q2 Step 4 — Resolve and render

- From Section 1, assign each of the 6 stages one state: primary / active / emerging / dormant / shadow. Typical shape: 1 primary, 1-2 active, 0-1 emerging, 0-1 shadow.
- Section 2 probes add +1 nudges and Section 3 ratings add or remove up to 1 (see above). If Q1 exists, its stage gets +½. Where a stage's total is above zero, mark that state's confidence HIGH, otherwise MEDIUM (the scan still decides the states).
- If the scan names several stages as primary, the one with the highest total keeps it and the others become active. If it names none, the active stage with the highest total becomes primary, and say it was inferred.
- If Q1 exists, end the summary with one sentence saying whether this agrees with it (*"This agrees with your first reading…"* or *"Your first reading placed you in X. This fuller one places you in Y."*).
- Render the ASCII arc with all 6 stages, each styled by state, e.g.:

```
  Unconscious  →  Persona  →  Shadow  →  Anima/Animus  →  Wise Elder  →  The Self
   Wholeness        ◌(active)   ●(primary)  ◌(emerging)    ·(shadow)      ·(dormant)

   ●  primary   ◌  active / emerging   ·  dormant / shadow (shadow noted in prose)
```

- Give a 1-2 sentence reading per active stage, then a summary line naming the primary, the trailing, the emerging, and any shadow, plus the present work. Confidence label: **deeper**. Use the softened lexicon and keep jargon light.

### Q2 Step 5 — Write the Q2 reading

Prepend a `## Q2 Reading — YYYY-MM-DD` section (above the Q1 reading) to `individuation.md` with: **Confidence: deeper**, the state per stage, the summary line, and a short bullet list of the per-active-stage readings.

### Q2 Step 6 — Offer to refresh the map

If a fuller Individuation map exists, offer to refresh it, merging Q2 with Q1 and harvested notes; the merged map should raise the confidence in its header.

## Q3 — Long reading (20-30 min)

The longest read: a settled full stage profile woven with the user's own words. 22 questions across seven sections — open-ended (~40% of the signal), multiple-choice, and Likert (1-5). One at a time, no commentary between. **State/stage tags are for your scoring only — never show them.** Shuffle multiple-choice option order. If Q1/Q2 exist, open by surfacing the latest reading; standalone-capable otherwise (Section 2 is the baseline). **No Q3 question repeats a Q1 or Q2 question**: Q3 reaches the same stages through different situations, so ask Q3 exactly as written here. Softened lexicon; describe the lived experience before naming the stage; sidestep gender-binary framing of the inner partner.

### Q3 Section 1 — Settling in (2 open-ended)

**1.** *Take a breath. In your own words: when you look at the work of your own becoming, what's most alive right now? What's been asking for you?*
**2.** *In plain language, who are you underneath what you do and how you're seen? Describe that person as they are right now.*

### Q3 Section 2 — Stage scan through lived situations (6 multiple-choice)

Q2 asked how present each stage is. Q3 asks the same thing through a concrete situation, so nothing repeats: each option still maps to *(primary)* / *(active)* / *(emerging)* / *(dormant)* / *(shadow)*. Shuffle, hide tags.

**3. A free afternoon, with nothing to do and nobody needing you:** *(Unconscious Wholeness)*
- It's lovely, and that's all. I don't go looking for anything deeper in it *(primary)*
- I drop into an easy, whole feeling, the way I could as a child *(active)*
- I keep wishing I could simply be in it, without the inner work getting in the way *(emerging)*
- It's a pleasant gap. It doesn't touch my inner life much *(dormant)*
- I get uneasy. Simple contentment feels like hiding from something *(shadow)*

**4. When your work, role or standing is questioned, or taken away for a while:** *(Persona)*
- It stings and I tend to it, but I'm still myself underneath *(active)*
- I keep clear of roles and titles, so there's nothing there to lose *(shadow)*
- It shakes me badly. Without it I hardly know who I am *(primary)*
- There isn't much to take yet. I want a place in the world I can build and stand in *(emerging)*
- It barely registers. My role sits lightly on me now *(dormant)*

**5. When someone tells you something unflattering about yourself, and it turns out to be true:** *(Shadow Work)*
- I'm starting to suspect there's more of this than I've let myself see *(emerging)*
- It doesn't come up much, and I'm not looking for it *(dormant)*
- I find a reason it isn't really true, and move on fast *(shadow)*
- That's most of my inner life right now. I keep meeting things like this and taking them in *(primary)*
- It still lands hard, but I know how to sit with it and own it *(active)*

**6. When you fall for someone, or for someone's way of being, what happens to it over time:** *(Anima/Animus)*
- I'd rather not look at it. Talk of an "inner partner" puts me off *(shadow)*
- I'm learning to find in myself what I fell for in them. That's the work right now *(primary)*
- I can usually tell when I've put something of mine on them, and take it back *(active)*
- It isn't a live question for me at the moment *(dormant)*
- I'm starting to notice I keep falling for the same quality, the one I don't have *(emerging)*

**7. When you face a choice you can't think your way through:** *(Wise Elder)*
- I reason it out as best I can. Nothing else seems to speak *(dormant)*
- I'm beginning to feel there's a deeper knowing in me I could learn to hear *(emerging)*
- Now and then I can drop below thinking and hear a quieter guidance *(active)*
- I don't trust "inner knowing". It is usually the ego in a robe *(shadow)*
- I wait, and something wiser than my reasoning usually answers. I try not to take credit for it *(primary)*

**8. On a hard day, when things go badly and you can't fix them:** *(The Self)*
- Some days I can feel a ground that isn't shaken. Other days it's gone *(active)*
- I just get through it. I don't think in terms of anything underneath *(dormant)*
- I sense there could be a steadiness under all of it, and I want to find it *(emerging)*
- Something steady holds underneath, even when I lose sight of it for a while *(primary)*
- Talk of an unshakeable centre sounds like a way of not feeling the day *(shadow)*

### Q3 Section 3 — Archetypal landscape (2 MC + 1 open-ended)

**9. The kind of story, film or book that gets under your skin most these days:** Someone rising or falling in public. Success, reputation, scrutiny *(Persona)* / A villain, or a character who does what I'd never let myself do *(Shadow)* / A love story where someone is changed by the person they fall for *(Anima/Animus)* / A mentor and a student. Wisdom being handed on *(Wise Elder)* / Opposites reconciled. A whole that finally holds every part *(The Self)* / Gentle stories where nothing much goes wrong *(Unconscious Wholeness)* / Nothing gets to me like that lately *(none)*
**10. The argument you keep having in your head with someone:** About what they did wrong, and why they're wrong *(Shadow)* / Trying to reach someone I can't stop thinking about *(Anima/Animus)* / Asking someone wiser than me what I should do *(Wise Elder)* / Mostly with myself, and I can listen to both sides *(The Self)* / Defending my record, my competence, my reputation *(Persona)* / I don't really have one *(none)*
**11.** *Open-ended: Describe a recent moment when you saw yourself clearly — not the mask, not the projection, but the actual texture of who you are. What did you see?*

### Q3 Section 4 — Adaptive deep-dive (3 open-ended)

From the running profile select 3 prompts: **1 on the primary stage**, **1 on the shadow stage** (or most-charged trailing if no clear shadow), **1 on the emerging stage** (or a second trailing if none emerged).

- **Unconscious Wholeness** — *"Describe the felt sense of wholeness as you've known it. When was it most present? What did it feel like in your body? Has it returned, or has it been lost?"*
- **Persona** — *"Tell me about the mask you wear in the world. What does it gain you? What does it cost you? Where does it begin to feel like a cage?"*
- **Shadow Work** — *"What's the part of yourself you'd most rather not meet? Describe it honestly. What's it been doing in your life from the dark?"*
- **Anima/Animus** — *"Describe the qualities you most envy, fear, or long for in others. What would it mean to have them in you? What's been in the way?"*
- **Wise Elder** — *"Describe the voice of inner wisdom as you've known it. When is it most clear? Where is the danger of mistaking it for your own ego speaking with authority?"*
- **The Self** — *"Describe a moment when you felt held by something larger that was also you. What was happening? What did it ask of you afterwards?"*

### Q3 Section 5 — Projection and integration (2 MC + 1 Likert)

**15. A day or two after a strong reaction to someone has passed:** I'm still building the case against them *(Shadow Work)* / I'm still thinking about them, wishing I were more like them *(Anima/Animus)* / I'm asking what the reaction showed me about me *(Shadow Work — the owning is under way)* / I can usually see what was mine in it, and own it *(The Self)* / It's gone. I don't think about it again *(Unconscious Wholeness)*
**16. The threshold you're being asked to cross now:** From simply living, to looking inward at all *(Wholeness → Persona/Shadow)* / From living through the mask, to meeting what's underneath *(Persona → Shadow)* / From meeting the disowned, to welcoming it home *(Shadow encounter → integration)* / From projecting the inner partner, to integrating those qualities *(Shadow → Anima/Animus)* / From integrating the inner partner, to listening to a deeper wisdom *(Anima/Animus → Wise Elder)* / From listening to wisdom, to living from wholeness *(Wise Elder → The Self)* / Not in a clear transition *(none)*
**17. Likert (1-5):** *When a dream or a strong reaction unsettles me, I go back to it to ask what it wants from me.* *(Shadow Work, Anima/Animus)*

### Q3 Section 6 — The Self and longing (2 Likert + 1 open-ended)

**18. Likert (1-5):** *I can feel part of something larger than me without losing myself in it.* *(Wise Elder, The Self)*
**19. Likert (1-5):** *I can hold opposites in me — light and dark, ego and shadow — without needing to resolve them.* *(The Self)*
**20.** *Open-ended: What do you long for that you don't yet have words for?* (longing language reveals the stage most asking to emerge)

### Q3 Section 7 — Closing reflection (1 open-ended)

**21.** *If the deepest part of you — the part Jung called the Self — could speak to you in one sentence right now, what would it say about where you are and where you're going?* — use as a direct quote at the top of the reading ("The Self said: …").

*(Note: the canonical spec lists 22 items; question 21 here is the closing reflection. If you also surface a final "anything else" invitation, keep it optional and brief.)*

### Q3 Step 8 — Resolve and render

- **Closed answers.** Give each stage a weight: 1 if the scan (Section 2) says primary, ½ if active, about ⅓ if emerging, 0 if dormant or shadow. Add ¼ for each probe pointing at it (Q9, Q10, Q15). Add the three ratings (Q17-19) to the stages named after them: +¼ for a 5, +⅛ for a 4, nothing for a 3, −⅛ for a 2, −¼ for a 1. If Q2 (or Q1) exists, add ¼ to the stage it placed.
- **Written answers.** Read Q1, Q2, Q11, Q20 and Q21 and judge which stage each one most evidences. Leave the three deep-dive answers out of this judgement, because each deep-dive question names its own stage; use them for texture and quotes. Long answers count for more than one-liners.
- **Together.** The written answers count for up to about a quarter of the placement on this map (the 40% in the spec, cut back because prose is harder to read reliably than a chosen answer). In practice: a single clear primary in the scan holds whatever was written. When the closed answers are close or split, or name no primary, what was written decides. Only a stage the scan gave as primary or active can become the primary. Never place the user in a stage their closed answers gave no weight to.
- If what they wrote points to a different stage than the one placed, say so in one sentence (*"What you wrote leans toward X, which is worth sitting with."*). If an earlier reading exists, say in one sentence whether this one agrees with it.
- Final state per stage: primary / active / emerging / dormant / shadow; HIGH on deepened stages, MEDIUM otherwise.
- Render: the closing **"What the Self said"** quote at the top; the ASCII arc with all 6 stages styled by state (same logic as Q2); a 2-4 sentence reading per active stage woven with the user's phrases; **"The threshold you're at"** named explicitly (e.g. *Shadow Work → Anima/Animus*) with concrete practice; **"What you fear losing in the crossing"** reflected back from Q16.
