# /hawkins-questionnaire — The Hawkins Consciousness questionnaire

One skill for the Hawkins map of consciousness. It runs in stages and knows where the user is:

- **Q1 — Quick placement** (3-5 min). Finds the dominant band, then resolves a specific level through a short tiebreaker. Confidence after Q1: **rough**.
- **Q2 — Full register profile** (10-12 min). Resolves a state for all seven bands, names the specific level where a band spans several, reads the user's relationship to the 200 line, and checks for bypass. Confidence after Q2: **deeper**.
- **Q3 — Settled reading** (20-30 min). A long reading, partly in the user's own words, settling the profile, the crossing they're at, and what they fear losing in it. Confidence after Q3: **settled**.

The canonical content (the 17 levels, 7 bands, the 200 threshold, gradient states, threshold readings, crossings with practices, bypass checks, evidence signals) lives in `hawkins-framework.md`, in this skill's folder. Both this questionnaire and `/maps hawkins` draw from it — adapt the tone to where the user actually is; do not quote it verbatim unless a step below says the copy is fixed.

The map itself is generated by `/maps hawkins`. This skill feeds it structured readings and offers to refresh it afterward.

**Core principle — where you are, not who you are.** Carry this through every reading: Grief is not a failure and Love is not a graduation. Each level teaches something only that level can teach, and the upper levels carry their own pitfalls. The map is descriptive, never a leaderboard.

**The 7 bands (17 levels):** Despair (Shame 20, Guilt 30, Apathy 50, Grief 75) · Reactive (Fear 100, Desire 125, Anger 150, Pride 175) · Courage (200) · Trust (Neutrality 250, Willingness 310) · Reason (Acceptance 350, Reason 400) · Love (Love 500, Joy 540) · Spirit (Peace 600, Enlightenment 700). The **200 threshold** splits force (below) from power (Courage and above).

**The numbers are gradient markers, not measurements.** Hawkins' calibration method is contested. Frame every result as a self-read of a register of experience. Nobody lives at one level.

**Questions stay in plain experiential language.** No band names, level names or numbers in the answer options. Names and numbers appear only on the result screen. When naming a level back, describe the lived experience first, then the label: *"You're in a register of clear thinking and orderly understanding. That's Reason, around 400."*

## Step 0 — Figure out where the user is

Read `context-library/maps/hawkins.md` if it exists and check which stages have run (`## Q1 Reading`, `## Q2 Reading`). Route:

- **Nothing done yet** → run Q1.
- **Q1 done, Q2 not** → offer Q2: *"You've got the quick placement. Ready to go deeper? The next reflection (about ten minutes) reads every register on the map: where you live, what's familiar, what's gathering, and what you push against. It also looks at where you stand in relation to the two-hundred line."* If they decline, stop gracefully.
- **Q1 and Q2 done, Q3 not** → offer Q3: *"You've seen the full profile. The long reading (20-30 minutes) settles it, partly in your own words: the register underneath your days, the crossing you're standing at, and what you'd be afraid to lose in it. Want to do it now, or another time?"* If they decline, stop gracefully.
- **All three done** → offer to re-run a stage for a fresh read, or refresh the map from everything since.

Never push toward deeper stages. Q2 and Q3 are standalone-capable if the user asks for them directly. Honour an explicit stage request.

## Q1 — Quick placement

### Q1 Step 1 — Open

Present this intro, then begin immediately.

> *Where does your inner life sit right now?*
>
> *The Hawkins map traces levels of inner experience: from the heaviness of despair, through fear and force, across the threshold of courage, into trust, reason, love, and the stillness of peace.*
>
> *This reads the register you're living in now, not who you are. Every level on the map has its own lessons.*
>
> *Eight short questions. Pick the closest fit. At the end, you'll see where you are, roughly.*

### Q1 Step 2 — Present the questions

One at a time. Wait for each answer. Accept the letter only. **No commentary between questions.** Each question has 7 options, one per band. The options below are listed in the app's display order (a Latin square for Q1-Q7, a fixed shuffle for Q8), so the letter never maps to a fixed band; you may reshuffle. Band names are hidden. **The parenthetical tag is for your scoring only — never show it to the user.** Present the option text exactly as written.

**1. When you wake up, the inner weather is closest to…**
(a) Heavy, small, or convinced something is fundamentally wrong with me *(Despair)*
(b) On edge, grasping, or braced for the next hit *(Reactive)*
(c) Willing to face the day even if it is uncertain *(Courage)*
(d) Neutral — not great, not terrible, just here *(Trust)*
(e) Clear-headed and ready to understand what is happening *(Reason)*
(f) Warm, grateful, or quietly connected to life *(Love)*
(g) Spacious, still, or touched by something sacred *(Spirit)*

**2. Under pressure, your default move is…**
(a) Fight, flee, fixate, or inflate my importance *(Reactive)*
(b) Take one honest step anyway *(Courage)*
(c) Pause, breathe, and respond without drama *(Trust)*
(d) Map the situation and choose the sensible path *(Reason)*
(e) Stay relational — listen, care, repair *(Love)*
(f) Drop into presence beneath the story *(Spirit)*
(g) Shut down, hide, or go numb *(Despair)*

**3. What costs you the most energy right now?**
(a) The gap between what I know and what I will do *(Courage)*
(b) Holding steady when others are volatile *(Trust)*
(c) Overthinking without landing in clarity *(Reason)*
(d) Carrying everyone's feelings or dreading loss *(Love)*
(e) Trying to grasp what cannot be grasped *(Spirit)*
(f) Self-attack, heaviness, or sadness that will not move *(Despair)*
(g) Anxiety, craving, resentment, or ego on the line *(Reactive)*

**4. Pick the sentence that feels most honest:**
(a) Most days I can work with what shows up. *(Trust)*
(b) I see the pattern; now I choose my response. *(Reason)*
(c) Connection matters more than being right. *(Love)*
(d) Something vast is holding this, even when I forget. *(Spirit)*
(e) I am not sure I deserve a different life. *(Despair)*
(f) If I relax my guard, something bad will happen. *(Reactive)*
(g) I can meet this — one brave step at a time. *(Courage)*

**5. When you imagine healing, it sounds like…**
(a) Seeing clearly and making room for what is true *(Reason)*
(b) Letting my heart stay open without burning out *(Love)*
(c) Resting in silence that needs no explanation *(Spirit)*
(d) Finally forgiving myself and coming back to my body *(Despair)*
(e) Releasing tension, compulsion, or the need to win *(Reactive)*
(f) Acting from integrity instead of performance *(Courage)*
(g) Letting life be workable without controlling it *(Trust)*

**6. In groups, you are most often…**
(a) Tuned to tone, care, and what is unspoken *(Love)*
(b) Quietly aware of the field we share *(Spirit)*
(c) Withdrawn, invisible, or convinced I do not belong *(Despair)*
(d) Defensive, competitive, or scanning for threat *(Reactive)*
(e) The one who will name the hard thing *(Courage)*
(f) Steady — neither inflaming nor collapsing the room *(Trust)*
(g) The one who integrates perspectives *(Reason)*

**7. What do you want from a map like this?**
(a) Orientation toward what is sacred in ordinary life *(Spirit)*
(b) Language for the weight I carry without judging it *(Despair)*
(c) Honesty about reactivity without self-attack *(Reactive)*
(d) Permission to cross the line into constructive power *(Courage)*
(e) A read on how to stay open without burning out *(Trust)*
(f) Clarity that includes head and heart *(Reason)*
(g) Reminder that warmth is a valid way to live *(Love)*

**8. Right now, your dominant inner climate is…**
(a) Balanced — steady or open more than fighting *(Trust)*
(b) Contracted — heaviness, self-attack, or numbness is loudest *(Despair)*
(c) Radiant — warmth and delight are the leading notes *(Love)*
(d) Activated — guarding, grasping, firing up, or defending is running *(Reactive)*
(e) Still — deep calm or unity is touching through *(Spirit)*
(f) Awakening — I am crossing into constructive action *(Courage)*
(g) Lucid — including and understanding what is here *(Reason)*

### Q1 Step 3 — Score the band

Tally **band content tags** (not letter positions) across Q1-Q8. The band with the most answers is the user's current band.

**Tie handling:** if two or more bands share the top count, ask the user to choose between them. This is the one place band names are shown, each with its theme so the choice reads as experience:

> *Two bands are equally loud. Which inner climate feels closer overall?*

List only the tied bands, e.g. *(a) Reactive — fear, craving, anger, and defensive pride* / *(b) Love — warmth, joy, and heart-led connection*. Themes: Despair — contraction, shame, and frozen grief · Reactive — fear, craving, anger, and defensive pride · Courage — the threshold where force turns toward power · Trust — steady openness and willingness · Reason — acceptance and clear seeing · Love — warmth, joy, and heart-led connection · Spirit — peace and non-separate awareness. The chosen band goes on to Step 4.

**Reason band — "Within this clear-seeing band, which feels closer?"**
(a) I am mapping patterns objectively *(Reason — 400)*

**Love band — "Within this warm band, which feels closer?"**

## Q2 — Full register profile (10-12 min)

**Gate:** Q2 is standalone-capable. If `hawkins.md` has a `## Q1 Reading`, open by surfacing it: *"Your first reading placed you around [level]. This one resolves a state for every register, names the specific level where the register spans several, and reads your relationship to the two-hundred line."* Otherwise open with: *"A deeper read of where you are on the map. About ten minutes. We'll look at each band, resolve the specific level where you sit now, and see what's still moving in you, what's emerging, and what you push against."*

Q2 resolves a **state per band** — dominant / familiar / emerging / shadow / dormant (see the framework for labels). 14-17 questions in four sections: 7 band scans, 0-3 level resolutions (adaptive), 4 context probes, 3 Likert. One at a time, no commentary between. Tell the user: *"Answer for the register your days actually run in."* **State, band and level tags are for your scoring only — never show them.** Shuffle option order on every multiple-choice question. Present option text exactly as written.

**4.** *Being willing, without needing it to go a particular way:* *(Trust)*
That is where I live *(dominant)* / Known ground for me *(familiar)* / It is beginning to be possible *(emerging)* / That reads as passivity to me and I refuse it *(shadow)* / Not in play for me *(dormant)*

That is where I live *(dominant)* / It has happened. I cannot produce it *(familiar)* / Something is thinning *(emerging)* / That reads as escapism to me *(shadow)* / Not in play for me *(dormant)*

Take them in band order (Despair → Spirit) and stop at three. If no band qualifies, skip the section. Below it, honestly *(Reactive · relation: below)* / Right at it *(Courage · relation: crossing)* / Above it *(Trust · relation: above)* / Back and forth, week to week *(Courage · relation: oscillating)* / I say above and I think I live below *(no nudge · relation: bypass — self-named)*

**11. Where the most emotional charge sits for you right now:**
On something I cannot lift, and have stopped trying to *(Despair)* / On something I want, or someone I am angry with *(Reactive)* / On something I am about to do and have been avoiding *(Courage)* / On understanding something I have been getting wrong *(Reason)* / On a person, and on wanting to be better to them *(Love)* / Nowhere in particular. ### Q2 Section 4 — Cross-cutting Likert (3 items, 1-5)

**12.** *I move between very different inner registers within the same week.* (recorded; high suggests active crossing territory, use it in the wording, it does not change the scoring)
**13.** *There is a register on this map I refuse to see in myself.* (recorded; cross-reference with any shadow band, does not change the scoring)
**14.** *I can stay with a difficult feeling without reframing it into something more acceptable.* (2 or lower → reframing note)

### Q2 Step 5 — Resolve

1. **States from Section 1.** One state per band.
2. **Dominant band.**
   - Exactly one dominant → that band.
   - More than one dominant → keep the **lowest** (Despair is lowest, Spirit highest) and mark the others familiar. This map's upper levels are the easy ones to reach for, so the lower claim is the honest read.
   - No dominant → pick from the familiar bands (or from all bands if none are familiar) the one with the most probe nudges; on a tie, the lower band. Mark it dominant and note internally that it was inferred.
3. **Dominant level.** The level the user picked in Section 2 for that band, else the band's anchor level (Despair → Shame, Reactive → Fear, Courage → Courage, Trust → Neutrality, Reason → Acceptance, Love → Love, Spirit → Peace). Record the value and whether it is **at or above 200**.
4. **Threshold relation** from Q10, with its reading from the framework.
5. **Bypass flag:** dominant band is Love or Spirit **and** Despair or Reactive is in shadow **and** the user did not choose "I say above and I think I live below".
6. **Confidence per band:** HIGH if at least one probe nudged toward that band, MEDIUM otherwise. A probe never overturns a scan answer; it only adds or withholds certainty.
7. **Notes**, in this order, each only if it applies. The copy is fixed:
   - *Self-named gap (Q10 "I say above…"):* "That is a brave thing to notice about yourself, and it is the most useful answer you gave. Nothing in this reading is going to argue with it."
   - *Bypass flag:* "You live in one of the upper registers while refusing the ones below two hundred outright. On this map the levels are moved through rather than jumped, and the pattern where the higher ones rest on unmetabolised lower material is well enough documented that the map itself can reinforce it. This is not a verdict and it does not change your placement — it is work still available, and it is usually the most interesting work on offer."
   - *Spirit refused:* "You push against the stillness at the top of the map. That is a real position rather than a symptom: for some people rest that deep genuinely does read as dissociation, and there are good reasons a nervous system might refuse it. It is recorded here as your position, not as something to fix."
   - *Q14 at 2 or lower:* "You said staying with a difficult feeling without reframing it is hard. On this map that habit is the specific risk: the upper registers make excellent hiding places, and a feeling reframed before it is felt tends to come back at the same strength."

### Q2 Step 6 — Render the result

- The vertical scale with all 17 levels and the 200 line, each band styled by state: **●** where you live (with the resolved level marked ← you) · **◉** familiar · **◌** emerging · **⊘** refused · **·** dormant. Adapt the Q1 diagram; put the state marker on the band row.
- One line per band using the framework's state readings (*Where you live: [theme].* / *Known ground: [theme].* / *Gathering: [theme].* / *Pushed away: [theme].* / *Not in play right now.*), then a 1-2 sentence reading for the dominant and each familiar band, naming the resolved level for the dominant band (lived experience first, then the label and number).
- The **threshold reading** for the user's Q10 answer.
- The **summary line**, built like this: *"Most of your days run in [Band], around [Level]. That sits above the two-hundred line."* (or *"That sits below the two-hundred line, which is where most of a life is spent."*) + *"[Familiar bands] is/are familiar ground."* + *"[Emerging bands] is gathering."* + *"You push against [shadow bands]."* + *"The numbers are a way of talking about registers of experience, not a measurement of you, and nobody lives at one level."* Skip any clause with nothing in it.

- Confidence: **deeper**. Restate "where you are, not who you are." CTA: *"Continue to the long reading when you're ready."*

## Tone reminders

- Wise, sincere, grounded. Witness before wisdom.
- No em dashes in your own wording. No "unlock," "transform," "breakthrough," "high vibration," "raise your frequency," "high vibe."
- Numina lexicon: ground, witness, threshold, pattern, terrain, level, register.
- **The numbers are gradient markers, not measurements.** Hawkins' calibration method is contested. Honour the sceptic.
- **The 200 line is the map's hinge.** Surface it honestly and gently. Most of a life is spent below it; crossing back and forth is normal; a user at Anger is not broken.
- **Bypass is the most important safety check, and it's said gently.** Don't collude with an upper claim resting on refused lower material, and don't turn it into a verdict either. It never changes the placement.
- **"Where you are, not who you are."** No level is a lesser self. A Love placement is not a graduation; a Despair placement is not a failure. Higher levels have pitfalls too (Reason detaches, Love clings, Peace bypasses).
- **Hold the map lightly.** Hawkins enthusiast culture can lean cultish. Cross-reference Individuation (shadow work), Spiral Dynamics, and Wounds where the user's material points there, so the map stays one description among many.
- If the user places in Despair and their answers carry real weight (self-attack, wanting to disappear), slow down. Reflect with care, and if anything suggests risk to their safety, step out of the questionnaire and attend to that first.
