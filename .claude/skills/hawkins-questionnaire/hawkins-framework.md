# Hawkins Consciousness framework — canonical content (NUM-299)

The reference the Hawkins Consciousness Map and `/hawkins-questionnaire` draw from. **Use as reference, not script.** The map output should match where the user's inner life actually sits, in their language — never quote these layers verbatim.

Frame for audiences drawn to contemplative practice, emotional work, and maps of inner development. Hawkins' own calibration method (muscle-testing, numbers assigned to people and ideas) is contested. Treat the map as a **phenomenological self-read**, not a measurement. The numbers are gradient markers for talking about registers of experience. Nobody lives at one level.

**Core principle — where you are, not who you are.** This map reads a *current register*, not a fixed identity or a score. A Grief placement is not a failure and a Love placement is not a graduation. Each level teaches something only that level can teach, and the upper levels carry their own pitfalls. The map is descriptive, never a leaderboard.

---

## The scale

David Hawkins' map of consciousness: 17 levels from Shame (20) to Enlightenment (700). Numina groups them into **7 bands** (also called registers). The map is **calibrational and gradient-based**: one placement on a scale, with the bands above and below forming the user's emotional-energetic terrain. Rendered as a vertical scale with the **200 threshold** marked.

| # | Band | Theme | Levels (value) | Anchor level* |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Despair | contraction, shame, and frozen grief | Shame (20), Guilt (30), Apathy (50), Grief (75) | Shame |
| 2 | Reactive | fear, craving, anger, and defensive pride | Fear (100), Desire (125), Anger (150), Pride (175) | Fear |
| 3 | Courage | the threshold where force turns toward power | Courage (200) | Courage |
| 4 | Trust | steady openness and willingness | Neutrality (250), Willingness (310) | Neutrality |
| 5 | Reason | acceptance and clear seeing | Acceptance (350), Reason (400) | Acceptance |
| 6 | Love | warmth, joy, and heart-led connection | Love (500), Joy (540) | Love |
| 7 | Spirit | peace and non-separate awareness | Peace (600), Enlightenment (700) | Peace |

\* **Anchor level** = the level used when a band is placed but no level within it has been resolved (the first option of that band's tiebreaker in the app). Courage is a single-level band and always resolves to itself.

### The 200 threshold — Force and Power

The map's defining feature. Below 200, Hawkins says, a life runs on **force**: constrictive, self-protective, pushing, defending, wanting. From 200 up it runs on **power**: expansive, life-affirming. Courage (200) is the crossing itself and counts as above the line.

- Below the line: Despair, Reactive. Above (or at) the line: Courage, Trust, Reason, Love, Spirit.
- Surface it honestly and gently. Most of a life is spent below it. Crossing back and forth week to week is what the crossing actually looks like from inside. Sustained life above it is rare.
- A user at Anger (150) is not broken. They're inhabiting a level with its own logic and gifts, and the threshold is closer than it looks.

## The 17 levels

Each level: what it's like (short label + description), what it means to sit there, and the movement it holds. Gifts and pitfalls are held at band level below.

### Band 1 — Despair (below 200)

- **Shame (20)** — *Wanting to disappear.* A sense of being fundamentally wrong or unworthy, leading to withdrawal, self-attack, and wanting to hide. The heaviest place — yet simply noticing it is already the first movement upward.
- **Guilt (30)** — *Stuck in should-have.* Attention loops around past mistakes and self-blame, making it hard to forgive yourself or move forward.
- **Apathy (50)** — *Numb and checked out.* Life feels flat; effort seems pointless and change impossible. The work here is to find one small spark of caring again.
- **Grief (75)** — *Holding what was lost.* The heart is oriented toward loss and tenderness, with waves of sadness moving through. Grief at least moves energy that apathy had frozen. Tears here are a thaw, not a failure.

**Gift:** honesty about weight; tenderness; the thaw that starts the climb. **Pitfall:** turning the weight against the self, or mistaking numbness for safety.

### Band 2 — Reactive (below 200)

- **Fear (100)** — *Living on guard.* Attention is dominated by what could go wrong. Anxious and protective — yet fear is also energy that can become forward movement.
- **Desire (125)** — *Reaching for relief.* Craving and longing pull toward imagined completion. The same energy, freed from grasping, becomes healthy motivation.
- **Anger (150)** — *Fire of no more.* Charged energy that can clarify boundaries. A step up from fear because it pushes against limits; it fuels real change if it doesn't consume you.
- **Pride (175)** — *Proving and defending.* Self-image must be protected at all costs. It gives strength, but it's brittle underneath: pride breaks under pressure.

**Gift:** energy is moving again; wanting and anger are movement, which apathy lacks. **Pitfall:** being run by the charge; defending instead of facing.

### Band 3 — Courage (the threshold, 200)

- **Courage (200)** — *Turning toward life.* Willingness to meet experience as it is and act constructively. The pivotal turn: you face life and take responsibility. The first empowering rung.

**Gift:** doing the thing that is yours to do without waiting to feel ready. **Pitfall:** bravado; arming yourself with effort and control instead of facing.

### Band 4 — Trust (above 200)

- **Neutrality (250)** — *Relaxed okay-ness.* Less compulsion to control; flexible response. Setbacks lose their sting and you can let go.
- **Willingness (310)** — *Leaning into growth.* Available to learn and show up without guarantees. An inner "yes" to life; challenges met as openings.

**Gift:** steadiness without bracing; effort that becomes simpler and sustainable. **Pitfall:** willingness that is really a story about giving up; quiet managing dressed as trust.

### Band 5 — Reason (above 200)

- **Acceptance (350)** — *Embracing what is here.* Life met with fewer inner arguments. You take ownership of your experience without self-attack; even pain is workable.
- **Reason (400)** — *Clear seeing.* Integrative, objective pattern recognition. You make sense of complexity — powerful, though it can get lost in the mind alone.

**Gift:** clarity and ownership. **Pitfall:** detachment; clear seeing as a way of standing outside your own life; making acceptance the destination instead of the doorway.

### Band 6 — Love (above 200)

- **Love (500)** — *Heart-led connection.* Care and empathy guide action. Unconditional, inclusive care that isn't about getting something back.
- **Joy (540)** — *Grateful aliveness.* Spontaneous appreciation for being here, not dependent on circumstances. Presence itself feels nourishing.

**Gift:** warmth that doesn't depend on the other person deserving it. **Pitfall:** love becoming attachment or performance; warmth used as a hiding place for what hasn't been felt.

### Band 7 — Spirit (above 200)

- **Peace (600)** — *Undisturbed presence.* Calm background; reactivity quiets. A profound stillness where the sense of separation softens.
- **Enlightenment (700)** — *Non-separate awareness.* The sense of a separate self relaxes into wholeness. The summit of the scale, beyond ordinary description, and rare.

**Gift:** rest that needs no explanation. **Pitfall:** peace as bypass; stillness used to avoid what is still alive below; calm mistaken for depth.

## Gradient states (Q2 / Q3 / the map)

Every band carries one state. These labels are for result screens and the map, never in question copy.

| State | Label shown | Means |
|---|---|---|
| dominant | Where you live | the register most days run in |
| familiar | Familiar | known ground, still available |
| emerging | Emerging | gathering, not yet home |
| shadow | Refused | the register you will not own |
| dormant | Dormant | not in play |

One-line band readings by state: *Where you live: [theme].* / *Known ground: [theme].* / *Gathering: [theme].* / *Pushed away: [theme].* / *Not in play right now.*

## The threshold relationship (Q2 / Q3)

The user's own answer about the 200 line, with the reading the app shows for each:

- **Below it** — *You place yourself under the threshold, where the map says a life runs on force — pushing, defending, wanting. That is not a failing grade. Most of a life is spent there, and the crossing is made from inside it rather than from above.*
- **At the crossing** — *You place yourself at the two-hundred line itself, which the map treats as the single most consequential move on it. Courage is the name for what it takes, and it is unglamorous: doing the thing that is yours to do without waiting to feel ready.*
- **Above it** — *You place yourself above the threshold, in the registers the map calls power rather than force. Worth noticing that the levels below do not disappear; they stop running the show.*
- **Crossing back and forth** — *You go over and under, which is what the crossing actually looks like from inside. The map draws a line; a life crosses it repeatedly before it stays.*
- **I suspect I claim above and live below** — *That is a brave thing to notice about yourself, and it is the most useful answer on this page. The map is unusually easy to use as a costume, because its upper levels are describable from a distance. Naming the gap is the thing that closes it.*

## The crossings (Q3 / the map)

Six crossings between adjacent bands, each with a concrete practice. Name the one the user is at.

| From | Crossing | Practice |
|---|---|---|
| Despair | From flatness into wanting something again | Anger and desire read as worse than apathy and are not: on this map they are movement, and movement is what apathy lacks. Let yourself want one small thing this week and go after it badly. |
| Reactive | From reacting into doing the thing anyway | Pick the one thing you have been avoiding that is genuinely yours to do, and do it before you feel ready. Readiness arrives afterwards on this map, not before. |
| Courage | From effort into willingness | Find the place where you are still forcing an outcome that is not yours to control, and stop pushing on it for a week. Watch what actually happens rather than what you predicted. |
| Trust | From willingness into seeing clearly | Take the situation you are most sure about and write down the strongest version of the account you disagree with. Not to adopt it — to see whether you can state it fairly. |
| Reason | From understanding into warmth | Understanding someone is not the same as being kind to them, and this crossing is where that stops being a technicality. Do one kind thing this week for someone whose position you think is wrong. |
| Love | From warmth into stillness | Nothing to add here. Whatever practice you already have is the practice; this crossing is not made by doing more. |

## Bypass — the central reading risk

This map is unusually easy to **wear rather than inhabit**. Its levels are numbered and ranked, which invites using a self-report as a verdict, and its upper registers are describable from a distance: someone can talk fluently about Love and Peace while everything below 200 is refused or marked absent. Hawkins is clear that the levels are **moved through rather than jumped**, and the pattern of upper levels resting on unmetabolised lower material is well documented.

Checks, in order of weight:

1. **Upper claim + refused lower register** — living in Love or Spirit while Despair or Reactive is actively refused. (Q2's flag.)
2. **Above the line with nothing below in play** — placed at 200+ while Despair and Reactive are both marked dormant, not even familiar. (Q3's flag.)
3. **Reframing** — the user finds it hard to stay with a difficult feeling without reframing it into something more acceptable. The upper registers make excellent hiding places.
4. **Self-named gap** — the user says they claim above and live below. This is not a flag to raise against them. It is the most useful answer they can give; never argue with it.

**Bypass is said gently and is never a demotion.** It doesn't change the placement. It's work still available, and usually the most interesting work on offer.

**Refusing Spirit is not a problem.** "Rest that deep reads as dissociation to me" is a real and defensible position. Record it as the user's position, not something to fix.

**Scepticism is honoured.** A user who doubts that inner states can be ranked on a numbered scale is seeing clearly. Point them to maps that describe overlapping ground without ranking it (Jung's Individuation, Spiral Dynamics).

## Evidence signals (for reading the band from entries)

| Band | Key signals |
|---|---|
| Despair | shame, guilt, worthless, numb, apathy, stuck, collapse, hopeless, grief that won't move, self-attack, wanting to disappear |
| Reactive | fear, afraid, anxious, craving, desire, anger, rage, resentment, defending, proving, needing to be right, scanning for threat |
| Courage | facing it, doing it anyway, naming the hard thing, taking responsibility, a choice to step toward |
| Trust | neutral, okay, balanced, willing, letting it be, not forcing, open to what comes |
| Reason | clarity, insight, understanding, patterns, accepting, taking ownership |
| Love | love, loving, compassion, warmth, gratitude, joy, care, connection |
| Spirit | peace, stillness, calm, silence, unity, oneness, non-dual, presence beneath the story |

Keyword vocabulary to weight toward this map: *Hawkins / map of consciousness / levels of consciousness / calibration / force and power / Letting Go / surrender / emotional release / vibration*.

**Grief vs Reactive:** the questionnaire places Grief (75) in Despair. In entries, grief that is moving (tears, tenderness) reads as the top of Despair, close to the thaw; frozen grief reads deeper in the band.

## Placement (from evidence)

- Place the user in one **current band** with a level inside it, plus the **familiar** and **emerging** bands where there's signal, and any **refused** band.
- Be conservative and structural. Read the register underneath the events, not the vocabulary. Fluent upper-level language with no lower-band material anywhere is itself a bypass signal, not evidence of an upper placement.
- When two bands are equally supported, the lower one is the honest read: this map's upper levels are the easy ones to reach for.

## Cross-map connections

The Hawkins map tells overlapping territory with **Spiral Dynamics** (values rather than emotional register), **Individuation** (shadow work: a refused band here is often the shadow there), and the **Wounds** map (a wound's protector often runs the Reactive band). Surface a cross-map line only as genuine synthesis when both maps show signal. Hawkins enthusiast culture can lean cultish; hold this map as one description among many.
