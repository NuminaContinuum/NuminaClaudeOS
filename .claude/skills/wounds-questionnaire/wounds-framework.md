# Wounds framework — canonical content (NUM-121)

The reference the Wounds Map and `/wounds-questionnaire` draw from. **Use as reference, not script.** The map output should match what's actually activated for the user based on their evidence, in their language — never quote these layers verbatim. Dormant wounds still show all four layers, rendered dimly with a note that no strong signal has been detected yet.

Do not name source frameworks in user-facing output. Frame for audiences drawn to IFS, shadow work, depth psychology, and Jungian work.

---

## 1. Rejection

- **Core fear:** "I am fundamentally flawed. I don't exist in a way that matters."
- **Protector mask:** Withdrawal, invisibility, preemptive disappearing; perfectionism to earn the right to exist.
- **Emotional pattern:** Shame, unworthiness, chronic sense of not belonging.
- **Shadow expression:** Disowned desire to be seen, to take up space, to be chosen.
- **Integrated gift:** Deep discernment; sovereign self-knowing; capacity to truly see and witness others.

## 2. Abandonment

- **Core fear:** "I'll be left. I'm not enough to make people stay."
- **Protector mask:** Clinging, fusion, constant reassurance-seeking; dependency as a survival strategy.
- **Emotional pattern:** Anxiety, longing, separation fear; over-attaching to people and situations.
- **Shadow expression:** Disowned self-reliance and independence.
- **Integrated gift:** Secure presence; capacity for deep, loyal connection without losing self.

## 3. Humiliation

- **Core fear:** "I am shameful. I'm too much — or not enough. I'll be exposed."
- **Protector mask:** Self-abasement and chronic self-deprecation; OR over-achievement to preempt shame; people-pleasing.
- **Emotional pattern:** Worthlessness; "I'm too much / not enough"; relentless self-criticism.
- **Shadow expression:** Disowned pride, legitimate anger, and healthy self-worth.
- **Integrated gift:** Embodied dignity; profound compassion for others' shame.

## 4. Betrayal

- **Core fear:** "I can't trust. People will always let me down."
- **Protector mask:** Control, dominance, self-sufficiency as armor; hypervigilance around promises and commitments.
- **Emotional pattern:** Suspicion; self-reliance as a shield; "I'll do it myself."
- **Shadow expression:** Disowned vulnerability; genuine need for support and softness.
- **Integrated gift:** Integrity; reliable, trustworthy leadership; the capacity to actually be trusted.

## 5. Injustice

- **Core fear:** "The world is unfair. I don't get what I deserve."
- **Protector mask:** Rigidity, perfectionism, emotional numbing; coldness as protection.
- **Emotional pattern:** Resentment; chronic frustration; persistent sense that things are wrong.
- **Shadow expression:** Disowned imperfection, messiness, and "good enough."
- **Integrated gift:** Justice, precision, and moral clarity; principled action.

## Evidence signals (for reading activation from entries)

| Wound | Key signals |
|---|---|
| Rejection | Shame, invisibility, not belonging, perfectionism, fear of judgment |
| Abandonment | Anxiety about being left, reassurance-seeking, clinging, loneliness |
| Humiliation | Worthlessness, self-criticism, "too much," embarrassment, people-pleasing |
| Betrayal | Distrust, control, "I'll do it myself," feeling let down, broken promises |
| Injustice | Unfairness, resentment, rigidity, "it's not right," frustration at systems |

## Intensity tiers (from evidence)

- **Dormant (○):** No signal. Node shown faint.
- **Stirring (◉):** Some signal — mild patterns surfacing.
- **Active (●):** Clear recurring patterns in recent entries.
- **Acute (●●):** Strong, repeated signal across multiple entries or a significant recent event.

Be conservative. Most wounds are Dormant or Stirring for most people most of the time. Only elevate to Active or Acute with clear, repeated evidence.
