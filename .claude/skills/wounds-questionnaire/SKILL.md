# /wounds-questionnaire — The Wounds map questionnaire

One skill for the Wounds assessment. It runs in stages and knows where the user is:

- **Q1 — Quick read** (5-8 min). Locates which of the 5 primary wounds (Rejection, Abandonment, Humiliation, Betrayal, Injustice) are most activated right now — as dynamic states, not fixed identity labels. Confidence after Q1: **rough**.
- **Q2 — Deeper dive** (8-10 min). Resolves an intensity state per wound, the protector mask + emotional pattern of the activated ones, and which wound (if any) is disowned. Confidence after Q2: **deeper**.
- **Q3 — Settled reading** (20-30 min). A long, open-ended reading settling the full constellation — protector, shadow, and the integrated gift underneath each activated wound. Confidence after Q3: **settled**.

The canonical content for all 5 wounds (core fear + 4 layers) lives in `wounds-framework.md`, in this skill's folder. Both this questionnaire and `/maps wounds` draw from it — adapt the tone to what's actually activated for the user; do not quote it verbatim.

The map itself is generated by `/maps wounds`. This skill feeds it structured readings and offers to refresh it afterward.

---

## Step 0 — Figure out where the user is

Read `context-library/maps/wounds.md` if it exists and check which stages have run (`## Q1 Reading`, `## Q2 Reading`). Route:

- **Nothing done yet** → run Q1.
- **Q1 done, Q2 not** → offer Q2: *"You've done the quick read. Ready to go deeper? The next reflection (about ten minutes) shows the whole constellation — which wounds are loud, which are quiet, which one you might be guarding, and how each is protecting you."* If they decline, stop gracefully.
- **Q1 and Q2 done, Q3 not** → offer Q3: *"You've seen the full profile. The long reading (20-30 minutes) goes underneath the protection, into what each wound is guarding and the gift on the other side. Want to do it now, or another time?"* If they decline, stop gracefully.
- **All three done** → offer to re-run a stage for a fresh read, or refresh the map from everything since.

These are tender territories — never push toward the deeper stages. Q2 and Q3 are standalone-capable if the user asks for them directly. Honour an explicit stage request.

## Q1 — Wound landscape read

### Q1 Step 1 — Open

Present this intro, then begin immediately. No preamble beyond this.

> *Which wounds are asking for your attention right now?*
>
> *We all carry the five primary wounds in some form. What changes is which ones are activated — loud, present, shaping how we move through the world — and which are quieter. This reading finds where the charge is now.*
>
> *Seven questions. Pick the closest fit. There are no right answers.*

### Q1 Step 2 — Present the questions

One at a time. Wait for each answer. Accept the letter only. **No commentary between questions.** Preserve the honest, meditative quality of the sequence. These are tender territories — move slowly. Option order is shuffled and wound names are hidden, so the result can't be gamed. **The parenthetical tag is for your scoring only — never show it to the user.**

**Q1. When you're triggered in a relationship, the feeling underneath is most often:**

(a) "I've been let down. I can't fully trust." *(Betrayal)*
(b) "I'm not enough, or I'm too much for people." *(Rejection)*
(c) "This isn't right. This isn't fair." *(Injustice)*
(d) "I'm going to be left." *(Abandonment)*
(e) "I don't deserve this, or I'm somehow less than." *(Humiliation)*

**Q2. What you most work to keep hidden from others:**

(a) That you don't feel worthy of what you want. *(Humiliation)*
(b) How much you need to belong and be accepted. *(Rejection)*
(c) How angry you are at how things are. *(Injustice)*
(d) How much you need people, and how frightening that is. *(Abandonment)*
(e) That you don't fully trust anyone to follow through. *(Betrayal)*

**Q3. Your most automatic protective response in close relationships:**

(a) Clinging, or leaving before they leave. *(Abandonment)*
(b) Taking control, doing it yourself, or keeping people at arm's length. *(Betrayal)*
(c) Performing, disappearing, or seeking reassurance about your worth. *(Rejection)*
(d) Withdrawing when something feels unjust, or insisting on being right. *(Injustice)*
(e) Shrinking, going along with things, making yourself smaller. *(Humiliation)*

**Q4. The need that you can now see wasn't reliably met growing up:**

(a) To be treated fairly, without arbitrary harshness. *(Injustice)*
(b) To be able to trust the people closest to you. *(Betrayal)*
(c) To be accepted exactly as you were, without conditions. *(Rejection)*
(d) To be respected, not shamed or humiliated. *(Humiliation)*
(e) To know you wouldn't be left, that someone would stay. *(Abandonment)*

**Q5. The belief that still runs underneath your behavior:**

(a) "I'll end up alone." *(Abandonment)*
(b) "The world is unfair and nothing ever really changes." *(Injustice)*
(c) "I'm fundamentally not enough." *(Rejection)*
(d) "If I let my guard down, I'll get hurt." *(Betrayal)*
(e) "I don't deserve what others have." *(Humiliation)*

**Q6. When you behave in ways you're not proud of, it tends to look like:**

(a) Micromanaging, refusing help, or shutting down emotionally. *(Betrayal)*
(b) Putting yourself down, or staying passive when you should speak up. *(Humiliation)*
(c) Becoming needy, or abruptly cutting someone off before they can leave. *(Abandonment)*
(d) Harsh self-criticism, or judging others for not meeting your standard. *(Rejection)*
(e) Stewing in resentment, or holding a grudge long past its usefulness. *(Injustice)*

**Q7. The pattern that keeps surfacing across your relationships and work:**

(a) Chronic frustration at how things around you are handled. *(Injustice)*
(b) Never quite feeling like you truly belong, even where you're welcomed. *(Rejection)*
(c) Difficulty trusting others to follow through. *(Betrayal)*
(d) A fear of closeness and a longing for it, at the same time. *(Abandonment)*
(e) Avoiding visibility or recognition, even when you want it. *(Humiliation)*

### Q1 Step 3 — Score inline

After Q7, tally **wound content tags** (not letter positions) across all answers — the option letters do not map to a fixed wound, so score by the parenthetical tag on each chosen option.

- Wound with the most tag hits = **most activated**
- Any wound within 1-2 votes of the leader = **also present**
- Others = **quiet** (not absent — less loud right now)

If tallies are highly spread with no clear leader, name it: *"Your answers spread across several wounds — that can mean more than one is activated at once, which is real. Here's where the weight seems to sit."* Then proceed with the plurality.

### Q1 Step 4 — Render the result

**ASCII pentagon constellation first**, top 1-2 wounds marked, others quiet. Adapt to the actual results:

```
                    REJECTION
                   [○ quiet]

    BETRAYAL                      ABANDONMENT
    [○ quiet]                     [● activated]

    INJUSTICE            HUMILIATION
    [○ quiet]            [◉ also present]

    ●  most activated    ◉  also present    ○  quiet
```

Then a brief reading (2-3 sentences). Use their actual answers to name the specific patterns — draw on `wounds-framework.md` for the wound's territory but adapt to what they said; do not restate generic content. Held as hypothesis, not verdict.

End with the confidence label and a CTA:

> *This is a rough reading — a first location, not a verdict. Run `/maps wounds` to see how this fits with everything else in your journals and entries.*

### Q1 Step 5 — Write the Q1 reading

Prepend a Q1 reading note below the file header of `context-library/maps/wounds.md`:

```markdown
## Q1 Reading — YYYY-MM-DD

**Confidence:** rough
**Most activated:** [Wound name]
**Also present:** [Wound name, or "none"]

[The 2-3 sentence reading]

---
```

If the file does not exist, create it with this section and a note that the full map is generated by `/maps wounds`.

### Q1 Step 6 — Offer to refresh the map

After writing the Q1 reading, check whether a fuller Wounds Map already exists (content beyond the reading sections — a `## Constellation overview`, prose, or prior `/maps` output).

If it does, offer it as a natural next step:

> *"You already have a Wounds Map. Want me to refresh it with this reading to raise its confidence? I'll merge what you just answered with what's already there from your notes and conversations."*

If yes, run the wounds refresh (same logic as `/maps wounds`), merging the new Q1 reading with existing harvested notes and prior readings. If no map exists yet, skip silently.

## Q2 — Full wound profile (8-10 min)

**Gate:** Q2 needs Q1. If `wounds.md` has no `## Q1 Reading`, run Q1 first (or, if the user asks for Q2 directly, run its Section 1 scan as the baseline). If Q1 exists, open by surfacing it: *"Q1 found [wound] carrying the most charge. Let's see the whole constellation, and how each wound is protecting itself."*

Q2 resolves an **intensity state per wound** — acute / active / stirring / dormant / **disowned** (defended against, the wound-map analogue of a shadow state) — plus the protector mask and emotional pattern of the activated ones. 14 questions in three sections. One at a time, no commentary between. **State tags are for your scoring only — never show them.** Shuffle option order.

### Q2 Section 1 — Wound scan (5 questions, one per wound)

For each wound's territory the options map to *(acute)* / *(active)* / *(stirring)* / *(dormant)* / *(disowned)* — shuffle, hide tags. Treat "disowned" as live only if other signals (Section 2) contradict the dismissal.

**1. Belonging and being seen (whether you're wanted, or fundamentally flawed):** Loud right now, maybe after a recent rejection *(Rejection acute)* / A familiar, recurring ache of not quite fitting *(active)* / A mild background hum *(stirring)* / Quiet, I mostly belong *(dormant)* / That's not really my issue *(disowned)*
**2. Closeness and being left (people leaving, or needing them too much):** Loud right now, gripping me around a relationship *(Abandonment acute)* / Recurring: I cling or I leave first *(active)* / Stirs in certain relationships *(stirring)* / Quiet, I feel secure *(dormant)* / I don't need people that way *(disowned)*
**3. Worth and exposure (being shameful, too much, or not enough):** Loud right now, maybe after being exposed *(Humiliation acute)* / Recurring pull to shrink or over-achieve *(active)* / Surfaces in specific situations *(stirring)* / Quiet, I can take up space *(dormant)* / Shame isn't my struggle *(disowned)*
**4. Trust and betrayal (relying on people, or doing it all yourself):** Loud right now, maybe a recent betrayal *(Betrayal acute)* / Recurring control / arm's length *(active)* / Stirs when I must depend on someone *(stirring)* / Quiet, I can lean on people *(dormant)* / I trust easily *(disowned)*
**5. Fairness and the world (things being just, or rigged against you):** Loud right now, maybe a recent injustice *(Injustice acute)* / Recurring frustration or grudge *(active)* / Stirs when I hit something unfair *(stirring)* / Quiet, I accept imperfection *(dormant)* / I don't get worked up about fairness *(disowned)*

### Q2 Section 2 — Protector and pattern probes (5 questions)

Each adds a +1 nudge to the wound it implies.

**6. Your most automatic protective move under stress:** Withdraw, disappear, become perfect *(Rejection)* / Reach, cling, seek reassurance, or cut off first *(Abandonment)* / Shrink and self-criticise, or overwork to prove worth *(Humiliation)* / Take control, armour up, do it all myself *(Betrayal)* / Go rigid, cold, or righteous *(Injustice)*
**7. The feeling that visits most often when you're activated:** Unworthiness, not mattering *(Rejection)* / Anxiety, longing, fear of separation *(Abandonment)* / Shame, "too much / not enough" *(Humiliation)* / Suspicion, hypervigilance *(Betrayal)* / Resentment, chronic frustration *(Injustice)*
**8. What you most often disown or push away in yourself:** My desire to be seen, to take up space *(Rejection shadow)* / My self-reliance, my okayness alone *(Abandonment shadow)* / My pride, my anger, my self-worth *(Humiliation shadow)* / My vulnerability, my real need for support *(Betrayal shadow)* / My own imperfection, messiness, "good enough" *(Injustice shadow)*
**9. The strongest emotional reaction you have toward other people tends to be toward:** People who seem to belong effortlessly *(Rejection)* / People who might leave, or pull away *(Abandonment)* / People who shame or expose others, or who are shameless *(Humiliation)* / People who break promises or can't be relied on *(Betrayal)* / People who act unfairly or get away with things *(Injustice)*
**10. The wound you'd least want to admit is active in you:** Rejection *(Rejection disowned candidate)* / Abandonment *(Abandonment disowned candidate)* / Humiliation *(Humiliation disowned candidate)* / Betrayal *(Betrayal disowned candidate)* / Honestly none, I can look at all of them *(no disowned signal)*

### Q2 Section 3 — Cross-cutting Likert (4 items, 1-5)

**11.** *More than one of these wounds feels active in me at the same time.* (high → multi-wound profile)
**12.** *There's a wound on this list I reacted to quickly with "that's not me."* (high → confirms a disowned wound; cross-reference Q10)
**13.** *My protective patterns cost me in my relationships.* (high → protector masks strongly active)
**14.** *I can feel a wound without being run by it.* (high → integration beginning)

### Q2 Step 4 — Resolve and render

- Section 1 → one state per wound. Section 2 probes add +1 nudges (Q8 reads the shadow expression; Q10 + Likert Q12 flag a disowned wound). Likert validates shape. Confidence HIGH where scan + probes agree, else MEDIUM. Typical shape: 1-2 active/acute, 1-2 stirring, rest dormant, 0-1 disowned.
- Render the pentagon styled by state (●● acute / ● active / ◉ stirring / ○ dormant / × disowned); a 1-2 sentence reading per active or acute wound naming its protector mask + emotional pattern in the user's terms; a **gentle** callout if a wound looks disowned (offered as a question, never "you're in denial"); a summary line. Confidence: **deeper**.

### Q2 Step 5 — Write the Q2 reading and offer refresh

Prepend a `## Q2 Reading — YYYY-MM-DD` section (above the Q1 reading) to `wounds.md` with: **Confidence: deeper**, the state per wound, the protector masks of the active ones, any disowned-wound note, and the summary line. Then offer to refresh the Wounds Map (merging Q2 with Q1 + harvested notes; raises the confidence header).

## Q3 — Long reading (20-30 min)

**Gate:** aware of Q1/Q2 (surface the latest reading if present); standalone-capable otherwise (Section 2 is the baseline). The longest, most delicate read: a **settled** state per wound with the protector mask, shadow expression, and the integrated gift underneath each activated wound, woven with the user's own words. 21 questions across seven sections — open-ended (~40% of the signal), multiple-choice, and Likert. One at a time, no commentary between. **State tags are for your scoring only — never show them.** Shuffle option order. Always close toward the gift.

### Q3 Section 1 — Settling in (2 open-ended)

**1.** *Take a breath. In your own words: what's tender in you right now? What gets touched, again and again, in your relationships or your work?*
**2.** *If you had to name what you most protect yourself from feeling, what would you say?*

### Q3 Section 2 — Quick scan of all 5 wounds (5 multiple-choice)

Same five territories and state mapping as Q2 Section 1 (acute / active / stirring / dormant / disowned), shuffled, tags hidden. Use this as the baseline if Q1/Q2 weren't taken, and to select the Section 4 deep-dive prompts.

**3. Belonging and being seen** — Loud now *(Rejection acute)* / Recurring ache *(active)* / Mild hum *(stirring)* / Quiet *(dormant)* / Not my issue *(disowned)*
**4. Closeness and being left** — Loud now *(Abandonment acute)* / I cling or leave first *(active)* / Stirs sometimes *(stirring)* / Secure *(dormant)* / Don't need people that way *(disowned)*
**5. Worth and exposure** — Loud now *(Humiliation acute)* / Shrink or over-achieve *(active)* / Surfaces sometimes *(stirring)* / Can take up space *(dormant)* / Not my struggle *(disowned)*
**6. Trust and betrayal** — Loud now *(Betrayal acute)* / Control / arm's length *(active)* / Stirs when I must depend *(stirring)* / Can lean on people *(dormant)* / Trust easily *(disowned)*
**7. Fairness and the world** — Loud now *(Injustice acute)* / Frustration or grudge *(active)* / Stirs at unfairness *(stirring)* / Accept imperfection *(dormant)* / Don't get worked up *(disowned)*

### Q3 Section 3 — Protector and shadow landscape (2 MC + 1 open-ended)

**8. The protector you reach for most when a wound is touched:** Withdrawal, invisibility, perfectionism *(Rejection)* / Clinging, reassurance, or leaving first *(Abandonment)* / Shrinking and self-deprecation, or over-achievement *(Humiliation)* / Control, self-sufficiency, hypervigilance *(Betrayal)* / Rigidity, coldness, righteousness *(Injustice)*
**9. The part of yourself you most disown:** My desire to be seen, to take up space *(Rejection shadow)* / My self-reliance, my okayness alone *(Abandonment shadow)* / My pride, my anger, my worth *(Humiliation shadow)* / My vulnerability, my need for support *(Betrayal shadow)* / My imperfection, messiness, "good enough" *(Injustice shadow)*
**10.** *Open-ended: Describe a recent moment when one of these wounds got activated. What happened, what did you feel, and what did you do to protect yourself?*

### Q3 Section 4 — Adaptive deep-dive (3 open-ended)

From the running profile select 3 prompts: **1 on the most-activated wound**, **1 on the most-charged or disowned wound**, **1 on a second active wound** (or a gentle move toward the disowned one).

- **Rejection** — *"Tell me about belonging and being seen. Where have you felt fundamentally wanted, and where have you felt like you don't quite exist in a way that matters? What do you do to earn the right to be there?"*
- **Abandonment** — *"Tell me about being left, and about needing people. What happens in you when someone pulls away? What would it mean to trust that someone could stay?"*
- **Humiliation** — *"Tell me about shame and worth. When have you felt too much, or not enough, or exposed? What do you hide to keep from being shamed?"*
- **Betrayal** — *"Tell me about trust. Who has let you down, and what did you decide about people afterwards? What would it cost you to let someone all the way in?"*
- **Injustice** — *"Tell me about fairness. Where does the sense that things are wrong or rigged live in you? What does your rigidity or your resentment protect?"*

### Q3 Section 5 — Integration and the gift (2 MC + 1 Likert)

**14. When a wound gets activated now, you most often:** Get fully run by it before I notice *(unintegrated)* / Notice after the fact and feel regret *(noticing)* / Catch it partway and can sometimes choose differently *(integration active)* / Feel it move through without taking the wheel *(integration deepening)*
**15. The gift you can sense waiting underneath your most active wound:** Discernment, the capacity to truly see and witness others *(Rejection gift)* / Secure presence, loyal connection without losing myself *(Abandonment gift)* / Embodied dignity, compassion for others' shame *(Humiliation gift)* / Integrity, the capacity to be genuinely trustworthy *(Betrayal gift)* / Moral clarity, principled and fair action *(Injustice gift)*
**16. Likert (1-5):** *I can feel one of these wounds without it deciding how I act.* (high → integration deepening)

### Q3 Section 6 — The gift and longing (2 Likert + 1 open-ended)

**17. Likert (1-5):** *There is something my woundedness has given me that I wouldn't trade.* (high → gift integration active)
**18. Likert (1-5):** *I can let myself need, be seen, or be imperfect, at least sometimes.* (high → shadow integration beginning)
**19.** *Open-ended: What do you long for that you don't yet have words for?*

### Q3 Section 7 — Closing reflection (1-2 open-ended)

**20.** *If the wound that's loudest in you could speak in one sentence, what would it say it has been trying to protect all this time?* — use as a direct quote at the top of the reading ("What your wound said it was protecting: …").
**21.** *(optional) Anything else that wants to be said before we settle the reading?*

### Q3 Step 8 — Resolve and render

- Combine Section 2 (state per wound), Section 3 (protector + shadow, lived moment), Section 4 open-ended (deep texture → HIGH confidence on covered wounds), Section 5 (integration level + named gift), Section 6 (gift-emergence + shadow-integration). Open-ended carries ~40%.
- Final state per wound: acute / active / stirring / dormant / disowned; HIGH on deepened wounds.
- Render: the closing **"What your wound said it was protecting"** quote at the top; the pentagon styled by state; a 2-4 sentence reading per active or acute wound naming protector mask, emotional pattern, shadow expression, and the integrated gift, woven with the user's phrases; a careful disowned note as a question; **"What your work is"** with gentle concrete practice on the most-activated wound; **"The gift underneath"** — one line on the gift waiting under it. Confidence: **settled**. Always end toward the gift, not the wound.

### Q3 Step 9 — Write the Q3 reading and close

- Prepend a `## Q3 Reading — YYYY-MM-DD` section (above prior readings) to `wounds.md` with: **Confidence: settled**, the state per wound, the protector + shadow + gift of the active ones, any disowned note, the wound quote, and the per-wound summary.
- Offer to refresh the Wounds Map (merging Q3 with prior readings + harvested notes; raises confidence).
- Seed 2-3 reflection prompts focused on the most-activated wound and its gift.

## Tone reminders

- Grounded and honest. These are tender territories. Move slowly.
- No em dashes. No "unlock," "transform," "breakthrough," "holistic."
- Numina lexicon: pattern, wound, protector, activation, threshold.
- "Most activated right now" — not "this is who you are." Always.
- The user knows their own patterns better than any questionnaire. Hold lightly.
