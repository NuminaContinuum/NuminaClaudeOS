# /sixteen-nanas-questionnaire — The 16 Ñāṇas questionnaire

One skill for the insight-knowledge assessment. It runs in stages and knows where the user is:

- **Q1 — Quick placement** (3-5 min). Finds the dominant band on the arc of insight, then resolves the specific ñāṇa with a short tiebreaker. Confidence after Q1: **rough**.
- **Q2 — Full state profile** (10-12 min). Resolves a state per band (Active / Familiar / Emerging / Dormant / Pushed against), the specific ñāṇa in each live multi-stage band, and flags rapid cycling. Confidence after Q2: **deeper**.
- **Q3 — Settled reading** (20-30 min). A long reading in the user's own words: the live terrain, the crossing they are at, what they fear losing in it, and how many times they have been round. Confidence after Q3: **settled**.

The canonical content for the 16 stages and 6 bands (meanings, classical markers, lived markers, gift, pitfall, the state notes, the crossings and their practices, the cycle readings, the Dark Night care line, evidence signals) lives in `sixteen-nanas-framework.md`, in this skill's folder. Both this questionnaire and `/maps nanas` draw from it. Adapt the tone to where the user actually is; do not quote it verbatim, except the notes the framework marks as shown copy.

The map itself is generated by `/maps nanas` (file: `context-library/maps/sixteen-nanas.md`). This skill feeds it structured readings and offers to refresh it afterward.

**Core principle — where you are, not who you are.** Carry this through every reading. This arc is a **cycle, not a ladder**: people go round it more than once, and where someone is, is a phase of it, not a rank on it. Pre-Cycle is not "behind" Equanimity.

**Two care rules that never bend:**
- **The Dark Night is difficult, normal and passing.** Never a failure, never a diagnosis, never celebrated. Witness it. Whenever the live terrain is the Dark Night, include the care line from the framework. If the user signals they are not safe, stop the questionnaire and attend to that first.
- **A path moment is never inferred from one experience.** A Path & Review result says the user *describes* a threshold-like shift. It never certifies a path moment. Hold it as hypothesis, every time.

**The 6 bands (16 stages):** Pre-Cycle (off-map) · Early Insights (1-3) · Arising & Passing (4) · Dark Night (5-10) · Equanimity (11) · Path & Review (12-16). See the framework for the stage list.

**Questions stay in plain experiential language.** No Pali, no band or stage names in the answer options. Translated stage names appear on result screens, with the Pali as a secondary gloss for serious practitioners. When naming a stage back, describe the lived experience first, then the label: *"You're in a territory where everything appears faulty, not worth holding. That's the seventh ñāṇa, **Disadvantages** (Ādinava Ñāṇa), often called Misery."*

## Step 0 — Figure out where the user is

Read `context-library/maps/sixteen-nanas.md` if it exists and check which stages have run (`## Q1 Reading`, `## Q2 Reading`, `## Q3 Reading`). Route:

- **Nothing done yet** → run Q1.
- **Q1 done, Q2 not** → offer Q2: *"You've got the quick placement. Ready to go deeper? The next reflection (about ten minutes) looks at every band of the arc, resolves the specific stage, and sees what else is moving, what's gathering at the edge, and what you might be pushing against."* If they decline, stop gracefully.
- **Q1 and Q2 done, Q3 not** → offer Q3: *"You've seen the full profile. The long reading (20-30 minutes) is the most complete. Much of it is in your own words: what this has actually been like, the crossing you're at, and how many times you've been round. Want to do it now, or another time?"* If they decline, stop gracefully.
- **All three done** → offer to re-run a stage for a fresh read (the arc moves, so a re-read weeks later is normal, not a correction), or refresh the map from everything since.

Never push toward deeper stages. Q2 and Q3 are standalone-capable if the user asks for them directly. Honour an explicit stage request.

## Q1 — Quick placement

### Q1 Step 1 — Open

Present this intro, then begin immediately.

> *Where are you on the arc of insight?*
>
> *The 16 stages of insight knowledge map a journey the mind moves through as it begins to see what's actually here. The building blocks of experience, the way they arise and pass, the difficulty in the middle, the rest before something shifts. It's a cycle: people go round it more than once.*
>
> *This reads where your practice is right now, not who you are. Eight short questions. Pick the closest fit.*

### Q1 Step 2 — Present the questions

One at a time. Wait for each answer. Accept the letter only. **No commentary between questions.** Present the options **in the order listed**: it is the app's fixed Latin-square order (Q1-Q6) plus two free orders (Q7-Q8), already shuffled so letter position never reveals the band. **The parenthetical tag is for your scoring only — never show it to the user.**

**1. Right now, your meditation or inner work feels closest to…**
(a) Settling the mind — not yet in a clear insight arc *(Pre-Cycle)*
(b) Sorting out what is mind, what is body, and how they relate *(Early Insights)*
(c) Vivid pulses — things appearing and vanishing quickly *(Arising & Passing)*
(d) Uneasy — formations feel hollow, harsh, or hard to stay with *(Dark Night)*
(e) Even — neither pulled in nor pushed away by what arises *(Equanimity)*
(f) A threshold quality — as if something fundamental just shifted *(Path & Review)*

**2. When you sit, what grabs attention most often?**
(a) Noticing categories: sensation, feeling tone, mental images *(Early Insights)*
(b) Rapid flicker of micro-moments — almost cinematic *(Arising & Passing)*
(c) Dread, boredom, or disgust toward the same old patterns *(Dark Night)*
(d) Composed watching — experiences come and go without a story *(Equanimity)*
(e) Clarity about what was seen, or a quiet afterglow of release *(Path & Review)*
(f) General mindfulness — breath, body, kindness — without a map *(Pre-Cycle)*

**3. Which description fits your recent inner weather?**
(a) Bright and intense — sometimes blissful, sometimes overwhelming *(Arising & Passing)*
(b) Heavy — wanting out, yet unable to pretend it is fine *(Dark Night)*
(c) Spacious patience — formations are allowed without preference *(Equanimity)*
(d) Reoriented — less identified, more interested in what remains *(Path & Review)*
(e) Between cycles — practice continues but the arc is not obvious *(Pre-Cycle)*
(f) Curious, analytical — piecing together how experience works *(Early Insights)*

**4. Pick the sentence that feels most honest:**
(a) Beauty and meaning feel thin; practice can feel pointless *(Dark Night)*
(b) Strong concentration with little drama either way *(Equanimity)*
(c) Something irrevocable happened — I am integrating it *(Path & Review)*
(d) I am building a foundation — not sure I am “on the map” yet *(Pre-Cycle)*
(e) I can separate observer from observed more than before *(Early Insights)*
(f) Reality feels like a strobe — nothing holds still *(Arising & Passing)*

**5. What do you want help with on a map like this?**
(a) Trusting steadiness before any big leap *(Equanimity)*
(b) Gentle framing if a threshold shift is part of my story *(Path & Review)*
(c) Orientation — where does the insight journey even begin? *(Pre-Cycle)*
(d) Language for early discernment without overclaiming *(Early Insights)*
(e) Grounding when intensity spikes or seduces *(Arising & Passing)*
(f) Company through difficulty — without spiritual bypass *(Dark Night)*

**6. In community or teaching contexts, you tend to…**
(a) Speak sparingly about breakthrough — integration matters more *(Path & Review)*
(b) Stay general — samatha, ethics, everyday awareness *(Pre-Cycle)*
(c) Talk about noticing, causes, impermanence in plain language *(Early Insights)*
(d) Mention peak experiences carefully — they pass quickly *(Arising & Passing)*
(e) Admit practice has been rough or disorienting lately *(Dark Night)*
(f) Emphasize balance and non-reactivity as the gift *(Equanimity)*

**7. Your relationship to “progress” in practice is…**
(a) Progress feels like less reactivity, more room *(Equanimity)*
(b) I am not tracking stages — I am showing up *(Pre-Cycle)*
(c) I see micro-insights stacking into understanding *(Early Insights)*
(d) Progress feels like loss — old comforts do not work *(Dark Night)*
(e) Progress feels like a line was crossed — now living it in *(Path & Review)*
(f) Progress feels explosive — then it changes shape *(Arising & Passing)*

**8. If you had to name your dominant climate this month…**
(a) Gritty — dread, misery, or reactivity toward formations *(Dark Night)*
(b) Off-map — cultivating basics or between arcs *(Pre-Cycle)*
(c) Post-threshold — quiet completion or careful reflection *(Path & Review)*
(d) Discerning — sorting experience into workable pieces *(Early Insights)*
(e) Balanced — formations are seen without grasping *(Equanimity)*
(f) Electric — arising and passing is unmistakable *(Arising & Passing)*

### Q1 Step 3 — Score the band

Tally **band content tags** (not letter positions) across Q1-Q8. The band with the most picks wins.

**Single winner:**
- **Pre-Cycle** → resolves directly to *Pre-Cycle (off-map)*. No tiebreaker.
- **Arising & Passing** → resolves directly to **4 Arising & Passing**. No tiebreaker.
- **Equanimity** → resolves directly to **11 Equanimity**. No tiebreaker.
- **Early Insights / Dark Night / Path & Review** → ask that band's tiebreaker (Step 4).

**Tie handling** (if 3+ bands tie, take the two that sit **earliest on the arc**):
- **Neither tied band has a tiebreaker** (any pair of Pre-Cycle, Arising & Passing, Equanimity) → resolve to the earlier band on the arc.
- **Exactly one has a tiebreaker** → that band wins; ask its tiebreaker.
- **Both have tiebreakers** → ask a combined tiebreaker: *"[Band A] and [Band B] tied. Which nuance fits better?"* Merge both bands' options below, shuffle them together, pick the closest fit. The band of the chosen option wins and the option sets the stage.

### Q1 Step 4 — Tiebreaker (branched by band)

One question. Options in the order listed (the app's seeded shuffle). Hide the tags.

**Early Insights — "Within early insight, which texture is closest?"**
(a) One experience clearly conditions the next *(2 Cause & Effect)*
(b) Impermanence, stress, and non-self are obvious *(3 Three Characteristics)*
(c) Mind and body are distinct in each moment *(1 Distinguishing Mental & Physical)*

**Dark Night — "Within this difficult stretch, which tone is loudest?"**
(a) Things dissolve before they fully form *(5 Dissolution)*
(b) Weariness — beauty and pleasure feel thin *(8 Disenchantment)*
(c) Everything feels faulty or not worth holding *(7 Disadvantages)*
(d) A hollow dread toward experience itself *(6 Fear)*
(e) Strong wish to escape the sit altogether *(9 Desire for Deliverance)*
(f) Restless investigation — poking at how to get free *(10 Reflective Contemplation)*

**Path & Review — "Within path-and-review territory, which fits best?"**
(a) Looking back — what changed, what remains *(16 Reviewing)*
(b) Quiet fruition — relief that does not need drama *(15 Fruition)*
(c) A clean pivot — worldly habits feel far away *(13 Change of Lineage)*
(d) Noble truths line up with direct seeing *(12 Conformity)*
(e) A decisive cut — some confusion simply ended *(14 Path Knowledge)*

### Q1 Step 5 — Render the result

**ASCII arc first**, the resolved stage lit (●), a soft halo (◌) on the **other stages in the same band**, everything else faint (·). Pre-Cycle shows the marker off-map above stage 1, with stage 1 haloed as the first station ahead. The arc loops back at the bottom. Adapt to the actual result:

```
   PRE-CYCLE          ·      off-map
   EARLY INSIGHTS     ·   1  Distinguishing Mental & Physical
                      ·   2  Cause & Effect
                      ·   3  Three Characteristics
   ARISING & PASSING  ·   4  Arising & Passing
   DARK NIGHT         ◌   5  Dissolution
                      ◌   6  Fear
                      ◌   7  Disadvantages
                      ◌   8  Disenchantment
                      ◌   9  Desire for Deliverance
                      ●  10  Reflective Contemplation      ← you
   EQUANIMITY         ·  11  Equanimity
   PATH & REVIEW      ·  12  Conformity
                      ·  13  Change of Lineage
                      ·  14  Path Knowledge
                      ·  15  Fruition
                      ·  16  Reviewing
                      ↺      the cycle begins again

   ●  you    ◌  same band    ·  elsewhere on the arc
```

Then the stage name (translated, primary) with the Pali (secondary) and its band, and a 2-3 sentence reading grounded in the user's actual answers: the lived experience, the gift, the typical pitfall. Draw on `sixteen-nanas-framework.md` but adapt to what they said; do not restate generic content.

Example microcopy (Dark Night, Reflective Contemplation):
> *Right now, you're in the territory of **Reflective Contemplation** (Paṭisaṅkhā Ñāṇa), the stretch often called Re-observation. You've moved through much of the dark night and you're still looking, even though it's heavy. The gift here is endurance. The danger is exhausting yourself, or mistaking the heaviness for the end of the road. Equanimity is closer than it feels.*

Example microcopy (Pre-Cycle):
> *You're not yet on the map, and that's a real place to be. Your practice is building foundations (concentration, ethics, everyday awareness) without a clear insight arc running right now. The first station ahead is **Distinguishing Mental & Physical**: telling mental events from physical sensations as they happen. The work right now is enough continuity for the cycle to start.*

**If the result is in the Dark Night**, add the care line from the framework.

**If the result is in Path & Review**, add:
> *One note: eight questions can tell me you describe a threshold-like shift. They can't tell me a path moment happened, and nothing here tries to. Hold this as a hypothesis. What matters is what stays changed in ordinary life over the coming weeks.*

End with the confidence label, the cyclical note and a CTA:

> *This is a rough placement: your current dominant stage only. The arc is a cycle, and practitioners move through it again, often more quickly. Q2 shows which stages you've visited, what's still active, and what's emerging. Confidence: rough.*

## Q2 — Full state profile (10-12 min)

**Gate:** Q2 is standalone-capable. If `sixteen-nanas.md` has a `## Q1 Reading`, open by surfacing it: *"Your first reading placed you at [stage]. This one looks at every band of the arc, resolves the specific stage, and sees what else is moving."* If not, open with: *"A deeper read of where you are on the arc. Ten to twelve minutes. We'll look at each band, resolve the specific stage where the work is most active, and see what's still moving in you, what's emerging, and what you're pushing against."*

Q2 resolves a **state per band**: Active / Familiar / Emerging / Dormant / Pushed against. Up to 16 questions in four sections (13 if no multi-stage band is live). One at a time, no commentary between. **State, band and stage tags are for your scoring only — never show them.** Shuffle option order.

### Q2 Section 1 — Band scan (6 questions, one per band)

*"For each, pick the description closest to your current experience."*

**1. Practice that hasn't yet pierced into insight, or daily life mode between cycles:**

I can drop into it *(Familiar)* / Pulling at me. Something is asking me to return to ordinary ground, to step out of the work for a while *(Emerging)* / Not in play *(Dormant)* / I push against it. Sitting in pre-insight ordinary life feels like wasted time *(Pushed against)* — *[Pre-Cycle]*

**2. The first clear seeing of mind-body, conditioning, and the three characteristics:**
Familiar. I've moved through these. They're available when I look *(Familiar)* / Pulling at me. I sense the components of experience starting to come apart *(Emerging)* / Currently the most active terrain *(Active)* / Not in play *(Dormant)* / I push against it. I don't want to see things break down into parts *(Pushed against)* — *[Early Insights]*

**3. The vivid seeing of birth and death of moments, bliss, rapture, light, vibration:**
Not in play right now *(Dormant)* / I push against it. The intensity scares me, or feels false *(Pushed against)* / Familiar. I've been here. I can drop into something like it *(Familiar)* / Emerging. The energy is starting to gather *(Emerging)* / Currently where I live. Vibrations, light, charged seeing *(Active)* — *[Arising & Passing]*

**4. Dissolution, fear, misery, disgust, longing to escape, re-observation:**
Currently active. I'm in some part of this territory now *(Active)* / Familiar. I know this country. I've come through *(Familiar)* / Pulling at me. I sense it gathering. The brightness is starting to dim *(Emerging)* / Not in play right now *(Dormant)* / I push against it hard. I do everything I can to avoid this territory *(Pushed against)* — *[Dark Night]*

**5. Deep rest, balance, watching from settledness, no grip on what arises:**
Familiar. I've rested here before *(Familiar)* / Pulling at me. Something is asking me to soften, let go, watch from rest *(Emerging)* / Currently where I live. I'm at home here *(Active)* / Not in play yet. It feels remote *(Dormant)* / I push against it. Equanimity feels like cold detachment to me *(Pushed against)* — *[Equanimity]*

**6. The threshold, the cessation event, and the reflective aftermath:**
Not in play. I haven't yet crossed a threshold like this *(Dormant)* / Familiar. I have had at least one path moment, and I know the review territory *(Familiar)* / Pulling at me. The threshold feels close *(Emerging)* / Currently where I live. Something recently shifted and I am sorting it *(Active)* / I push against it. I distrust the whole idea of a "path moment" or "cessation" *(Pushed against)* — *[Path & Review]*

### Q2 Section 2 — Stage resolution (0-3 questions)

Ask **only** for the multi-stage bands (Early Insights, Dark Night, Path & Review) that scored **Active or Emerging** in Section 1, in arc order. Skip the section if none did.

**7. Within the early insights, the stage that fits most right now:**
Mind and body feel like distinct streams. Mental events are one thing, physical sensations are another. They are not the unified "me" I once thought *(1 Distinguishing Mental & Physical)* / I am seeing how every phenomenon arises from causes. The seeming continuity I called "self" is more like a chain of conditioning *(2 Cause & Effect)* / The instability of things is undeniable. Sensations do not last. The unsatisfactoriness of trying to grab them is felt directly *(3 Three Characteristics)*

**8. Within the dark night, the stage that fits most right now:**
Disgust, revulsion, disenchantment. The world feels tasteless and dreary. I am done with it *(8 Disenchantment)* / Re-observation. The hardest part. I keep looking even though it is painful *(10 Reflective Contemplation)* / Dissolution. Things only seem to vanish. Phenomena have a ghostly quality *(5 Dissolution)* / Desire for deliverance. An urgent wish to be free of all this. I want out *(9 Desire for Deliverance)* / Fear. Phenomena themselves carry threat. Existence feels frightening *(6 Fear)* / Misery. Everything appears dangerous, miserable, unsatisfactory *(7 Disadvantages)*

**9. Within the threshold, the moment that fits most:**
Something cessation-like just happened. A break, a discontinuity, a quiet that was not there before *(14 Path Knowledge)* / I sense the threshold close. Equanimity is deepening into something that feels close to giving way *(12 Conformity)* / Reflecting on what shifted. Making sense of what I have seen *(16 Reviewing)*

### Q2 Section 3 — Context probes (4 questions)

Each answer adds a **+1 nudge** to the tagged band. Some answers carry no nudge. *(Shuffle order.)*

**10. The pace of your sensate experience right now:**
Fast. Sensations flicker, vibrate, change rapidly *(+Arising & Passing)* / Slow and heavy. Sensations feel viscous, painful, or empty *(+Dark Night)* / Steady. **11. How long this current quality has been with you:**
Hours to days. It came on suddenly *(+Arising & Passing)* / Weeks to months. A sustained territory *(+Dark Night)* / Recently shifted. A line was crossed in the last days or weeks *(+Path & Review)* / Years. This has been my baseline for a long time *(+Pre-Cycle)* / Hard to say. Time has lost its grip a bit *(+Equanimity)*

**12. What practice, if any, preceded this current state:**
Intensive retreat. Long sits, silent practice, sustained continuity *(no nudge)* / Daily practice for years, but no recent retreat *(+Pre-Cycle)* / Sporadic practice. Periods on and off *(+Pre-Cycle)* / No formal practice. This is arising in daily life *(no nudge)* / Therapy, psychedelics, or non-meditation contemplative work that may have catalysed this *(no nudge)*

**13. What you most want from the next phase of this work:**
Stability. Ground. To feel sane again *(+Dark Night)* / Insight. To keep seeing what is actually here *(+Early Insights)* / Rest. To sit in what is here without striving *(+Equanimity)* / Integration. To live from what just shifted *(+Path & Review)* / A beginning. To enter the cycle in earnest *(+Pre-Cycle)*

### Q2 Section 4 — Cross-cutting Likert (3 items, 1-5)

**14.** *I notice phenomena, sensations, thoughts, emotions, arising and passing more clearly than I used to.*
**15.** *Something feels different than it did, in a way I can't fully explain.*
**16.** *There is a stage on the arc I avoid thinking about.*

The Likert items do not change any state. Read them as texture: Q14 low with an insight band Active → mention gently that the clarity may be newer or quieter than the placement suggests; Q15 high → supports a recent threshold, a strong Arising & Passing, or a strong Dark Night; Q16 high with no band Pushed against → name that some avoidance may not have shown up in the scan.

### Q2 Step 5 — Resolve and render

- **States:** each band takes the state of its Section 1 answer (unanswered → Dormant).
- **Nudges:** tally Section 3 per band.
- **Rapid cycling:** if two or more bands are Active and any two of them are **not adjacent** on the arc, flag rapid cycling. Keep every Active band Active; nothing is demoted.
- **No Active band:** infer one. Take the Familiar band with the most nudges (if no band is Familiar, the band with the most nudges overall; ties go to the earlier band). Mark it Active and say plainly it was inferred: *"Nothing came up as clearly live, so I've placed the marker where the rest of your answers lean."*
- **The marker** goes on the Active band with the most nudges; ties go to the **later** band on the arc.
- **Specific stage:** the Section 2 answer for that band. If the band was not resolved, name the band and not a stage (Arising & Passing and Equanimity are single stages; Pre-Cycle is off-map).
- **Confidence per band:** HIGH if a Section 3 probe nudged it, MEDIUM if it rests on Section 1 alone.

Render:
- The 16-stage arc styled by band state: **●** Active (with **◆** on the resolved stage), **◌** Familiar, **◦** Emerging, **·** Dormant, **⊘** Pushed against. One legend line below.
- A 1-2 sentence reading per Active and Familiar band (resolved stage named inside Active multi-stage bands), lived experience first, label second. Familiar Path & Review means the user *reports* having crossed before; reflect it in their words and never certify it.
- A summary line, built like this: *"The live terrain is [band]. [Familiar bands] is/are known country. [Emerging] is gathering at the edge. You push against [blocked]. [The cycle appears to be turning quickly.] This arc repeats. Where you are is a phase of it, not a rank on it."* Then one line on the work right now, e.g. *"Your work right now is to keep observing without exhausting yourself. Equanimity is closer than it feels."*
- The **rapid-cycling note** and the **pushed-against note** from the framework, verbatim, when they fire.
- The **Dark Night care line** if the marker band is the Dark Night.
- Confidence: **deeper**. Restate "where you are, not who you are."

### Q2 Step 6 — Write the Q2 reading and offer refresh

Prepend a `## Q2 Reading — YYYY-MM-DD` section (above the Q1 reading) to `sixteen-nanas.md` with: **Confidence: deeper**, the state per band (with per-band confidence), the marker band and resolved stage(s), whether the Active band was inferred, rapid-cycling / pushed-against flags, and the summary line. Then offer to refresh the 16 Ñāṇas map (merging Q2 with Q1 + harvested notes; raises the confidence header).

## Q3 — Long reading (20-30 min)

**Gate:** aware of Q1/Q2 (surface the latest reading if present); standalone-capable otherwise (Section 2 is the baseline). Open with:

- If a prior reading exists: *"Your earlier reading placed you at [stage]. This is the long one: several questions are yours to answer in your own words, and you can stop and come back at any point."*
- Otherwise: *"This is the long reading. Several questions are yours to answer in your own words, and you can stop and come back at any point. There are no right answers, only honest ones."*

23 questions across eight sections: 9 open-ended, 14 structured (multiple-choice + Likert). One at a time, no commentary between. **Tags are for your scoring only — never show them.** Shuffle option order. Voice input welcome on the open-ended questions; the user can pause and resume. If they pause after Q11: *"You're past the halfway mark. There's no rush."*

### Q3 Section 1 — Settling in (2 open-ended)

**1.** *Describe what your practice and your experience have been like over the last few months. Not the technique — what it has actually been like to be you.* (3-4 min)
**2.** *What brought you to this today? Say the plain reason, whether that is curiosity, worry, or wanting a name for something.* (2 min)

### Q3 Section 2 — Band scan (6 multiple-choice)

Each option keys to a state. Shuffle, hide tags.

**3. Practice building ground, with no insight arc obviously running:** That is where I am *(Active)* / I know that place. I am not in it now *(Familiar)* / Things are quieting back down toward it *(Emerging)* / Not where I am *(Dormant)* / I would find going back there unbearable *(Pushed against)* — *[Pre-Cycle]*
**4. Experience starting to come apart into parts, with causes visible:** That is the live terrain *(Active)* / Known country. I can find it again *(Familiar)* / It is beginning to happen *(Emerging)* / Not in play *(Dormant)* / I avoid looking that closely *(Pushed against)* — *[Early Insights]*
**5. Everything vividly arising and vanishing, often with energy and delight:** That is where I am *(Active)* / I have been there. I remember it well *(Familiar)* / Something is speeding up *(Emerging)* / Not in play *(Dormant)* / I do not want that intensity again *(Pushed against)* — *[Arising & Passing]*
**6. Dissolution, dread, disgust, or the sense of going over the same ground:** That is where I am *(Active)* / I have been through it *(Familiar)* / Something is turning that way *(Emerging)* / Not in play *(Dormant)* / I refuse to go there and I will not discuss it *(Pushed against)* — *[Dark Night]*
**7. Steady, unbothered awareness of whatever turns up:** That is where I am *(Active)* / I have had it. It comes and goes *(Familiar)* / Things are settling toward it *(Emerging)* / Not in play *(Dormant)* / That reads as numbness to me *(Pushed against)* — *[Equanimity]*

If the user picks "I refuse to go there and I will not discuss it" for the Dark Night, respect it: do not probe that band in Section 4 beyond the optional pushed-against prompt, and keep its reading to one line.

**11.** *Open-ended: Describe a specific recent moment when whatever is happening was most vivid. On the cushion or off it. What did you notice, in the body and in the mind, and what was it like afterwards?* Hint: *"Sensate detail helps more than interpretation. What it was like, rather than what it meant."* (3-4 min)

> *Scoring:* Q11 is the most diagnostic open answer. Read the texture against the framework's stage markers; it is the main way to name a specific ñāṇa in Q3.

### Q3 Step 8 — Resolve and render

- **States:** from the Section 2 scan (unanswered → Dormant).
- **Nudges:** +1 per band from Q9, Q10, Q15 and Q16. Q22 carries none.
- **Ranking:** structured nudges decide. The open-ended answers break ties only: a person's direct answer outranks anything inferred from their prose. The open answers still carry the depth of the reading (texture, quotes, the specific ñāṇa).
- **Rapid cycling:** two or more Active bands, any two non-adjacent. Keep them all Active.
- **No Active band:** infer one from the Familiar bands (or all bands), ranked as above; say it was inferred.
- **Marker band:** the highest-ranked Active band; ties go to the later band on the arc.
- **Specific stage:** carry forward the Q2-resolved stage if it sits in the marker band; otherwise name one only if Q11 or the deep-dives clearly describe its texture; otherwise name the band.
- **Crossing:** the Q15 answer; if skipped, the crossing out of the marker band.
- **Confidence:** HIGH on bands covered by a deep-dive or corroborated by nudges, MEDIUM for the rest.

Render, in this order:
1. **"What your practice said"** — the Q21 quote.
2. The 16-stage arc styled by band state (same symbols as Q2), the marker on the live band.
3. The **recent moment** (Q11) reflected back with the markers it carries.
4. A **2-4 sentence reading per Active and Familiar band**, woven with the user's own phrases; specific ñāṇa named where resolved; lived experience first, label second.
5. **"The crossing you're at"** — named explicitly (e.g. *Dark Night → Equanimity*), with the practice suggestion for that crossing from the framework.
6. **What you fear losing** — the Q16 answer surfaced back as witness, not fixed.
7. **Cycle reading** — the framework reading for the Q22 answer, plus what the user said in Q23 if they answered.
8. **Notes**, when they fire: the Dark Night care line (marker band is the Dark Night), the rapid-cycling note, the pushed-against note. Verbatim from the framework.
9. **Summary line**, built like Q2's, adding *"This is your first pass, and everything on it is arriving as news."* (first) or *"Many cycles in, your own sense of this will be finer than the map."* (many) before the closing *"This arc repeats. Where you are is a phase of it, not a rank on it."*

If the marker band is Path & Review, repeat the path-moment line from Q1 in your own words. Confidence: **settled**. Always restate "where you are, not who you are."

## Tone reminders

- Wise, sincere, grounded. Witness before wisdom.
- No em dashes in your own prose. No "unlock," "transform," "breakthrough," and no "awakening" as marketing.
- Numina lexicon: ground, witness, threshold, pattern, terrain, cycle, arc.
- **Hold the map lightly.** Stages describe what happens; they are not categories a person *is*, and not a leaderboard.
- **The Dark Night is real territory, not a diagnosis.** Don't pathologise it, don't celebrate it. Witness it. It is difficult, normal and passing.
- **Path moments are real events for some and contested constructs for others.** Allow both. A user who distrusts the framing has not failed the map, and no answer here certifies one.
- **The cycle matters more than any single stage.** A many-cycles practitioner is in a different relationship to the map than a first-time traveller; let the reading show it, and let their own sense outrank the map.
- Cross-reference: the Yin & Yang map shares the contemplative ground of stillness practice; the Wounds & Gifts map sometimes lights up in the Dark Night. Mention only when both maps show signal.
