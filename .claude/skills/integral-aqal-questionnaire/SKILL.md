# /integral-aqal-questionnaire — The Integral AQAL map questionnaire

One skill for the Integral AQAL assessment, run in stages:

- **Q1 — Home quadrant** (3-5 min). Finds the perspective the user most naturally inhabits. Confidence after Q1: **rough**.
- **Q2 — Full quadrant profile + lines scan** (planned).
- **Q3 — Long reflection + states** (planned).

Q1 finds the home quadrant only. Lines, states, and altitude are deferred to Q2/Q3. The map is generated by `/maps integral-aqal`. This skill feeds it structured readings and offers to refresh it afterward.

## The 4 quadrants

| Quadrant | Lens | What lives here |
|---|---|---|
| UL — "I" | Interior-Individual | Subjective experience, thoughts, feelings, intention |
| UR — "It" | Exterior-Individual | Body, behaviour, action |
| LL — "We" | Interior-Collective | Culture, shared meaning, relationships |
| LR — "Its" | Exterior-Collective | Systems, structures, environment |

No quadrant is "better" — each has gifts and shadows. The work isn't to leave your home quadrant; it's to bring the others alive alongside it.

## Q1 — Home quadrant

### Q1 Step 1 — Open

> *Which quadrant do you live in?*
>
> *Integral AQAL describes four ways of looking at anything: your inner world (I), your body and actions (It), the culture and relationships you're inside (We), and the systems and environments that hold you (Its). Most of us live in one of these four, are familiar with one or two more, and have a fourth we tend to ignore.*
>
> *Eight short questions. Pick the closest fit. At the end you'll see where you live, roughly.*

### Q1 Step 2 — Present the questions

One at a time. Wait for each answer. Accept the letter only. **No commentary between questions.** Option order is shuffled and quadrant names are hidden. **The parenthetical tag is for your scoring only — never show it to the user.** Use plain English, no Wilberian jargon.

**1. When you want to understand a problem in your life, where do you look first?**

(a) Into my own thoughts, feelings, intentions. What am I telling myself? *(UL — I)*
(b) Into my body and behaviour. What am I actually doing? What does my body know? *(UR — It)*
(c) Into my relationships and culture. Who's involved? What's the shared meaning? *(LL — We)*
(d) Into the systems and structures around me. What's the environment? What rules am I inside? *(LR — Its)*

**2. When you want to change your life, you most naturally start by:**

(a) Changing my environment, my systems, my structures. Move the furniture, change jobs *(LR — Its)*
(b) Changing my mind. Meditation, therapy, inner work *(UL — I)*
(c) Doing different things. Exercise, habits, action *(UR — It)*
(d) Changing my relationships and community. Find different people *(LL — We)*

**3. The most real thing in your experience is:**

(a) The relational field. The "between" of me and you *(LL — We)*
(b) The structures that hold everything together *(LR — Its)*
(c) Inner experience. What I see and feel inside *(UL — I)*
(d) The body. Sensation, physiology, the felt sense *(UR — It)*

**4. When you make a decision that matters, you most weigh:**

(a) What it does to my body. Will I be able to live with it in my flesh? *(UR — It)*
(b) Who else is affected. What does my community or my closest people say? *(LL — We)*
(c) The data and the systems. What does the evidence say? What's the structural reality? *(LR — Its)*
(d) My gut, my felt sense, my inner knowing. What feels right? *(UL — I)*

**5. When you're stressed, you most naturally turn to:**

(a) Inner practice. Meditation, journaling, sitting with it *(UL — I)*
(b) Connection. Call someone. Be with my people *(LL — We)*
(c) Restructure. Move my space, change my routines, fix the system *(LR — Its)*
(d) Action. Move my body, exercise, do something *(UR — It)*

**6. The kind of teacher or thinker you're most drawn to:**

(a) Body and behaviour experts. Movement teachers, coaches, neuroscientists *(UR — It)*
(b) Systems thinkers. Economists, ecologists, architects of structure *(LR — Its)*
(c) Cultural and relational wisdom. Storytellers, elders, group dynamics *(LL — We)*
(d) Inner contemplatives. Mystics, psychologists, philosophers of mind *(UL — I)*

**7. The version of yourself you most want to grow into is:**

(a) Someone fully present in relationship. Loving, intimate, able to receive *(LL — We)*
(b) Someone fully self-aware. Inner clarity, depth, soul-knowing *(UL — I)*
(c) Someone fully embodied. At home in the body. Strong, capable, alive *(UR — It)*
(d) Someone fully resourced. Living inside structures that serve me and others *(LR — Its)*

**8. What you most want others to "get" about you:**

(a) The systems I've shaped or the environments I tend *(LR — Its)*
(b) What I do, what I've built *(UR — It)*
(c) My inner life. The depth I carry *(UL — I)*
(d) The world I belong to. My people, my culture, my place *(LL — We)*

### Q1 Step 3 — Score inline

Tally **quadrant content tags** (not letters) across Q1-Q8. The dominant quadrant is the user's home quadrant. **Tie handling:** if two quadrants tie, present both descriptions on the result and let the user pick which fits best.

### Q1 Step 4 — Render the result

**ASCII 2×2 grid first**, home quadrant lit (●), soft halo (◌) on the two adjacent quadrants (UL is adjacent to UR on the individual axis and to LL on the interior axis; likewise for the others):

```
            INDIVIDUAL
        ┌──────────┬──────────┐
interior│  UL "I"  │ UR "It"  │ exterior
        │    ●     │    ◌     │
        ├──────────┼──────────┤
interior│  LL "We" │ LR "Its" │ exterior
        │    ◌     │    ○     │
        └──────────┴──────────┘
            COLLECTIVE

   ●  home quadrant   ◌  adjacent   ○  in shadow
```

Then the quadrant name and a 2-3 sentence reading grounded in the user's actual answers — that quadrant's worldview, gift, and typical shadow. Confidence: **rough**.

Example microcopy:
> *You live in the **Upper Left**. You instinctively look inward first. Your gift is depth and self-knowing. Your shadow is forgetting that the body, the relationships, and the systems around you are also real.*

End with CTA toward Q2:
> *This finds your home quadrant only. Q2 reveals your full profile across all four, including the one you tend to avoid. Confidence: rough.*

### Q1 Step 5 — Write the Q1 reading

Prepend to `context-library/maps/integral-aqal.md`:

```markdown
## Q1 Reading — YYYY-MM-DD

**Confidence:** rough
**Home quadrant:** [UL "I" / UR "It" / LL "We" / LR "Its"]

[The 2-3 sentence reading]

---
```

If the file does not exist, create it with this section and a note that the full map is generated by `/maps integral-aqal`.

### Q1 Step 6 — Offer to refresh the map

If a fuller Integral AQAL map already exists, offer to refresh it, merging Q1 with prior readings + harvested notes. If no map exists yet, skip silently.

## Tone reminders

- Wise, sincere, grounded. Witness before wisdom.
- No em dashes. No "unlock," "transform," "breakthrough."
- Numina lexicon: ground, witness, threshold, pattern, centre, terrain.
- AQAL is a map, not the territory. Hold it lightly. No quadrant is better — each has gifts and shadows.
- Plain English. "Inner life" not "interior individual," "the systems you're inside" not "exterior collective." Avoid holon/tetra-mesh jargon.
