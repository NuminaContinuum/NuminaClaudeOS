# /integral-aqal-questionnaire — The Integral AQAL map questionnaire

One skill for the Integral AQAL assessment, run in stages:

- **Q1 — Home quadrant** (3-5 min). Finds the perspective the user most naturally inhabits. Confidence after Q1: **rough**.
- **Q2 — Full quadrant profile + lines scan** (8-10 min): a state per quadrant plus a 6-line intelligence profile. Confidence after Q2: **deeper**.
- **Q3 — Long reading** (20-30 min): a settled quadrant + lines profile with open-ended depth, plus a light states intro. Confidence after Q3: **settled**.

Q1 finds the home quadrant only. Lines, states, and altitude are deferred to Q2/Q3. The map is generated by `/maps integral-aqal`. This skill feeds it structured readings and offers to refresh it afterward.

## The 4 quadrants

| Quadrant | Lens | What lives here |
|---|---|---|
| UL — "I" | Interior-Individual | Subjective experience, thoughts, feelings, intention |
| UR — "It" | Exterior-Individual | Body, behaviour, action |
| LL — "We" | Interior-Collective | Culture, shared meaning, relationships |
| LR — "Its" | Exterior-Collective | Systems, structures, environment |

No quadrant is "better" — each has gifts and shadows. The work isn't to leave your home quadrant; it's to bring the others alive alongside it.

---

## Step 0 — Figure out where the user is

Read `context-library/maps/integral-aqal.md` if it exists and check which stages have run (`## Q1 Reading`, `## Q2 Reading`). Then route:

- **Nothing done** → run Q1.
- **Q1 done, Q2 not** → offer Q2: *"Q1 found your home quadrant. Ready to go deeper? The next reflection takes about ten minutes — it reads how all four quadrants are doing, including the one you tend to avoid, plus your six lines of intelligence (mind, body, heart, relating, ethics, soul)."* If they decline, stop gracefully.
- **Q1 and Q2 done, Q3 not** → offer Q3: *"You've found your home quadrant and the full profile. The long reading (20-30 minutes) is the most complete — mostly in your own words, settling the quadrants and lines and introducing the states of consciousness. Want to do it now, or another time?"* If they decline, stop gracefully.
- **All three done** → offer to re-run a stage or refresh the map from everything since.

Q3 is standalone-capable — its Section 2/3 scans provide the baseline if Q1/Q2 weren't taken.

Honour an explicit request.

## Q1 — Home quadrant

### Q1 Step 1 — Open

> *Which quadrant do you live in?*
>
> *Integral AQAL describes four ways of looking at anything: your inner world (I), your body and actions (It), the culture and relationships you're inside (We), and the systems and environments that hold you (Its). Most of us live in one of these four, are familiar with one or two more, and have a fourth we tend to ignore.*
>
> *Eight short questions. Pick the closest fit. At the end you'll see where you live, roughly.*

### Q1 Step 2 — Present the questions

One at a time. Wait for each answer. Accept the letter only. **No commentary between questions.** Option order is shuffled and quadrant names are hidden. **The parenthetical tag is for your scoring only — never show it to the user.** Use plain English, no Wilberian jargon.

**1. When you want to understand a problem in your life, where do you look first?**

(a) Into my own thoughts, feelings, intentions. What am I telling myself? *(UL — I)*
(b) Into my body and behaviour. What am I actually doing? What does my body know? *(UR — It)*
(c) Into my relationships and culture. Who's involved? What's the shared meaning? *(LL — We)*
(d) Into the systems and structures around me. What's the environment? What rules am I inside? *(LR — Its)*

**2. When you want to change your life, you most naturally start by:**

(a) Changing my environment, my systems, my structures. Move the furniture, change jobs *(LR — Its)*
(b) Changing my mind. Meditation, therapy, inner work *(UL — I)*
(c) Doing different things. Exercise, habits, action *(UR — It)*
(d) Changing my relationships and community. Find different people *(LL — We)*

**3. The most real thing in your experience is:**

(a) The relational field. The "between" of me and you *(LL — We)*
(b) The structures that hold everything together *(LR — Its)*
(c) Inner experience. What I see and feel inside *(UL — I)*
(d) The body. Sensation, physiology, the felt sense *(UR — It)*

**4. When you make a decision that matters, you most weigh:**

(a) What it does to my body. Will I be able to live with it in my flesh? *(UR — It)*
(b) Who else is affected. What does my community or my closest people say? *(LL — We)*
(c) The data and the systems. What does the evidence say? What's the structural reality? *(LR — Its)*
(d) My gut, my felt sense, my inner knowing. What feels right? *(UL — I)*

**5. When you're stressed, you most naturally turn to:**

(a) Inner practice. Meditation, journaling, sitting with it *(UL — I)*
(b) Connection. Call someone. Be with my people *(LL — We)*
(c) Restructure. Move my space, change my routines, fix the system *(LR — Its)*
(d) Action. Move my body, exercise, do something *(UR — It)*

**6. The kind of teacher or thinker you're most drawn to:**

(a) Body and behaviour experts. Movement teachers, coaches, neuroscientists *(UR — It)*
(b) Systems thinkers. Economists, ecologists, architects of structure *(LR — Its)*
(c) Cultural and relational wisdom. Storytellers, elders, group dynamics *(LL — We)*
(d) Inner contemplatives. Mystics, psychologists, philosophers of mind *(UL — I)*

**7. The version of yourself you most want to grow into is:**

(a) Someone fully present in relationship. Loving, intimate, able to receive *(LL — We)*
(b) Someone fully self-aware. Inner clarity, depth, soul-knowing *(UL — I)*
(c) Someone fully embodied. At home in the body. Strong, capable, alive *(UR — It)*
(d) Someone fully resourced. Living inside structures that serve me and others *(LR — Its)*

**8. What you most want others to "get" about you:**

(a) The systems I've shaped or the environments I tend *(LR — Its)*
(b) What I do, what I've built *(UR — It)*
(c) My inner life. The depth I carry *(UL — I)*
(d) The world I belong to. My people, my culture, my place *(LL — We)*

### Q1 Step 3 — Score inline

Tally **quadrant content tags** (not letters) across Q1-Q8. The dominant quadrant is the user's home quadrant. **Tie handling:** if two quadrants tie, present both descriptions on the result and let the user pick which fits best.

### Q1 Step 4 — Render the result

**ASCII 2×2 grid first**, home quadrant lit (●), soft halo (◌) on the two adjacent quadrants (UL is adjacent to UR on the individual axis and to LL on the interior axis; likewise for the others):

```
            INDIVIDUAL
        ┌──────────┬──────────┐
interior│  UL "I"  │ UR "It"  │ exterior
        │    ●     │    ◌     │
        ├──────────┼──────────┤
interior│  LL "We" │ LR "Its" │ exterior
        │    ◌     │    ○     │
        └──────────┴──────────┘
            COLLECTIVE

   ●  home quadrant   ◌  adjacent   ○  in shadow
```

Then the quadrant name and a 2-3 sentence reading grounded in the user's actual answers — that quadrant's worldview, gift, and typical shadow. Confidence: **rough**.

Example microcopy:
> *You live in the **Upper Left**. You instinctively look inward first. Your gift is depth and self-knowing. Your shadow is forgetting that the body, the relationships, and the systems around you are also real.*

End with CTA toward Q2:
> *This finds your home quadrant only. Q2 reveals your full profile across all four, including the one you tend to avoid. Confidence: rough.*

### Q1 Step 5 — Write the Q1 reading

Prepend to `context-library/maps/integral-aqal.md`:

```markdown
## Q1 Reading — YYYY-MM-DD

**Confidence:** rough
**Home quadrant:** [UL "I" / UR "It" / LL "We" / LR "Its"]

[The 2-3 sentence reading]

---
```

If the file does not exist, create it with this section and a note that the full map is generated by `/maps integral-aqal`.

### Q1 Step 6 — Offer to refresh the map

If a fuller Integral AQAL map already exists, offer to refresh it, merging Q1 with prior readings + harvested notes. If no map exists yet, skip silently.

## Q2 — Full quadrant profile + lines scan (8-10 min)

**Gate:** Q2 needs Q1. If `integral-aqal.md` has no `## Q1 Reading`, run Q1 first. If Q1 exists, open by surfacing it: *"Q1 placed you in the [quadrant]. Let's see how the other three are doing, and how your different intelligences compare."*

Q2 resolves a **state for each of the 4 quadrants** and adds a **lines scan** across 6 developmental intelligences. 14 questions in three sections. One at a time, no commentary between. **State labels in parentheses are for your scoring only — never show them to the user.** Option order is shuffled. Plain English, no jargon.

### Q2 Section 1 — Quadrant state scan (4 questions, one per quadrant)

**1. The Upper Left — your inner life. Thoughts, feelings, inner experience:**
(a) Where I live. My inner world is my home *(dominant)*
(b) Active. I check in with myself regularly *(active)*
(c) Underdeveloped. I don't really know what's going on in there *(underdeveloped)*
(d) I avoid it. Going inside feels indulgent, dangerous, or just irrelevant *(shadow)*

**2. The Upper Right — your body, behaviour, observable action:**
(a) Active. I take care of my body and pay attention to what I do *(active)*
(b) I avoid it. The body and the doing feel disconnected from what matters *(shadow)*
(c) Where I live. My body and my actions are how I know I'm alive *(dominant)*
(d) Underdeveloped. I forget I have a body *(underdeveloped)*

**3. The Lower Left — your relationships, culture, shared meaning:**
(a) Underdeveloped. I don't have a strong "we" *(underdeveloped)*
(b) Where I live. My people and my culture are where I'm most myself *(dominant)*
(c) I avoid it. Other people are mostly noise or obstacle *(shadow)*
(d) Active. I tend my relationships and pay attention to the field *(active)*

**4. The Lower Right — the systems, structures, environments you're inside:**
(a) I avoid it. Systems and structures bore me or feel inhuman *(shadow)*
(b) Underdeveloped. I don't really see them, even though I'm inside them *(underdeveloped)*
(c) Active. I notice the systems I'm in and shape them when I can *(active)*
(d) Where I live. Structure is how I make sense of things *(dominant)*

### Q2 Section 2 — Lines scan (6 questions, one per developmental line)

**5. Cognitive line — thinking, perspective-taking, complexity:**
(a) Strong. I take multiple perspectives, hold complexity, think clearly *(strong)*
(b) Active. I think well enough for what I need *(active)*
(c) Underdeveloped. Careful thinking isn't my strength *(underdeveloped)*
(d) Avoided. I distrust thinking. I lean on other ways of knowing *(shadow)*

**6. Emotional line — feelings, affect, emotional intelligence:**
(a) Active. I can name and feel what's moving in me *(active)*
(b) Avoided. Feelings overwhelm me or feel false. I steer around them *(shadow)*
(c) Strong. I'm at home in emotional terrain, mine and others' *(strong)*
(d) Underdeveloped. I struggle to know what I feel *(underdeveloped)*

**7. Somatic line — body awareness, movement, the felt sense:**
(a) Underdeveloped. I live in my head; my body is dim to me *(underdeveloped)*
(b) Strong. I read my body well. Movement is one of my languages *(strong)*
(c) Avoided. The body feels dangerous, alien, or shameful *(shadow)*
(d) Active. I take care of my body and listen to it *(active)*

**8. Interpersonal line — intimacy, relating, the capacity to meet others:**
(a) Avoided. Real intimacy is too much for me. I keep distance *(shadow)*
(b) Underdeveloped. I struggle to read or meet other people *(underdeveloped)*
(c) Active. I can meet people, hold the relational field *(active)*
(d) Strong. Deep relating is one of my gifts *(strong)*

**9. Moral line — discernment, integrity, values in action:**
(a) Underdeveloped. I don't think much about right and wrong *(underdeveloped)*
(b) Strong. I take ethics seriously. I live by what I believe *(strong)*
(c) Avoided. "Right and wrong" feels naive or oppressive to me *(shadow)*
(d) Active. I act with reasonable integrity most of the time *(active)*

**10. Spiritual line — ultimate concern, transcendence, contact with the sacred:**
(a) Active. I have a practice, a relationship with something larger *(active)*
(b) Underdeveloped. I'm new to this terrain *(underdeveloped)*
(c) Strong. The sacred is a daily reality for me *(strong)*
(d) Avoided. Spirituality feels like delusion or bypass to me *(shadow)*

### Q2 Section 3 — Cross-cutting Likert (4 items, 1-5)

Ask each as "1 = not at all true, 5 = very true."

**11.** *I move easily between my inner world and my outer life.* (low → UL/UR or UL/LR split)
**12.** *I move easily between my individual concerns and the collective I'm part of.* (low → upper/lower split)
**13.** *There's a quadrant I actively avoid or dismiss.* (high → confirms shadow quadrant)
**14.** *My different intelligences (mind, body, heart, relating, ethics, soul) feel roughly balanced.* (low → confirms uneven lines)

### Q2 Step 4 — Resolve and render

- Section 1 → a state per quadrant (dominant / active / underdeveloped / shadow). Typical: 1 dominant, 1-2 active, 0-1 underdeveloped, 0-1 shadow.
- Section 2 → a state per line (strong / active / underdeveloped / shadow). Typical: 1-2 strong, 2-3 active, 1-2 underdeveloped, 0-1 shadow.
- Section 3 Likert confirms shape (HIGH if confirmed, else MEDIUM).
- Render the ASCII 2×2 grid with each quadrant styled by state, plus a 6-line bar profile, e.g.:

```
            INDIVIDUAL
        ┌──────────┬──────────┐
        │  UL "I"  │ UR "It"  │
        │ ● domin. │ · under. │
        ├──────────┼──────────┤
        │ LL "We"  │ LR "Its" │
        │ ◌ active │ × shadow │
        └──────────┴──────────┘
            COLLECTIVE

   Lines:
   Cognitive     ████████  strong
   Emotional     ██████    active
   Somatic       ██        underdeveloped
   Interpersonal ██████    active
   Moral         ██████    active
   Spiritual     ████████  strong

   ●  dominant   ◌  active   ·  underdeveloped   ×  shadow
```

- Give a 1-2 sentence reading per active quadrant and per notable line (strongest, most underdeveloped, any shadow), then a summary line naming home quadrant, shadow quadrant, strongest line, most underdeveloped line, and the present work. Plain English. Confidence label: **deeper**.

### Q2 Step 5 — Write the Q2 reading

Prepend a `## Q2 Reading — YYYY-MM-DD` section (above the Q1 reading) to `integral-aqal.md` with: **Confidence: deeper**, the state per quadrant, the state per line, and the summary line.

### Q2 Step 6 — Offer to refresh the map

If a fuller Integral AQAL map exists, offer to refresh it, merging Q2 with Q1 and harvested notes; the merged map should raise the confidence in its header.

## Q3 — Long reading (20-30 min)

The longest read: a settled profile across quadrants and lines, plus a light introduction to states of consciousness. 23 questions across seven sections — open-ended (~40% of the signal), multiple-choice, and Likert (1-5). One at a time, no commentary between. **State tags are for your scoring only — never show them.** Shuffle multiple-choice option order. Plain English, no jargon. If Q1/Q2 exist, surface the latest reading; standalone-capable otherwise. Levels (overlap with Spiral Dynamics) and Types are out of scope.

### Q3 Section 1 — Settling in (2 open-ended)

**1.** *Take a breath. In your own words: when you look at your whole life right now — your inner world, your body, your relationships, the systems you're inside — what's most alive? What's most neglected?*
**2.** *If you had to describe how you typically meet your life — through your inner experience, through your body and your actions, through your relationships, or through the structures around you — what would you say?*

### Q3 Section 2 — Quick quadrant scan (4 multiple-choice)

Options map to *(dominant)* / *(active)* / *(underdeveloped)* / *(shadow)* — shuffle, hide tags.

**3. Inner life — thoughts, feelings, inner experience:** Where I live *(dominant)* / Active, I check in regularly *(active)* / Underdeveloped, I don't really know what's in there *(underdeveloped)* / I avoid it *(shadow)*
**4. Body and behaviour — what you do, how you act:** Active, I take care of it *(active)* / I avoid it *(shadow)* / Where I live *(dominant)* / Underdeveloped, I forget I have one *(underdeveloped)*
**5. Relationships and culture — your people, your "we":** Underdeveloped, I don't have a strong "we" *(underdeveloped)* / Where I live *(dominant)* / I avoid it *(shadow)* / Active, I tend it *(active)*
**6. Systems and structures — environments, institutions you're inside:** I avoid it *(shadow)* / Underdeveloped, I don't really see them *(underdeveloped)* / Active, I notice and shape them *(active)* / Where I live *(dominant)*

### Q3 Section 3 — Lines profile (6 MC + 1 open-ended)

Each line's options map to *(strong)* / *(active)* / *(underdeveloped)* / *(shadow)* — shuffle, hide tags.

**7. Cognitive — thinking, perspective-taking, complexity:** Strong / Active / Underdeveloped / Avoided
**8. Emotional — feelings, affect, emotional intelligence:** Active / Avoided / Strong / Underdeveloped
**9. Somatic — body awareness, movement, felt sense:** Underdeveloped / Strong / Avoided / Active
**10. Interpersonal — intimacy, relating, meeting others:** Avoided / Underdeveloped / Active / Strong
**11. Moral — ethics, integrity, values in action:** Underdeveloped / Strong / Avoided / Active
**12. Spiritual — ultimate concern, sacred contact, transcendence:** Active / Underdeveloped / Strong / Avoided
**13.** *Open-ended: Pick the line you most want to grow into. Why? What's been in the way?* (growth-edge → selects a Section 4 prompt)

### Q3 Section 4 — Adaptive deep-dive (3 open-ended)

From the running profile select 3 prompts: **1 on the dominant quadrant**, **1 on the shadow quadrant** (or most underdeveloped if no clear shadow), **1 on the growth-edge line** (Q13, or the most underdeveloped line).

**Quadrant prompts:**
- **Upper Left (I)** — *"Tell me about your inner life. What do you find when you look inside? What's been opening, what's been closing? What part of you most wants to be witnessed?"*
- **Upper Right (It)** — *"Tell me about your body and your actions. What is your body asking of you? What are you actually doing, day to day, and how does it line up with what matters to you?"*
- **Lower Left (We)** — *"Tell me about your 'we.' Your people, your culture, your closest relationships. Where is the field of belonging strong, and where is it thin or broken?"*
- **Lower Right (Its)** — *"Tell me about the systems and structures you live inside. Which serve you, which constrain you? What would you build differently?"*

**Line prompts:**
- **Cognitive** — *"Where do you think most clearly, and where does your thinking get muddy or self-deceiving?"*
- **Emotional** — *"What's the most honest thing you can say about what you're feeling right now, and what those feelings have been doing in your life?"*
- **Somatic** — *"Tell me about your body. What does it carry? What does it know that your mind doesn't?"*
- **Interpersonal** — *"Describe the people you let in fully, and the people you keep at arm's length. What is the difference, in you?"*
- **Moral** — *"What's the ethical fault line in your life right now — a place where what you do and what you believe don't quite match?"*
- **Spiritual** — *"What is your relationship to the sacred? Where do you meet it, and where do you avoid it?"*

### Q3 Section 5 — States introduction (2 MC + 1 Likert)

States are temporary modes of consciousness available to everyone, distinct from developmental stages. Introduce lightly.

**17. The state you most reliably access in your practice or daily life:** Ordinary waking, sensory, present *(Gross)* / Imaginal, dreamlike, archetypal, luminous *(Subtle)* / Spacious, formless, deep stillness, vast emptiness *(Causal)* / The boundary between me and the world dissolves *(Non-dual)* / I don't notice states distinctly *(Pre-states)*
**18. The state that feels most foreign, or that you're most curious about:** Ordinary waking *(Gross)* / Imaginal, luminous *(Subtle)* / Spacious, formless, vast *(Causal)* / Boundary-dissolving, unity *(Non-dual)*
**19. Likert (1-5):** *I've had at least one experience where the boundary between me and the world dissolved.*

### Q3 Section 6 — Integration and gaps (2 Likert + 1 open-ended)

**20. Likert (1-5):** *My inner clarity carries through into my body, my relationships, and the systems I'm in.* (high → quadrants integrated)
**21. Likert (1-5):** *I can witness my inner experience without being lost in it.* (high → witnessing capacity active)
**22.** *Open-ended: What integration is your life asking of you right now? What gap between quadrants, or between lines, do you most feel?*

### Q3 Section 7 — Closing reflection (1 open-ended)

**23.** *If your whole life — inner and outer, individual and collective — could speak to you in one sentence right now, what would it say?* — use as a direct quote at the top of the reading ("Your life said: …").

### Q3 Step 8 — Resolve and render

- Combine Section 2 (state per quadrant), Section 3 (state per line), Section 4 open-ended (deep texture → HIGH confidence on covered areas), Section 5 (accessible + foreign-curious state), Section 6 (integration challenge). Open-ended carries ~40%.
- Final state per quadrant: dominant / active / underdeveloped / shadow. Final state per line: strong / active / underdeveloped / shadow. HIGH on deepened areas.
- Render: the closing **"What your life said"** quote at the top; the ASCII 2×2 grid styled by state + a 6-line bar profile (same logic as Q2); a small **states badge** (accessible state(s) + foreign-curious state); a 2-4 sentence reading per active/shadow quadrant and per notable line (strongest, growth-edge, any shadow); **"The integration you're being asked to make"** named as a bridge (e.g. *Upper Left → Upper Right*) with 2-3 concrete practices; **"The state you're invited into"** — one line on the foreign-curious state with a practice doorway. Plain English. Confidence: **settled**.

### Q3 Step 9 — Write the Q3 reading and close

- Prepend a `## Q3 Reading — YYYY-MM-DD` section (above prior readings) to `integral-aqal.md` with: **Confidence: settled**, the state per quadrant, the state per line, the accessible/foreign states, the integration bridge, and the life quote.
- Offer to refresh the Integral AQAL map (merging Q3 with prior readings + harvested notes; raises confidence).
- Seed 2-3 reflection prompts focused on the dominant quadrant, the shadow quadrant, and the growth-edge line.

## Tone reminders

- Wise, sincere, grounded. Witness before wisdom.
- No em dashes. No "unlock," "transform," "breakthrough."
- Numina lexicon: ground, witness, threshold, pattern, centre, terrain.
- AQAL is a map, not the territory. Hold it lightly. No quadrant is better — each has gifts and shadows.
- Plain English. "Inner life" not "interior individual," "the systems you're inside" not "exterior collective." Avoid holon/tetra-mesh jargon.
