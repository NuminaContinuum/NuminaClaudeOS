# /sufi-maqamat-questionnaire — The Sufi Maqamat questionnaire

One skill for the stations-of-the-path assessment (NUM-304). It runs in stages and knows where the user is:

- **Q1 — Quick placement** (3-5 min, NUM-320). Finds the dominant band, then resolves the station with a one-question tiebreaker. Confidence after Q1: **rough**.
- **Q2 — Full station profile** (10-12 min, NUM-321). Resolves a state per band, the specific station in the live bands, the **hal-versus-maqam** check and the **ujb** check. Confidence after Q2: **deeper**.
- **Q3 — Settled reading** (20-30 min, NUM-322). A long, open-ended reading: the moment the station held and the moment it slipped, the transition the user is in, the pitfall they can name. Confidence after Q3: **settled**.

The canonical content for all 10 stations (essence, lived markers, gift, pitfall, classical line), the maqam/hal distinction, the ahwal, the ujb spine and the evidence signals live in `sufi-maqamat-framework.md`, in this skill's folder. Both this questionnaire and `/maps sufi` draw from it. Adapt the tone to where the user actually is; do not quote it verbatim.

The map itself is generated by `/maps sufi`. This skill feeds it structured readings and offers to refresh it afterward.

**Core principle — where you are, not who you are.** Carry this through every reading: a seeker deeply in Tawba is on a real station with its own gift, never a lesser soul. The stations are earned slowly, and they are not a ranking of souls.

**Maqam and hal.** Maqamat are earned, stable stations. Ahwal are states, given and passing. **A passing state is never read as an earned station**, and **Fana is never claimed from a single experience.** When the evidence points to hal, say so plainly and warmly: honour what was shown, and name that the work of making it home is still ahead.

**The 10 stations (5 bands):** Turning (Tawba, Wara') · Letting Go (Zuhd, Faqr) · Trial & Trust (Sabr, Tawakkul) · Devotion (Rida, Mahabba) · Knowing (Marifa, Fana). The path is spiral, not a ladder: noticing pride at any station is a fresh Tawba.

**Questions stay in plain experiential language.** No Arabic terms and no station names in the answer options. The Arabic names (script + transliteration + translation) appear only on the result screen, translated name primary, exactly as the other tradition maps handle their source vocabulary.

**Respect the tradition.** Sufism is the mystical heart of Islam. Honour that without imposing Islamic theology on users from other paths. Use the user's own word for what they turn toward: the Beloved, the Friend, the Real, the holy, the more-than-me.

## Step 0 — Figure out where the user is

Read `context-library/maps/sufi-maqamat.md` if it exists and check which stages have run (`## Q1 Reading`, `## Q2 Reading`). Route:

- **Nothing done yet** → run Q1.
- **Q1 done, Q2 not** → offer Q2: *"You've got the quick placement. Ready to go deeper? The next reflection (about ten minutes) looks at every band of the path, resolves the specific station, and asks whether what you're describing is ground or weather."* If they decline, stop gracefully.
- **Q1 and Q2 done, Q3 not** → offer Q3: *"You've seen the full profile. The long reading (20-30 minutes) settles it in your own words: the moment your station held, the moment it slipped, and the transition you're in. Want to do it now, or another time?"* If they decline, stop gracefully.
- **All three done** → offer to re-run a stage for a fresh read, or refresh the map from everything since.

Never push toward deeper stages. Q2 and Q3 are standalone-capable if the user asks for them directly. Honour an explicit stage request.

## Q1 — Quick placement

### Q1 Step 1 — Open

Present this intro, then begin immediately.

> *Where are you on the path of stations?*
>
> *Sufi teachers describe a sequence of maqamat (stations) through which the seeker passes on the way to knowing the Beloved. From turning back, through letting go, through trial and trust, into devotion, and finally into knowing and dissolving.*
>
> *This reads where your heart and practice are right now. Not where you hope to be, and not who you are.*
>
> *Eight short questions. Pick what feels most honest.*

### Q1 Step 2 — Present the questions

One at a time. Wait for each answer. Accept the letter only. **No commentary between questions.** Options are listed in the app's shuffled display order (a Latin square for 1-5, free shuffles for 6-8), so a letter never maps to a fixed band. Present them in this order or reshuffle; never group them by band. **The parenthetical tag is for your scoring only — never show it to the user.**

**1. Right now, your inner life feels closest to…**
(a) I am turning back — facing what I have avoided with new honesty *(Turning)*
(b) I am loosening my grip — wanting less ownership of outcomes *(Letting Go)*
(c) I am in the fire — learning to stay, then to lean on what holds me *(Trial & Trust)*
(d) My heart is softening into gratitude and a quieter love *(Devotion)*
(e) Something in me knows without needing to prove it — self feels thinner *(Knowing)*

**2. When you sit or remember, what is loudest?**
(a) The pull of release — what I cling to is costing the heart *(Letting Go)*
(b) Difficulty that asks for patience, then for trust beyond my plans *(Trial & Trust)*
(c) Warmth and yes — contentment, or love that reorders my days *(Devotion)*
(d) Clear seeing or a quiet dissolving of the one who claims the path *(Knowing)*
(e) Remorse and resolve — a wish to reorient before going further *(Turning)*

**3. Pick the sentence that feels most honest:**
(a) I am holding steady under pressure, then releasing the result *(Trial & Trust)*
(b) I meet what comes with a deeper yes — sometimes as love itself *(Devotion)*
(c) Insight is tasting, not theorizing — or the seeker's claim is softening *(Knowing)*
(d) I am cleaning the mirror — conscience awake, willing to amend *(Turning)*
(e) I am travelling lighter — the world can stay without owning me *(Letting Go)*

**4. What do you want from a map like this?**
(a) A name for settled peace and the fire of devotion *(Devotion)*
(b) Orientation near the summit — knowing and the end of spiritual ownership *(Knowing)*
(c) Language for the first turn — honesty without self-hatred *(Turning)*
(d) Permission to renounce what dulls the heart, without contempt for life *(Letting Go)*
(e) Companionship for the hard middle — endurance and reliance *(Trial & Trust)*

**5. In difficulty, your default move is…**
(a) See — or let the separate claim dissolve in what is Real *(Knowing)*
(b) Return — admit the miss, choose the cleaner path again *(Turning)*
(c) Release — stop gripping what I cannot keep *(Letting Go)*
(d) Stay — then trust a wisdom larger than my control *(Trial & Trust)*
(e) Receive — meet the moment with contentment or with love *(Devotion)*

**6. Your relationship to "spiritual attainment" language is…**
(a) I am still in the school of patience and reliance *(Trial & Trust)*
(b) I need basics first — turning and keeping the heart clean *(Turning)*
(c) The word feels almost beside the point — knowing, or no one left to claim it *(Knowing)*
(d) I care more about emptying than about titles of progress *(Letting Go)*
(e) Labels matter less than a heart that is pleased and loving *(Devotion)*

**7. Which image resonates most this month?**
(a) A polished mirror — or a flame that leaves no separate wick *(Knowing)*
(b) A garden at dusk — peace with what is, or love as the only gravity *(Devotion)*
(c) A door opening inward — facing the old pattern without flinching *(Turning)*
(d) A storm held with a steady heart, then a sail that trusts the wind *(Trial & Trust)*
(e) Open hands — possessions and pride set down beside the path *(Letting Go)*

**8. If you had to name your dominant climate…**
(a) Releasing — renouncing grip, travelling empty-handed *(Letting Go)*
(b) Turning — reorienting, conscience careful *(Turning)*
(c) Devoted — content, or lit by love *(Devotion)*
(d) Knowing — tasted insight, or the soft end of the separate claim *(Knowing)*
(e) Enduring and trusting — trial as teacher, reliance as rest *(Trial & Trust)*

### Q1 Step 3 — Score inline, then the tiebreaker

Tally **band content tags** (not letter positions) across Q1-Q8. Every band holds two stations, so a tiebreaker always follows.

- **One dominant band** → ask that band's tiebreaker (2 options, shuffled, station tags hidden).
- **Tie** → take the **first two tied bands in path order** (Turning → Letting Go → Trial & Trust → Devotion → Knowing) and ask one combined tiebreaker with all four options, shuffled together: *"[Band A] and [Band B] tied. Which nuance fits better?"* The option picked sets both the band and the station.

**Tiebreakers** (one at a time, accept the letter only):

**Turning** — *Within this turning season, which fits closer?*
- A deep reorientation — facing what I avoided and choosing again *(Tawba)*
- A careful conscience — preferring the cleaner path when doubt arises *(Wara')*

**Letting Go** — *Within letting go, which fits closer?*
- Loosening attachment — the world in the hand, not in the heart *(Zuhd)*
- Empty-handed dependence — no claim of self-sufficiency left *(Faqr)*

**Trial & Trust** — *Within trial and trust, which fits closer?*
- Holding steady through hardship without collapsing or fleeing *(Sabr)*
- Acting fully, then releasing the result into a larger care *(Tawakkul)*

**Devotion** — *Within devotion, which fits closer?*
- A settled yes to what is — peace without passivity *(Rida)*
- Love as gravity — warmth that reorders what I reach for *(Mahabba)*

**Knowing** — *Within knowing, which fits closer?*
- Insight tasted through nearness — clear seeing with humility *(Marifa)*
- The separate claim softening — life continues without a hero of the path *(Fana)*

If the user won't choose, default to the band's first station (Tawba, Zuhd, Sabr, Rida, Marifa). Never default to Fana.

### Q1 Step 4 — Render the result

**ASCII rising path first**, ten gates bottom to top, current station lit, a soft halo (◌) on the other station in the same band and on the first station of the next band. Adapt to the actual result:

```
   Knowing         ·  Annihilation    Fana      الفناء
                   ·  Gnosis          Marifa    المعرفة
   Devotion        ·  Love            Mahabba   المحبة
                   ◌  Contentment     Rida      الرضا
   Trial & Trust   ◌  Reliance        Tawakkul  التوكّل
                   ●  Patience        Sabr      الصبر     ← you
   Letting Go      ·  Spiritual Pov.  Faqr      الفقر
                   ·  Renunciation    Zuhd      الزهد
   Turning         ·  Scrupulousness  Wara'     الورع
                   ·  Turning         Tawba     التوبة

   ●  current    ◌  nearby    the path is spiral, not a ladder
```

Then the station name (translated, primary) with Arabic script and transliteration (secondary), and a 2-3 sentence reading grounded in the user's actual answers: the lived experience, the gift, the traditional pitfall. Held as hypothesis, with weight rather than flourish. Draw on `sufi-maqamat-framework.md` but adapt to what they said.

Example microcopy (Sabr):
> *You're at the station of **Patience** (الصبر, Sabr). You're in trial, and you're not bitter about it. The gift here is the depth that's being forged. The pitfall, traditional teachers warn, is using patience to harden into stoicism. Sabr is open patience, not gritted teeth.*

Example microcopy (Fana):
> *You're at the station of **Annihilation** (الفناء, Fana), the thinning of the "I" into something larger. The gift is freedom from the small self's grip. The pitfall, Sufi teachers have warned for centuries, is that authentic Fana doesn't leave a Fana-ist behind to claim it. If part of you is enjoying having arrived here, the work isn't yet done.*

**For Marifa or Fana**, always add: *"Eight questions can't tell a station from a state. A moment of dissolving, however real, is a hal, given and passing. The station is lived over years. Q2 asks about exactly this."*

End with the confidence label and a CTA:

> *This places your current station only, roughly. Q2 shows which stations are familiar, which are emerging, which you may be pushing against, and whether what you're touching is ground or weather. Confidence: rough.*

If yes, run the refresh (same logic as `/maps sufi`), merging the Q1 reading with existing harvested notes and prior readings. If no map exists yet, skip silently.

## Q2 — Full station profile + hal/maqam + ujb (10-12 min)

**Gate:** standalone-capable. If `sufi-maqamat.md` has a `## Q1 Reading`, open by surfacing it: *"Your first reading placed you at [station]. This one looks at every band of the path, resolves the specific station, and asks whether what you are describing is ground or weather."* Otherwise open with:

> *A deeper read of the stations of the path. Ten to twelve minutes, up to fifteen questions. We'll look at each band, resolve the station where you stand now, and see what's still moving in you, what's emerging, and what you push against.*

Q2 resolves a **state per band** — Current / Familiar / Emerging / Dormant / Resisted. One at a time, no commentary between. **State, band and station tags are for your scoring only — never show them.** Shuffle option order.

### Q2 Section 1 — Band scan (5 questions, one per band)

Ask each as *"For each, pick the closest to your experience:"* followed by the prompt. Shuffle, hide tags.

**1. The awakening of conscience, becoming careful, turning back from what no longer fits:** *(Turning)*
Familiar. I know this work. I've done it *(Familiar)* / Pulling at me. I sense something is asking me to wake up and turn *(Emerging)* / Currently where I am. I'm in the work of turning *(Current)* / Not in play right now *(Dormant)* / I push against it. The "should I be careful" voice feels prudish to me *(Resisted)*

**2. Renunciation of attachments, the gift of spiritual poverty:** *(Letting Go)*
Currently where I am. I'm in the shedding *(Current)* / Familiar. The work of letting go is something I know *(Familiar)* / Pulling at me. Something wants to be released *(Emerging)* / Not in play *(Dormant)* / I push against it. Renunciation feels like deprivation to me *(Resisted)*

**3. Patient endurance and surrender of outcomes:** *(Trial & Trust)*
Pulling at me. I sense a trial coming, or already here *(Emerging)* / Currently where I am. Patience and trust are the daily work *(Current)* / Familiar. I've leaned on these stations before *(Familiar)* / Not in play *(Dormant)* / I push against it. Surrender feels like giving up *(Resisted)*

**4. Contentment with what is, love of the Beloved:** *(Devotion)*
Familiar. The heart has opened before *(Familiar)* / Pulling at me. The heart is starting to soften *(Emerging)* / Currently where I am. Devotion is the texture *(Current)* / Not in play yet *(Dormant)* / I push against it. Devotional language feels saccharine or naive to me *(Resisted)*

**5. Direct gnosis and the thinning of the self:** *(Knowing)*
Not in play. The "I" is firmly in place; no thinning *(Dormant)* / I push against it. Annihilation or non-self language feels like dissociation to me *(Resisted)* / Familiar. I've touched this. Briefly *(Familiar)* / Pulling at me. The "I" is starting to thin *(Emerging)* / Currently where I am. The Beloved and the self are barely two *(Current)*

### Q2 Section 2 — Station resolution (adaptive, 0-3 questions)

Ask one resolution question for each band scored **Current** or **Emerging** in Section 1, in path order, **up to three**. Two options each, shuffled, station tags hidden.

- **Turning** — *Within the turning, which is closer?* Becoming careful about what I take in. Conscience-led caution about food, media, company, action *(Wara')* / Catching myself in old patterns and turning back. The act of return itself is the work *(Tawba)*
- **Letting Go** — *Within the letting go, which is closer?* Releasing attachments. Worldly comforts hold less grip. Things lose their hooks *(Zuhd)* / Empty-handed. I feel the gift of having little, not just the discipline of renouncing *(Faqr)*
- **Trial & Trust** — *Within the trial, which is closer?* Patient endurance. Showing up day after day through what's hard, without bitterness *(Sabr)* / Surrendering the outcome. Trust deepening as I let the Beloved hold what I can't *(Tawakkul)*
- **Devotion** — *Within the devotion, which is closer?* Love. The heart is the centre. Devotion, longing, even tears *(Mahabba)* / Contentment. What is, is good. I want what I have. The wanting itself has softened *(Rida)*
- **Knowing** — *Within the knowing, which is closer?* Direct knowing. A way of seeing that doesn't need thought. The Beloved is known directly *(Marifa)* / Annihilation. The "I" has thinned. Sometimes it dissolves entirely *(Fana)*

### Q2 Section 3 — Context probes (4 questions)

Probes 1, 2 and 4 each add a +1 nudge to a band. Probe 3 is the stability check and carries no nudge. *(Shuffle order.)*

**The texture of your heart right now:** Sharp, alert, sometimes uneasy *(Turning)* / Lightening, freer, less occupied *(Letting Go)* / Tested, weathered, holding *(Trial & Trust)* / Warm, soft, sometimes overflowing *(Devotion)* / Permeable, vast, almost transparent *(Knowing)*

**The rhythm of your practice:** Newly serious. I've started, or restarted, with intent *(Turning)* / Spare. Less effort, less attachment to the practice itself *(Letting Go)* / Endurance. Daily, through dry times and through grief *(Trial & Trust)* / Heart-led. I'm drawn rather than disciplined *(Devotion)* / There isn't really a practice anymore. It's how I am *(Knowing)*

**How stable is what you are reporting?** *(the hal-versus-maqam check)* Stable. This is where I live, not just where I've visited. I can return to it day after day *(Maqam)* / Recent. I've been in this for some weeks or months. Not sure yet if it's home *(Possibly maqam)* / Visiting. It's been arising lately, but it doesn't feel like where I live *(Hal)* / Mixed. Some of it is stable, some of it comes and goes *(Mixed)*

**What you most need to watch for at your current station:** Excessive self-scrutiny becoming a new ego project *(Turning)* / Renunciation becoming spiritual showing-off *(Letting Go)* / Patience hardening into stoicism, surrender becoming passivity *(Trial & Trust)* / Devotion becoming sentimentality, love becoming need *(Devotion)* / Secret pride at having attained higher stations *(Knowing)*

### Q2 Section 4 — Cross-cutting Likert (3 items, 1-5)

Ask: *"From 1 (not at all) to 5 (completely):"*

- *The station I am reporting feels stable. I can return to it daily, through dry times and bright ones.* (stability; 1-2 → hal-not-maqam)
- *I sometimes catch myself secretly pleased about where I've reached on the path.* (ujb; 4-5 → ujb flag when Devotion or Knowing is Current)
- *The Beloved goes by many names. My path is one of many real paths.* (pluralism; 1-2 → pluralism note)

### Q2 Step 4 — Resolve and render

- **States:** Section 1 gives one state per band (unanswered = Dormant).
- **Exactly one Current band:** if none was claimed, pick from the Familiar bands (or all bands if none are Familiar) the one with the most probe nudges, ties to the earlier band, and say plainly that the placement was inferred. If several were claimed, keep the one with the most nudges (ties to the earlier band) and move the others to Familiar.
- **Station:** the Section 2 answer for the Current band. If the Current band was inferred and so never resolved, use the band's second resolution option (Tawba, Faqr, Tawakkul, Rida), **except Knowing, which falls back to Marifa**. Fana is only placed when the user chose it explicitly.
- **Hal-not-maqam:** true if stability = *Visiting* OR the stability Likert is 1-2. It lowers confidence to MEDIUM on every band (the whole profile is describing weather rather than ground); it does not move the placement. Name the Current station as **"touched as hal"**.
- **Ujb flag:** Current band is Devotion or Knowing AND the ujb Likert is 4-5.
- **Pluralism note:** pluralism Likert 1-2.
- **Confidence per band:** HIGH where a probe nudge corroborates the band, MEDIUM otherwise; all MEDIUM when hal-not-maqam.
- **Render** the rising path with all ten gates styled by band state: ● Current (with halo) / ◉ Familiar / ◦ Emerging (at the band's leading edge) / · Dormant / ⊘ Resisted. The Current station is lit; its partner in the band is not. Then:
  - 1-2 sentences per Current and Familiar band (translated name primary, Arabic secondary), naming the specific station for the Current band.
  - **Maqam/hal line:** maqam → *"You're at the station of [Mahabba] (Love), held as maqam. Earned, stable, returnable. The heart is your daily home."* Hal → *"You're touching the station of [Mahabba] (Love), but as hal: a given state, not yet a stable maqam. Honour what's been shown. The work of making it yours is still ahead."* Possibly maqam or mixed → say it is still settling.
  - **Notes, if triggered** (use this wording, adapted lightly):
    - Hal: *"What you describe reads as a hal rather than a maqam: a state that has been visiting rather than a station you live in. The tradition treats that distinction as important and not as a lesser result. States are given; stations are earned, and they are usually earned slowly, in the one below the one being reported."*
    - Ujb: *"You placed yourself in the later stations and also recognised the quiet pleasure of having got there. That recognition has a name in this tradition, ujb, and noticing it is itself part of the station rather than a failure of it. It is worth watching without turning the watching into another accomplishment."*
    - Pluralism: *"This map is one language for the path among several. If it starts to feel like the only true one, that is usually a signal to read your experience against another map for a while."*
  - **Summary line:** *"You are standing at [station]. [Familiar bands] is/are familiar ground. [Emerging band] is beginning to ask for you. You push against [Resisted band]. [If hal: Read this as weather rather than ground for now.] The stations are earned slowly, and they are not a ranking of souls."*
  - Confidence: **deeper**. Restate "where you are, not who you are."

### Q2 Step 5 — Write the Q2 reading and offer refresh

Prepend a `## Q2 Reading — YYYY-MM-DD` section (above the Q1 reading) to `sufi-maqamat.md` with: **Confidence: deeper**, the state per band, the Current station and whether it is held as maqam or touched as hal, any ujb / pluralism flag, whether the Current band was inferred, and the summary line. Then offer to refresh the Sufi Maqamat map (merging Q2 with Q1 + harvested notes; raises the confidence header).

## Q3 — Long reading (20-30 min)

**Gate:** aware of Q1/Q2 (surface the latest placement if present: *"Your earlier reading placed you at [station]. This is the long one: some questions are yours to answer in your own words, and you can stop and come back."*); standalone-capable otherwise (Section 2 is the baseline). Otherwise open with:

> *The long reading. Twenty to thirty minutes, twenty-three questions. Some you'll answer in your own words, some are choices. This is the fullest read of where you stand on the path of stations: which ones move in you still, what's emerging, what you push against, and whether what you're touching is held as a stable station or as a passing state. You can pause and come back. There are no right answers, only honest ones.*

23 questions: 10 open-ended, 13 structured. One at a time, no commentary between. **Tags are for your scoring only — never show them.** Shuffle option order (except the transition list, which reads best in path order). Voice input welcome on the open-ended questions; the user can pause and resume.

### Q3 Section 1 — Settling in (2 open-ended)

**1.** *Take a breath. In your own words: what is your inner life asking of you right now, and what is the texture of your relationship to the Beloved, or to whatever you call the more-than-you?* (2-5 min)
**2.** *If you had to describe where you stand on the path of stations in plain language, without using technical terms, what would you say?* (2-3 min)

> *Scoring:* band themes from language (turning/conscience, letting go/simplicity, trial/patience/trust, devotion/love/contentment, knowing/thinning/dissolution). Note any signal of pride in one's place on the path for the ujb cross-check.

### Q3 Section 2 — Quick band scan (5 multiple-choice)

Plain states, shuffled, tags hidden. Doubles as the baseline and the selector for Section 4.

**3. Awakening conscience, becoming careful, returning:** *(Turning)* Familiar / Pulling at me / Currently where I am / Not in play / I push against it
**4. Renunciation, simplicity, spiritual poverty:** *(Letting Go)* Currently where I am / Familiar / Pulling at me / Not in play / I push against it
**5. Patient endurance, surrender of outcomes:** *(Trial & Trust)* Pulling at me / Currently where I am / Familiar / Not in play / I push against it
**6. Contentment with what is, love of the Beloved:** *(Devotion)* Familiar / Pulling at me / Currently where I am / Not in play yet / I push against it
**7. Direct gnosis, thinning of the self, annihilation:** *(Knowing)* Not in play / I push against it / Familiar / Pulling at me / Currently where I am

*(Familiar = Familiar, Pulling at me = Emerging, Currently where I am = Current, Not in play = Dormant, I push against it = Resisted.)*

### Q3 Section 3 — Hal vs maqam, and the lived moment (1 MC + 2 open-ended)

**8. The station you are reporting: how does it actually live in you?** Stable. It's where I live. I can return to it day after day, through dry times and bright ones *(Maqam)* / Recent and consistent. I've been in this for months, but the longer rhythm hasn't been tested *(Possibly maqam)* / Visiting. It's arising lately, but it doesn't feel like home *(Hal)* / Mixed. Stable in some domains, fleeting in others *(Mixed)*
**9.** *Describe a recent moment when you were most fully at the station you would name as your current one. What was happening? What was alive in you? What were you doing, or not doing?* (2-3 min)
**10.** *Describe a recent moment when the station seemed to slip, when you fell out of it, or noticed it was not there. What was that like?* (2-3 min) — tell the user: *"This one and the last are the pair that tells a passing state from an earned station."*

> *Scoring:* the stable moment + slip moment pair is the most diagnostic in the reading. Frequent, vivid slips point to hal; consistent return points to maqam. Note phrases to weave into the result.

If the user paused around here, on return: *"You're past the halfway mark. There's no rush. The path itself isn't fast."*

### Q3 Section 4 — Adaptive deep-dive (3 open-ended)

From the Section 2 scan select 3 bands: **the Current band**, **the first Resisted band (or the first Familiar if none)**, **the first Emerging band (or the first Familiar)**. Drop duplicates and fill from path order (Turning first) until there are three.

- **Turning** — *"Tell me about what you've been turning away from. The old patterns, the old self, the things you no longer want to carry. What does the turning feel like in the body, in the day? Where does conscience flare? Where do you still betray what you know?"*
- **Letting Go** — *"Describe what's falling away. Things, identity, attachments, status. What is the texture of the shedding: relief, grief, fear, freedom, some mix? Where does the renunciation become a new ego project?"*
- **Trial & Trust** — *"Tell me about the trial you're in, or the one you've just come through. Where does patience hold and where does it crack into stoicism? Where does surrender feel like trust, and where does it feel like resignation? What is the Beloved doing in this, if anything?"*
- **Devotion** — *"Describe the heart opening. The love, the contentment, the longing. Where does it flow on its own and where do you reach for it? How is it different from sentimentality or romantic projection? When the Beloved feels close, what is that closeness made of?"*

**11, 12, 13.** The three selected prompts. Each 2-4 min, voice or text.

> *Scoring:* deep texture per band → HIGH confidence on deepened bands, plus 2-3 quotable phrases. Re-read ujb risk in the texture, especially on Devotion and Knowing.

### Q3 Step 10 — Resolve and render

- **Hal-not-maqam:** stability = *Visiting*, OR stability = *Mixed* AND the slip moment (Q10) is a substantive description. Label the Current station **"touched as hal"**; otherwise **"held as maqam"** (or "still settling" for *Recent and consistent*).
- **Ujb:** Current band is Devotion or Knowing AND Q16 is 4-5.
- **Over-identified:** Q18 is 1-2 OR Q23 is 1-2.
- **Transition:** Q14 (default: none).
- **Render**, in this order:
  - **"What the Beloved said"** — the Q20 answer as a direct quote at the top (trim to about two sentences).
  - The rising path with all ten gates styled by band state (as Q2), the Current station lit.

    - → Tawba: *Name one thing you keep returning to that you no longer want to carry. Turning starts as noticing, not as reform.*
    - → Zuhd: *Give one thing away that you would rather keep, without telling anyone you did.*
    - → Faqr: *Notice where the having-little has become something you are proud of. That pride is the thing still owned.*
    - → Sabr: *Stay one day longer in the difficulty than feels reasonable, without narrating it to anyone.*
    - → Tawakkul: *Pick one outcome you are managing and stop managing it for a week. Watch what you do with the free hands.*
    - → Rida: *Say what is actually good about the situation you did not choose, without adding the word but.*
    - → Mahabba: *Let yourself want it. Contentment that has never wanted anything is not yet contentment.*
    - → Marifa: *Sit with the Beloved without asking for anything, including closeness. Notice who is disappointed.*
    - → Fana: *When the knowing arrives, do not write it down. Let it pass unrecorded and see what remains.*

  - **The pitfall you named** (Q15) surfaced back as the user's own self-knowledge; the recognition is part of the work.

  - **Resisted integration:** if Q21 was answered, reflect the user's own words back.

## Tone reminders

- Wise, sincere, grounded. Witness before wisdom. Weight, not flourish.
- In your own words: no em dashes. No "unlock," "transform," "transcend," "breakthrough," "awakening" as marketing. (Question and option copy is the shipped app wording and stays as is.)
- Numina lexicon overlaps with Sufi: turning, longing, the Beloved, the Friend, witness, threshold, simplicity, surrender, ground, terrain.
- **Maqam vs hal is honoured on every result.** The user has the right to know whether they are living at a station or touching it by grace. Both are real; they are different things. Never collapse them.
- **Ujb is surfaced as part of the path, never as accusation.** Noticing it is already a turn. The path is spiral.
- **Don't sentimentalise.** The early stations are rigorous; the later ones are earned over years. A deep Tawba is not a lower self.
- **The Beloved is not a metaphor for self-improvement.** Hold the devotional register even for agnostic users, in their own word for it.
- **"Where you are, not who you are."** The stations are not a ranking of souls.
- Cross-reference only as genuine synthesis, when another map in `context-library/maps/` shows matching signal. The mountain is one; the maps name different parts of it.
