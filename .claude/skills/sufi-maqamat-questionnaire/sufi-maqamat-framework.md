# Sufi Maqamat framework — canonical content (NUM-304)

The reference the Sufi Maqamat Map and `/sufi-maqamat-questionnaire` draw from. **Use as reference, not script.** The map output should match where the user's heart and practice actually are, in their language. Never quote these layers verbatim.

Frame for audiences drawn to devotional, contemplative, and mystical practice, inside or outside Islam. Keep the Arabic terms out of question copy; they appear on result screens and in maps with translation, translated names primary, Arabic beside them. Arabic is part of the dignity of the tradition: show it, never gate understanding on knowing it.

**Core principle — where you are, not who you are.** This map reads a *current station* on a path of the heart, not a rank of souls. A seeker can be rightly, deeply in Tawba after decades. The early stations are rigorous, not lesser. The stations are earned slowly, and they are not a ranking of souls.

**Respect for the tradition.** Sufism (tasawwuf) is the mystical heart of Islam. Honour those roots, name them plainly when it helps, and never impose Islamic theology on a user from another path. "The Beloved" (al-Mahbub), "the Friend", "the Real" (al-Haqq), "the more-than-me": use the user's own word for what they turn toward. The devotional language is shared across mystical traditions (Christian bridal mysticism, Bhakti, Hasidic devotion). Don't sentimentalise. This is centuries of disciplined work, not a weekend retreat.

## The path

The extended maqamat (stations) list: Abu Nasr al-Sarraj's seven in *Kitab al-Luma* (Tawba through Rida), plus Mahabba, Marifa and Fana from later teachers (al-Qushayri, Ibn Arabi, Rumi). Ten stations, grouped into **five bands** of two stations each. The bands are what Q1-Q3 score; the station within a band is resolved by a short follow-up.

Rendered as a rising path of ten gates (geometric, garden or calligraphic, never figurative), the Arabic name of each station shown beside its translation.

| # | Band | Station | Arabic | Classical phase |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Turning | Repentance / Turning — Tawba | التوبة | purification (takhliya) |
| 2 | Turning | Scrupulousness — Wara' | الورع | purification |
| 3 | Letting Go | Renunciation — Zuhd | الزهد | purification |
| 4 | Letting Go | Spiritual Poverty — Faqr | الفقر | discipline (tahliya) |
| 5 | Trial & Trust | Patience — Sabr | الصبر | discipline |
| 6 | Trial & Trust | Reliance / Surrender — Tawakkul | التوكّل | discipline |
| 7 | Devotion | Contentment — Rida | الرضا | illumination (tajliya) |
| 8 | Devotion | Love — Mahabba | المحبة | illumination |
| 9 | Knowing | Gnosis — Marifa | المعرفة | union (wisal) |
| 10 | Knowing | Annihilation — Fana | الفناء | union |

Band themes (used for scoring open-ended language):

| Band | Theme |
|---|---|
| Turning | reorientation, conscience, the first honest turn |
| Letting Go | renunciation and open-handed poverty of ego |
| Trial & Trust | patience under fire, reliance beyond control |
| Devotion | contentment and love that reorders the heart |
| Knowing | tasted insight and the softening of the separate claim |

The phases carry the classical soul-states (nafs): purification loosens the commanding self (nafs al-ammara, النفس الأمارة); discipline is the battleground of the self-blaming soul (nafs al-lawwama, النفس اللوامة); illumination and union approach the soul at peace (nafs al-mutma'inna, النفس المطمئنة). Use these only as a gloss on a result, never as a verdict on the user's soul.

**The path is spiral, not a ladder.** Every noticing of pride is a fresh Tawba. A seeker returns to the early stations again and again, and that return is the work, not a regression.

## Maqam and hal — the central distinction

- **Maqam (مقام, station, plural maqamat):** earned through practice and trial. Stable. Returnable day after day, through dry times and bright ones.
- **Hal (حال, state, plural ahwal):** given by grace. Arrives and passes. Real, honoured, and not yet yours to live in.

A user can have the *state* of love without yet being in the *station* of Mahabba. Reporting a hal as a maqam is the characteristic error of self-assessment on this path, so the questionnaire asks about stability directly. **A passing state is never read as an earned station.** When the stability evidence points to hal, the reading says so plainly: "touched as hal", "read this as weather rather than ground for now". It is never framed as a lesser result.

The common ahwal (for recognising a state when it shows up in entries or answers):

| Hal | Arabic | Texture |
|---|---|---|
| Intimacy — Uns | الأنس | felt nearness, comfort, belonging |
| Awe — Haybah | الهيبة | tremulous reverence before majesty |
| Expansion — Inbisat | الانبساط | the heart opening in joy and beauty |
| Contraction — Qabd | القبض | the felt presence withdrawn; dryness that purifies |
| Annihilation — Fana (as hal) | الفناء | a moment of the self dissolving in presence |
| Subsistence — Baqa | البقاء | returning after dissolution, acting from what remains |

Note that Fana appears twice: a *moment* of dissolving is a hal; Fana as a *station* is the stable end of spiritual ownership. **Fana is never claimed from a single experience.** One vivid dissolving, however real, is a state. The station is sober, lived over years, and leaves no one behind to claim it.

## 1. Tawba — Repentance / Turning (التوبة)

- **Essence:** The turning of the heart away from heedlessness toward the Real. Not guilt but an honest reckoning: remorse for the past, stopping in the present, resolve for the future.
- **Lived markers:** Seeing old patterns with new honesty; catching oneself and turning back; the wish to reorient before going further.
- **Gift:** The soil of humility everything else grows from.
- **Pitfall:** Self-scrutiny becoming a new ego project; conscience hardening into relentless self-attack.
- **Classical line:** "Turn to God with sincere repentance." (Quran 66:8)
- **Integration signal:** Turning becomes a rhythm rather than a crisis.

## 2. Wara' — Scrupulousness (الورع)

- **Essence:** Declining what is doubtful so the heart stays clear. Gentle vigilance, not anxiety about purity.
- **Lived markers:** Care about what one takes in (food, media, company, action); preferring the cleaner path when something feels murky.
- **Gift:** Remembrance that stays unclouded.
- **Pitfall:** Scruple turning harsh, prudish, or judgemental toward self and others.
- **Classical line:** "Leave what makes you doubt for what does not make you doubt." (Hadith)
- **Integration signal:** Care without rigidity; the conscience awake and kind.

## 3. Zuhd — Renunciation (الزهد)

- **Essence:** Freedom from the world's grip on the heart, not hatred of the world. The world in the hand, not in the heart.
- **Lived markers:** Possessions, status and outcomes loosening their hooks; travelling lighter.
- **Gift:** Room for what endures.
- **Pitfall:** Renunciation as spiritual showing-off (riya, الرياء); contempt for life dressed as detachment.
- **Classical line:** "Do not let the life of this world deceive you." (Quran 35:5)
- **Integration signal:** Having little stops being a discipline and starts being a relief.

## 4. Faqr — Spiritual Poverty (الفقر)

- **Essence:** Utter dependence. Emptying the self of every claim to self-sufficiency, including spiritual attainment. The hollow reed.
- **Lived markers:** Empty-handed; the gift of having little, not only the discipline of giving up.
- **Gift:** A clear vessel, owning nothing, receiving everything.
- **Pitfall:** Poverty becoming a new identity; pride in having little (the pride is the thing still owned).
- **Classical line:** "You are the ones in need of God." (Quran 35:15)
- **Integration signal:** No claim left to defend when trial comes.

## 5. Sabr — Patience (الصبر)

- **Essence:** Holding difficulty without being shattered or fleeing. Dynamic containment, not suppression.
- **Lived markers:** Showing up day after day through what's hard, without bitterness; practice through dry times and grief.
- **Gift:** Depth forged under pressure.
- **Pitfall:** Patience hardening into stoicism. Sabr is open patience, not gritted teeth.
- **Classical line:** "God is with the patient." (Quran 2:153)
- **Integration signal:** The holding starts to soften into trust.

## 6. Tawakkul — Reliance / Surrender (التوكّل)

- **Essence:** Acting with full effort, then releasing the result to a care larger than one's own.
- **Lived markers:** Effort and surrender coexisting; calm when the outcome is uncertain.
- **Gift:** A heart that rests without needing control.
- **Pitfall:** Surrender becoming passivity or fatalism. Tawakkul acts without bargaining; it does not stop acting.
- **Classical line:** "Whoever puts their trust in God, He is sufficient for them." (Quran 65:3). Practice often named: *Hasbunallah wa ni'mal-Wakil* (God is enough for us, the best of trustees).
- **Integration signal:** Doing gives way to receiving.

## 7. Rida — Contentment (الرضا)

- **Essence:** Peace with what is. An active, inner yes to reality as it unfolds, not resignation.
- **Lived markers:** Meeting what comes without complaint; joy and sorrow received with an even heart; the wanting itself softened.
- **Gift:** An inner world stable whatever the weather.
- **Pitfall:** Contentment becoming complacency; acceptance that has never wanted anything.
- **Classical line:** "God is pleased with them and they are pleased with Him." (Quran 98:8)
- **Integration signal:** Contentment warms into love.

## 8. Mahabba — Love (المحبة)

- **Essence:** Love as the heart's gravity, reordering every lesser affection toward the Beloved.
- **Lived markers:** Heart-led practice; devotion, longing (shawq, شوق), sometimes tears; earthly beauty becoming a mirror.
- **Gift:** Love without bargaining.
- **Pitfall:** Devotion becoming sentimentality; love becoming need or romantic projection.
- **Classical line:** "He loves them and they love Him." (Quran 5:54)
- **Integration signal:** Love quiets into a knowing that needs no proof.

## 9. Marifa — Gnosis (المعرفة)

- **Essence:** Knowledge of the Real that is tasted, not argued. The heart knows by nearness.
- **Lived markers:** Insight arriving as intimacy; clear seeing that clarifies action without inflating the seer.
- **Gift:** Seeing without the claim of mastery.
- **Pitfall:** Knowing becoming an experience the self collects.
- **Classical line:** "Those who strive for Us, We will surely guide them to Our ways." (Quran 29:69)
- **Integration signal:** The knower thins along with the claim.

## 10. Fana — Annihilation (الفناء)

- **Essence:** The soft dissolving of the separate claim of self. Not the erasure of life but the end of pretending to be the centre. Life lived through, not as, the separate self; followed by baqa, return and service.
- **Lived markers:** Service and presence continuing without a hero of the path; the claim of spiritual ownership loosening.
- **Gift:** Freedom from the small self's grip.
- **Pitfall:** The most warned-against of all. Authentic Fana does not leave a Fana-ist behind to claim it. If part of you is enjoying having attained it, the work isn't done.
- **Classical line:** "Everything will perish except His Face." (Quran 28:88)
- **Integration signal:** Sobriety. Ordinary life, lived without a centre to defend.

## Ujb — the safety spine

The Sufi teachers wrote more about spiritual pride than any other pitfall: **ujb** (العجب, secret self-admiration), **riya** (الرياء, showing off), **takabbur** (التكبر, arrogance). Al-Muhasibi, al-Ghazali and Ibn Ata' Allah returned to it constantly.

- Ujb is read only where a user has placed themselves in the later stations (Devotion or Knowing current). It is specifically the pride of having arrived.
- Surface it as a stage of the work, never as accusation. The user who notices their own ujb has already begun the next turn: Tawba again. The path is spiral.
- Watch that the watching does not become the next thing to be pleased about.

## Pluralism

The Beloved goes by many names. Ibn Arabi: "My heart has become capable of every form." This map is one cartography of overlapping territory. When a user holds it as the only true one, suggest reading their experience against another map for a while. Cross-map lines only when both maps actually show signal: Sabr with a long dark stretch of trial, Rida with acceptance and peace, Fana with the "both forgotten" stages of other contemplative maps, Mahabba with devotional love elsewhere.

## Placement (from evidence)

- Place the user at one **current station** with a faint **edge** on the next station that is gathering.
- Be conservative. Most sincere seekers live for years in the first three bands. Only place in Knowing with repeated, sober, *lived* evidence (humility, service, a loosened claim), never from one luminous entry.
- **Stability over vividness.** Repeated return across entries reads as maqam. A single intense experience reads as hal, whatever the station it resembles. A vivid dissolving is a hal of Fana, not the station.
- Love-language alone does not place someone in Mahabba; it may be a hal of uns or inbisat. Look for love that reorders choices over time.
