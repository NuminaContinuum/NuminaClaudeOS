# /gunasthanas-questionnaire — The Guṇasthānas questionnaire

One skill for the Jain ladder assessment. It runs in stages and knows where the user is:

- **Q1 — Quick placement** (3-5 min). Scores five bands across stages 1-7, resolves the stage within the winning band, and runs one vow check. Confidence after Q1: **rough**.
- **Q2 — Full stage profile** (10-12 min). Resolves a state for each of the seven placeable stages and applies the **full attainment ceiling** (vow, renunciation, vigilance). Confidence after Q2: **deeper**.
- **Q3 — Settled reading** (20-30 min). A long reading where what the user describes doing (an ordinary day, a vow inventory) carries the weight, the ceiling runs again, and the four passions are named. Confidence after Q3: **settled**.

The canonical content for all fourteen stages (meaning, classical description, characteristics, lived markers, next real step), the bands, the states, the ceiling copy, the four passions, and the evidence signals lives in `gunasthanas-framework.md`, in this skill's folder. Both this questionnaire and `/maps gunasthanas` draw from it. Adapt the tone to where the user actually is; do not quote it verbatim, except the fixed notes (calibration, ceiling, depth, pride) and the next real step, which are canonical copy.

The map itself is generated by `/maps gunasthanas`. This skill feeds it structured readings and offers to refresh it afterward.

**Core principle — where you are, not who you are.** The first stage is where the path begins, not a failing grade. Someone thirty years into practice can sit rightly and deeply at stage four. The map is hierarchical and there is no hiding that; the tone that works is matter-of-fact: this is where the conduct is, and here is the next real step.

**The placeable range.** Only stages **1-7** can be placed. Stages **8-10** (the purification ascent) are visible on the result but never claimable. Stages **11-14** (passions suppressed, passions destroyed, omniscience, final liberation) are narrative only: described as the summit, never offered as an answer, never placed. No question in any stage addresses them.

**Rounding.** On this ladder an honest reading rounds down, never up. Every tie, every multi-claim, every cap resolves to the lower stage. Depth of practice never inflates a placement.

**Questions stay in plain language.** No Sanskrit, no stage names, no band labels in the answer options. The Sanskrit names (with translation) appear only on the result, transliterated name first: **Avirata Samyagdṛṣṭi** (Right Faith Without Vows, stage 4).

## Step 0 — Figure out where the user is

Read `context-library/maps/gunasthanas.md` if it exists and check which stages have run (`## Q1 Reading`, `## Q2 Reading`, `## Q3 Reading`). Route:

- **Nothing done yet** → run Q1.
- **Q1 done, Q2 not** → offer Q2: *"You've got the quick placement. The next reflection (about ten minutes) looks at all seven stages you can honestly report on: which one you live in, which you've passed through, which is pulling at you, and where your conduct actually sits. Want to do it now?"* If they decline, stop gracefully.
- **Q1 and Q2 done, Q3 not** → offer Q3: *"You've seen the whole ladder. The long reading (20-30 minutes) settles it from what you actually do: an ordinary day, what you've given up and kept giving up, and the passion with the strongest grip. Want to do it now, or another time?"* If they decline, stop gracefully.
- **All three done** → offer to re-run a stage for a fresh read, or refresh the map from everything since.

Never push toward deeper stages. Q2 and Q3 are standalone-capable if the user asks for them directly. Honour an explicit stage request.

## Q1 — Quick placement

### Q1 Step 1 — Open

Present this intro, then begin immediately.

> *Where does the path actually begin for you?*
>
> *The Jain tradition maps fourteen stages of spiritual development, from living entirely on reaction to complete liberation. Most of a lifetime is spent in the first handful, and that is not a failing. It is what the map is for.*
>
> *Answer eight short questions. At the end you will see roughly where you stand, and the one next step that would be real rather than aspirational.*
>
> *Answer for what you actually do, not what you intend.*

### Q1 Step 2 — Present the questions

One at a time. Wait for each answer. Accept the letter only. **No commentary between questions.** Option order is already shuffled below (the letter does not map to a fixed band) and band names are hidden, so the result can't be gamed. **The parenthetical tag is for your scoring only — never show it to the user.**

**1. When something goes wrong in an ordinary day, what usually happens in you?**
(a) I react before I notice I am reacting. It runs the whole day sometimes *(Unexamined)*
(b) I catch it afterwards, sometimes hours later, and wish I had seen it sooner *(Glimpsed)*
(c) I see it clearly as it happens, and I still do the thing anyway *(Seeing)*
(d) I catch it in time more often than not, because I have made specific commitments about it *(Partial)*
(e) It rarely takes hold. Watching for it is simply what I do now *(Committed)*

**2. Which sentence is the most honest description of your inner life this year?**
(a) Something opened once and then closed again. I keep half-looking for it *(Glimpsed)*
(b) I understand what matters. My life has not rearranged itself around that yet *(Seeing)*
(c) I have changed some concrete things, and kept them changed *(Partial)*
(d) The whole shape of my life is organised around this now *(Committed)*
(e) I have not really thought of my life in these terms *(Unexamined)*

**3. What is your relationship to the idea of taking a vow?**
(a) I believe in it. I have not actually taken one *(Seeing)*
(b) I keep a small number of specific ones, and I know exactly what they are *(Partial)*
(c) I have taken them completely and they govern everything *(Committed)*
(d) It is not something I have considered *(Unexamined)*
(e) I have made promises to myself and broken most of them *(Glimpsed)*

**4. Which of these is closest to how certainty feels to you right now?**
(a) It settled, and then it started asking things of me *(Partial)*
(b) It is no longer separate from how I live *(Committed)*
(c) I am not looking for certainty. Things are as they are *(Unexamined)*
(d) It comes and goes. Some weeks I see it, some weeks it is fog *(Glimpsed)*
(e) It has settled. What is true stopped being a question for me *(Seeing)*

**5. Where does most of your energy go?**
(a) Practice itself. It is not one part of my life among others *(Committed)*
(b) Getting through the day, wanting what I want *(Unexamined)*
(c) Back and forth between wanting to change and going back to normal *(Glimpsed)*
(d) Understanding. Reading, thinking, seeing more clearly *(Seeing)*
(e) Holding a few real limits, inside an otherwise ordinary life *(Partial)*

**6. What most reliably knocks you off course?**
(a) Nothing knocks my understanding. My habits are another matter *(Seeing)*
(b) Nothing knocks me off. There is no course yet *(Unexamined)*
(c) Inattention. Not appetite, just moments of going slack *(Committed)*
(d) Almost anything. That is the part I find discouraging *(Glimpsed)*
(e) Pressure from other people, or my own tiredness, at the edges of what I have committed to *(Partial)*

**7. Which of these have you actually done, not intended to do?** *(the vow-calibration question, see Step 3)*
(a) There are specific things I have given up, by name, and kept giving up *(Partial)*
(b) I have restructured my life around a full set of commitments *(Committed)*
(c) None of this is part of how I think about my life *(Unexamined)*
(d) I have decided several things are wrong for me. I have not stopped doing them *(Seeing)*
(e) I have started things like this and not sustained them *(Glimpsed)*

**8. If you had to name where you honestly are:**
(a) Something opened, and I cannot get back to it reliably *(Glimpsed)*
(b) I have changed some things for real, within an ordinary life *(Partial)*
(c) At the beginning, or not on the path at all *(Unexamined)*
(d) I have given the whole thing over to this *(Committed)*
(e) I can see clearly. I have not changed accordingly *(Seeing)*

### Q1 Step 3 — Score inline

Tally **band content tags** (not letter positions) across Q1-Q8. The dominant band wins.

**Resolve the stage within the band:**

- **Unexamined** → stage 1 (Mithyātva). No tiebreaker.
- **Seeing** → stage 4 (Avirata Samyagdṛṣṭi). No tiebreaker.
- **Partial** → stage 5 (Deśa Virata). No tiebreaker.
- **Glimpsed** → fire the Glimpsed tiebreaker.
- **Committed** → fire the Committed tiebreaker.

**Tiebreakers** (only when that band wins; shuffle the two options; tags hidden). Introduce with: *"One last distinction, then I'll show you where this lands on the ladder."*

*Glimpsed (stages 2-3)* — **Which is closer to how it actually goes?**
- It opened once, clearly, and then closed. I remember the taste of it *(Stage 2, Sāsvādana)*
- It alternates. Clarity and confusion trade places and neither wins *(Stage 3, Mishra)*

*Committed (stages 6-7)* — **Within that commitment, which is closer?**
- The commitment is complete. My attention to it still lapses *(Stage 6, Pramatta Sarva Virata)*
- The commitment is complete and the attention holds steady *(Stage 7, Apramatta Sarva Virata)*

**Band tie handling:**

- If **none** of the tied bands has a tiebreaker (Unexamined, Seeing, Partial), resolve to the **lowest** tied band. Rationale: on this ladder an honest reading rounds down, never up.
- If **exactly one** tied band has a tiebreaker (Glimpsed or Committed), that band wins and its tiebreaker fires.
- If **both** Glimpsed and Committed are tied, merge both tiebreakers' four options, shuffle, and ask: *"Glimpsed and Committed came out level. Which nuance fits better? Options from both are shuffled together. Pick the closest fit."* (The only place a band label is shown.) The option picked sets the stage.

**Vow calibration (runs last, overrides any path into a placement).** If the resolved stage is in **Partial** or **Committed** (stages 5-7) and the answer to Q7 was tagged Unexamined, Glimpsed or Seeing (no concrete, named, kept restraint), resolve the placement to **stage 4 (Avirata Samyagdṛṣṭi)** and surface the calibration note. Downgrade only, never an upgrade.

### Q1 Step 4 — Render the result

**ASCII ladder first**, all fourteen stages, grouped by phase. The resolved stage lit (●), a soft halo (◌) on the other stage in the same band (Glimpsed and Committed only; none for single-stage bands). Stages 8-10 marked as the unclimbed ascent (┆), 11-14 as the summit (△), above a line marking the end of the placeable range. Adapt to the actual result:

```
   △  14  Ayogi Kevalī               Omniscient without Activity
   △  13  Sayogi Kevalī              Omniscient with Activity
   △  12  Kṣīṇa Moha                 Passions Destroyed
   △  11  Upaśānta Moha              Passions Suppressed
   ┆  10  Sūkṣma Sāmparāya           Subtle Passions
   ┆   9  Anivṛtti Karaṇa            Advanced Purification
   ┆   8  Apūrva Karaṇa              Unprecedented Spiritual Progress
  ─────────────────────────────────── the ladder above is shown, never placed
   ◌   7  Apramatta Sarva Virata     Complete Vows Without Negligence
   ●   6  Pramatta Sarva Virata      Complete Vows with Negligence     ← you
   ·   5  Deśa Virata                Partial Vows
   ·   4  Avirata Samyagdṛṣṭi        Right Faith Without Vows
   ·   3  Mishra                     Mixed Belief
   ·   2  Sāsvādana                  Taste of Right Faith
   ·   1  Mithyātva                  Wrong Belief

   ● you   ◌ same band   ┆ the ascent   △ the summit
   liberation ▸ purification ▸ discipline (5-7) ▸ faith (4) ▸ delusion (1-3)
```

Then the stage name (transliterated Sanskrit first, then the translation and number), and a 2-3 sentence reading drawn from the stage's **meaning** in `gunasthanas-framework.md`, grounded in what the user actually answered. Held as hypothesis.

Example microcopy (stage 4):
> *You're at **Avirata Samyagdṛṣṭi** (Right Faith Without Vows, stage 4). Right seeing has dawned: you can tell what's true from what isn't, and your conduct hasn't caught up yet. Vision arrives before discipline, for almost everyone. This is not a lower self. It's the rung where the work becomes specific.*

If the vow calibration fired, add the calibration note verbatim:
> *Your answers describe clear seeing more than changed conduct. On this map those are different stages, and the difference is not a judgement: seeing arrives before behaviour does, for almost everyone. We have placed you where the conduct is. One named, kept restraint is what moves it.*

Then **the next real step**, verbatim from the framework file for the resolved stage (one small, concrete, keepable move).

End with the confidence label, a line that Q1 places one stage only, and the CTA:

> *This is a rough placement, one stage only. Q2 resolves a state for every stage you can honestly report on and applies the full ceiling, so conduct, not self-rating, sets the rung. Want the fuller reading, with every stage and an honest ceiling? It takes about ten minutes. Confidence: rough.*

## Q2 — Full stage profile + attainment ceiling (10-12 min)

**Gate:** standalone-capable. If `gunasthanas.md` has a `## Q1 Reading`, open by surfacing it: *"Your first reading placed you at [Sanskrit name] ([translation]). This one looks at every stage you can honestly report on, and checks where conduct actually sits."* Otherwise use the intro alone.

> *The whole ladder, and where your conduct actually sits.*
>
> *Ten to twelve minutes. Fourteen questions. We will look at each of the seven stages you can honestly report on, and see which one you live in, which you have already passed through, which is pulling at you, and where the honest ceiling falls.*
>
> *Answer for what you actually do, not what you intend.*

Q2 resolves a **state per stage** for stages 1-7 (current / familiar / emerging / dormant / not_yet) and applies the ceiling. 14 questions in three sections. One at a time, no commentary between. **State and stage tags are for your scoring only — never show them.** Shuffle option order on every question.

### Q2 Section 1 — Stage scan (7 questions, one per placeable stage)

Introduce once: *"Pick the description closest to your current experience."*

**1. Living on reaction, before the question opens:** This is where I live. My reactions run most days *(current)* / Familiar. I know this state well and still fall back into it *(familiar)* / Dormant. Not where I am now *(dormant)* / Pulling at me. Honestly, I feel myself sliding back toward it *(emerging)* / I have never thought about my life this way at all *(not_yet)* — *Stage 1*
**2. Having tasted something clearer, and lost it:** This is where I live. I had it once and I am trying to find my way back *(current)* / Familiar. It happened, and I have moved past the ache of it *(familiar)* / Dormant. Not part of my experience *(dormant)* / Pulling at me. Something is starting to open *(emerging)* / Nothing like this has happened to me *(not_yet)* — *Stage 2*
**3. Clarity and confusion alternating, neither winning:** This is where I live. I go back and forth and cannot settle it *(current)* / Familiar. I know the wavering. It has mostly resolved *(familiar)* / Dormant. My mind is not divided this way *(dormant)* / Pulling at me. The certainty I had is starting to come apart *(emerging)* / I have not got far enough for this to apply *(not_yet)* — *Stage 3*
**4. Seeing clearly while behaviour stays where it was:** This is where I live. I know what is true and I have not changed accordingly *(current)* / Familiar. I passed through it. Conduct has caught up since *(familiar)* / Dormant. Not the tension I am in *(dormant)* / Pulling at me. Things are becoming clear and I can feel what it will ask *(emerging)* / Not yet. Nothing has settled into that kind of clarity *(not_yet)* — *Stage 4*
**5. Keeping specific, named restraints inside an ordinary life:** This is where I live. There are things I have given up and kept giving up *(current)* / Familiar. I have done this, though it has loosened *(familiar)* / Dormant. Not something I am working with *(dormant)* / Pulling at me. I know which commitment is asking to be made *(emerging)* / Not yet. I have not taken anything on in this way *(not_yet)* — *Stage 5*
**6. Complete commitment, with attention that still lapses:** This is where I live. The commitment is whole. My vigilance is not *(current)* / Familiar. I have lived this way before *(familiar)* / Dormant. Far from where I am *(dormant)* / Pulling at me. I can feel the whole thing asking for me *(emerging)* / Not yet. This is not within reach of my life *(not_yet)* — *Stage 6*
**7. Complete commitment held with steady attention:** This is where I live. It holds without effort now *(current)* / Familiar. I have touched it and lost it *(familiar)* / Dormant. Not in play *(dormant)* / Pulling at me. Steadiness is what I am working toward *(emerging)* / Not yet. This is well beyond me *(not_yet)* — *Stage 7*

### Q2 Section 2 — Conduct probes (4 questions)

Each answer adds a +1 nudge toward the stage it implies. Probes 8, 9 and 10 also feed the ceiling.

**8. What do you actually abstain from, right now?** Nothing in particular *(Stage 1)* / I try to cut back on things, without much success *(Stage 3)* / I know what I should stop and I have not stopped it *(Stage 4)* / A short, specific list I could recite to you *(Stage 5; vow evidence)* / A full set of commitments that shape everything *(Stage 6; vow evidence)*
**9. What most often breaks your vigilance?** Nothing breaks it, because I am not holding one *(Stage 1)* / Wanting things. Appetite wins *(Stage 3)* / I see it coming and go along with it anyway *(Stage 4)* / Pressure and tiredness at the edges of what I have committed to *(Stage 5)* / Going slack for a moment. Not appetite, just inattention *(Stage 6)* / It rarely breaks *(Stage 7; vigilance evidence)*
**10. What have you formally committed to, in front of someone else?** Nothing *(Stage 1)* / Things I said out loud and quietly dropped *(Stage 3)* / Nothing formal, though I have decided a lot privately *(Stage 4)* / Specific undertakings I have named to someone who would notice *(Stage 5; commitment evidence)* / Full vows, taken in a tradition or a community *(Stage 6; commitment evidence)*
**11. What are you most tired of defending?** I am not defending anything *(Stage 1)* / The version of me that keeps starting over *(Stage 3)* / The gap between what I say matters and what I do *(Stage 4)* / The exceptions I make to my own rules *(Stage 5)* / The idea that my practice is going well *(Stage 7)*

### Q2 Section 3 — Cross-cutting Likert (3 items, 1-5)

Ask each as "1 = not at all true, 5 = very true."

**12.** *There are specific things I have given up and not resumed.* (restraint; 4+ counts as vow evidence)
**13.** *My attention to how I act is steady rather than intermittent.* (vigilance; 4+ counts as vigilance evidence)
**14.** *I would describe my life as organised around practice rather than containing it.* (organised; 4+ counts as commitment evidence)

### Q2 Step 4 — Resolve

1. **States.** Section 1 gives one state per stage (an unanswered stage counts as dormant).
2. **One current stage.** If no stage came out current, take the **highest familiar** stage as current (or stage 1 if nothing is familiar) and say so. If several came out current, take the **lowest** as current and mark the others familiar: on this ladder an honest reading rounds down.
3. **The attainment ceiling**, in order, on the current stage (copy in `gunasthanas-framework.md`):
   - **Vow check.** Stage 5+ needs probe 8 = "short, specific list" or "full set of commitments", or Likert 12 ≥ 4. Otherwise cap at **stage 4**.
   - **Renunciation check.** Stage 6+ needs probe 10 = "specific undertakings named to someone" or "full vows", or Likert 14 ≥ 4. Otherwise cap at **stage 5**.
   - **Vigilance check.** Stage 7 needs probe 9 = "It rarely breaks", or Likert 13 ≥ 4. Otherwise resolve to **stage 6**.
   Caps only move down; several can fire in sequence. When the placement moves, the stage that was claimed becomes **emerging** (the edge being reached for) and the capped stage becomes current.
4. **Confidence per stage.** HIGH where any probe nudged toward that stage; MEDIUM where the scan alone placed it. The scan always decides the state; probes only corroborate.

Typical shape: 1 current, 1-2 familiar, 0-1 emerging, the rest dormant or not_yet.

### Q2 Step 5 — Render the result

- The ASCII ladder (same layout as Q1), stages 1-7 styled by state: **●** current, **◌** familiar, **◦** emerging, **·** dormant, blank for not_yet. 8-10 as the ascent (┆), 11-14 as the summit (△).
- A 1-2 sentence reading for the current, each familiar and each emerging stage, built from the state reading lines in the framework file ("Known ground: …", "Reaching toward this: …"), grounded in the user's answers.
- If the current stage was inferred (no stage claimed): *"None of the stages came out as where you live now, so I've placed you at the highest one you called familiar. Treat it as a starting point."*
- **A ceiling note for each cap that fired**, verbatim from the framework file, framed as a more honest reading rather than a demotion, naming what would move it.
- **The next real step** for the resolved current stage.
- **Summary line**, in this shape: *"You live at [current]. [Familiar stage(s)] is/are still familiar underneath. [Emerging stage(s)] is/are what you are reaching for."* Use the Sanskrit name with translation, e.g. *"You live at Avirata Samyagdṛṣṭi (Right Faith Without Vows). Mishra (Mixed Belief) is still familiar underneath. Deśa Virata (Partial Vows) is what you are reaching for."*
- Confidence: **deeper**. Restate "where you are, not who you are." CTA: *"Continue to the long reading when you're ready."*

### Q2 Step 6 — Write the Q2 reading and offer refresh

Prepend a `## Q2 Reading — YYYY-MM-DD` section (above the Q1 reading) to `gunasthanas.md` with: **Confidence: deeper**, the state per stage (1-7), the claimed stage and the current stage, each ceiling rule that fired, the next real step, and the summary line. Then offer to refresh the Guṇasthānas map (merging Q2 with Q1 + harvested notes; raises the confidence header).

## Q3 — Long reading (20-30 min)

**Gate:** aware of Q1/Q2, standalone-capable otherwise (Section 2 is the baseline). Open with:

- If a prior reading exists: *"Your earlier reading placed you at [Sanskrit name] ([translation]). This is the long one: several questions are yours to answer in your own words, and you can stop and come back. What you describe doing counts for more here than what you name."*
- Otherwise: *"This is the long reading. Several questions are yours to answer in your own words, and you can stop and come back at any point. What you describe doing counts for more here than what you name."*

The longest read: a **settled** state per stage, decided by described conduct and woven with the user's own words. **23 questions across seven sections** (9 open-ended, 11 multiple-choice, 3 Likert). One at a time, no commentary between. **Tags are for your scoring only — never show them.** Shuffle multiple-choice option order. Voice input welcome on the open-ended questions; the user can pause and resume. Show the time hint in brackets after each open-ended prompt.

### Q3 Section 1 — Settling in (2 open-ended)

**1.** *Before any of the questions: what made you sit down with this today? Not the tidy answer — the actual reason, even if it is boredom or a bad week.* (2-3 min)
**2.** *How would you describe the way you live now to someone who has never met you? Not your beliefs. What you do with your days.* (3-4 min) — hint: *Take your time. The rest of the reading leans on this one.*

### Q3 Section 2 — The seven-stage scan (7 multiple-choice)

Deliberately not the Q2 wording: Q2 asks where each rung sits; this asks the same thing through what a week actually looks like. Introduce once: *"Pick the description closest to your daily reality."*

**3. A week where your reactions ran the show and no question opened:** That is most weeks, honestly *(current)* / I know those weeks. They still happen *(familiar)* / Rare now *(dormant)* / I can feel myself sliding back toward that *(emerging)* / I have never looked at a week that way *(not_yet)* — *Stage 1*
**4. A week spent trying to get back something you saw once and lost:** That is where I am. I am trying to find my way back *(current)* / I had that stretch. The ache has passed *(familiar)* / Not part of my experience *(dormant)* / Something is starting to open that I might lose *(emerging)* / Nothing like that has happened to me *(not_yet)* — *Stage 2*
**5. A week of arguing both sides with yourself and settling neither:** That is where I am. I cannot get it to resolve *(current)* / I know that wavering. It has mostly settled *(familiar)* / My mind is not divided that way *(dormant)* / What I was certain of is coming apart *(emerging)* / I have not got far enough for that to apply *(not_yet)* — *Stage 3*
**6. A week where you knew exactly what was true and changed nothing:** That is where I am, and it is uncomfortable *(current)* / I passed through it. Conduct caught up *(familiar)* / Not the tension I am in *(dormant)* / Things are clarifying and I can feel what it will cost *(emerging)* / Nothing has settled into that kind of clarity *(not_yet)* — *Stage 4*
**7. A week inside an ordinary life with specific things you did not touch:** That is my week. There is a list and I keep it *(current)* / I have lived that way. It has loosened *(familiar)* / Not something I am working with *(dormant)* / I know which commitment is asking to be made *(emerging)* / I have not taken anything on that way *(not_yet)* — *Stage 5*
**8. A week shaped whole by commitment, with attention that kept slipping:** That is my week. The commitment holds; the attention does not *(current)* / I have lived that way before *(familiar)* / Far from where I am *(dormant)* / I can feel the whole thing asking for me *(emerging)* / That is not within reach of my life *(not_yet)* — *Stage 6*
**9. A week where the commitment held and the attention did not lapse:** That is my week. It holds without effort now *(current)* / I have touched that and lost it *(familiar)* / Not in play *(dormant)* / Steadiness is what I am working toward *(emerging)* / That is well beyond me *(not_yet)* — *Stage 7*

### Q3 Section 3 — Conduct in practice (2 open-ended + 2 multiple-choice)

**10.** *Describe an ordinary day. What you eat and do not eat. What you buy, keep, refuse, or throw away. Who you speak sharply to and what you do afterwards. What you reach for when the day goes badly.* (4-5 min) — hint: *The unremarkable details are the useful ones. This question places you more accurately than anything else here.*
**11.** *List what you have actually given up and not resumed. Say when each one started and whether it has held. If the honest answer is nothing, write that — it is a real answer and it costs you nothing here.* (2-3 min) — hint: *Intentions do not count on this map. A restraint you could name to someone else does.*
**12. Of the things you just listed, which is closest to true?** There was nothing to list *(Stage 1)* / Things I cut back on and drift from *(Stage 3)* / I know what I should stop and I have not stopped it *(Stage 4)* / A short, specific list I have kept *(Stage 5; vow evidence)* / A full set of commitments that shape everything *(Stage 6; vow evidence)*
**13. What have you committed to in front of someone who would notice?** Nothing *(Stage 1)* / Things I said out loud and quietly dropped *(Stage 3)* / Nothing formal, though I have decided a lot privately *(Stage 4)* / Specific undertakings someone else could hold me to *(Stage 5; commitment evidence)* / Full vows, taken in a tradition or a community *(Stage 6; commitment evidence)*

> *Scoring:* Q10 (the ordinary day) is the load-bearing question: what someone actually eats, refuses, keeps and reacts to places them where no self-rating can. ### Q3 Section 4 — Adaptive deep-dive (3 open-ended)

From the Section 2 scan, select 3 stages, in this order, skipping duplicates:

1. **The anchor:** the current stage (else the emerging, else the familiar, else stage 1).
2. **The stage carrying the most charge:** the emerging stage (what they're reaching for), else the familiar one (what they still fall back to).
3. **The next rung:** the stage just above the anchor, the next real question rather than a distant one.

If fewer than 3 distinct stages result, fill from stages 1-7 in ladder order. Only stages 1-7 ever get a deep-dive. Each 2-4 min, voice or text.

- **Stage 1 (Mithyātva)** — *"Describe a reaction you had this week that you would rather not have had. What set it off, how long it ran, and what it cost. Not what you should have done — what happened."*
- **Stage 2 (Sāsvādana)** — *"Describe what you saw when it was clear, and describe losing it. What was different about you then? What have you been doing since to get back?"*
- **Stage 3 (Mishra)** — *"Describe the thing you are most divided about. Give both sides as fairly as you can, then say which one you act on when nobody is watching."*
- **Stage 4 (Avirata Samyagdṛṣṭi)** — *"Describe the gap between what you know and what you do. What is the specific thing you have not stopped? What would actually happen if you stopped it?"*
- **Stage 5 (Deśa Virata)** — *"Describe how one of your restraints works on a hard day. Where does it bend? What exception have you started making that you would rather not admit to?"*
- **Stage 6 (Pramatta Sarva Virata)** — *"Describe a lapse. Not an appetite you gave in to — a moment the attention simply went slack. What was happening around it, and how long before you noticed?"*
- **Stage 7 (Apramatta Sarva Virata)** — *"Describe the steadiness without praising it. What is it actually like from inside? Where does the pleasure at having it creep in?"*

## Tone reminders

- Wise, sincere, grounded. Witness before wisdom.
- No em dashes in your own copy (quoted question copy is canonical and stays as written). No "unlock," "transform," "breakthrough," "enlightenment," "purity."
- Matter-of-fact about the hierarchy. Never shame a low placement, and never let the map become a purity score.
- **A cap is information, not a verdict.** Say what would move it. Naming a more honest stage gives better guidance than a flattered one.
- **Conduct beats self-rating.** An intention is not a vow. A restraint you could name to someone else is.
- The Jain frame is ethical and specific (*ahiṃsā*, *aparigraha*). Describe the vows plainly, not as generic wellness.
- **"Where you are, not who you are."** The first stage is where the path begins. A long, serious life can sit rightly at stage four.
- Cross-reference: the Wounds & Gifts map often shows the shape of the reactions that run the early rungs; the Yin & Yang map reads practice where this one reads conduct. Suggest only when both show signal.
