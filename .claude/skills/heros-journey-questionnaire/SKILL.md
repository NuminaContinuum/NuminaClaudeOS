# /heros-journey-questionnaire — The Hero's Journey map questionnaire

One skill for the whole Hero's Journey assessment. It runs in stages and knows where the user is:

- **Q1 — Quick placement** (3-5 min). A rough location on the arc — which of five phases the user is in. Confidence after Q1: **rough**.
- **Q2 — Deeper dive** (10-12 min). The relational and transformational texture: mentors and allies, the ordeal, what's being released, early signs of change, the emerging elixir. Confidence after Q2: **medium**.
- **Q3 — Settled reading** (20-30 min). A long reflection (open-ended + structured) that settles the placement on the 12-stage arc, woven with the user's own words. Confidence after Q3: **settled**.

The map itself is generated by `/maps heros-journey`. This skill feeds it structured readings and offers to refresh it after each stage.

---

## Step 0 — Figure out where the user is

Read `context-library/maps/heros-journey.md` if it exists and check which stages have run:

- A `## Q1 Reading` section means Q1 is done.
- A `## Q2 Reading` section means Q2 is done.

Then route:

- **Nothing done yet** → run Q1.
- **Q1 done, Q2 not** → offer Q2: *"You've placed yourself on the arc. Ready to go deeper? The next reflection takes about ten minutes — it looks at who's walked with you, the ordeal you're in or just through, and what's beginning to come back with you."* If they decline, stop gracefully.
- **Q1 and Q2 done, Q3 not** → offer Q3: *"You've placed yourself and gone deeper. The long reflection (20-30 minutes) is the most complete — mostly in your own words, it settles where you stand on the whole arc. Want to do it now, or another time?"* If they decline, stop gracefully.
- **All three done** → *"You've completed all three readings. Want to re-run one for a fresh placement, refresh the map from everything since, or leave it?"* Respect the answer.

Q3 is standalone-capable — if the user wants the long reflection without Q1/Q2, run it.

If the user explicitly asks for a specific stage, honour that.

## Q1 — Quick placement

### Q1 Step 1 — Open

> *Where are you on the Hero's Journey?*
>
> *A 3-4 minute reflection. Eight questions. Pick the answer closest to true. There are no right answers, only the one that meets you where you are.*

### Q1 Step 2 — Present the questions

One at a time. Wait for each answer. Accept the letter only. **No commentary between questions.**

**Q1. When you wake up these days, the first feeling is closest to:**

(a) Things are mostly fine, but there's a quiet restlessness
(b) Something is calling and I can't unhear it
(c) I've stepped into the work and it's reshaping me
(d) I'm in the middle of something hard, the old shape is breaking
(e) Something cracked open. I'm finding my feet again

**Q2. Your relationship with change:**

(a) I sense it coming but I haven't moved
(b) I've started, even if the steps are small
(c) I'm in it. Patterns I didn't see before are surfacing
(d) I'm being asked to let go of more than I expected
(e) I'm bringing what I've learned back into ordinary life

**Q3. Practices, teachers, or frameworks (meditation, therapy, plant medicine, journaling, traditions):**

(a) Curious, haven't really started
(b) Found one or two that speak to me, I'm learning
(c) Practising regularly and meeting what arises
(d) The practice isn't optional anymore. It's holding me through something
(e) I've integrated several and I'm finding my own language for it

**Q4. When you turn your attention inward:**

(a) I notice I avoid it more than I'd like
(b) I'm starting to look, with help
(c) I'm finding allies and naming the inner voices
(d) I'm sitting with something I used to run from
(e) Witnessing has become a way of being

**Q5. Community on the path:**

(a) Mostly alone with this
(b) I've started finding people who get it
(c) I have allies, and I've also met my inner critics
(d) The journey has felt lonely lately, even with others around
(e) I find myself being a guide for people who are earlier on

**Q6. The hardest thing you're holding right now:**

(a) Whether to begin at all
(b) The discomfort of leaving who I was
(c) Confronting a pattern I keep repeating
(d) A loss, an ego death, or meeting my shadow
(e) Translating insight into ordinary, daily life

**Q7. Looking at the last 12 months, the change in you is best described as:**

(a) Mostly the same, though something is asking for more
(b) Subtle but real. New questions are open
(c) Visible. People close to me have noticed
(d) Disorienting. I don't recognise parts of my old life
(e) Quiet and deep. I feel more myself than before

**Q8. If you imagine your future self five years from now:**

(a) I can barely picture them
(b) I see them and the road feels long
(c) I see them and I'm walking toward them
(d) The picture broke. Something truer is forming
(e) They're already arriving, in small daily ways

### Q1 Step 3 — Score inline

Count letter frequency across Q1-Q8. The dominant letter determines the phase:

| Letter | Phase | Stages |
|--------|-------|--------|
| (a) | A. Ordinary World and The Call | 1, 2, 3 |
| (b) | B. Crossing the Threshold | 4, 5 |
| (c) | C. Tests, Allies, and the Inmost Cave | 6, 7 |
| (d) | D. The Ordeal and The Reward | 8, 9 |
| (e) | E. The Return | 10, 11, 12 |

**Tie handling:** if two letters tie, ask the tiebreaker (Step 4) and let the user pick the closer fit.

If the tally is highly spread with no clear dominant letter, name it: *"The spread across your answers suggests you're in multiple phases at once — that's real, not a problem with the questionnaire. Here's where the weight seems to be sitting."* Then proceed with the plurality.

### Q1 Step 4 — Tiebreaker (only if needed)

Ask one branched question based on which two phases tied. The user picks the closer fit.

**Phase A tiebreaker (stages 1-3):**
> *One more question. In the Ordinary World, some people haven't yet heard a call — settled, or restless but not yet moved. Others have heard it but are holding back. Others are refusing it outright. Which is closest to true right now?*
- Stage 1: settled, no signal
- Stage 2: the call has arrived
- Stage 3: refusing or postponing the call

**Phase B tiebreaker (stages 4-5):**
> *You're at the threshold. Have you found a guide or practice helping you cross — or have you committed fully, past the point of turning back?*
- Stage 4: found a mentor or practice, still learning
- Stage 5: committed, no turning back

**Phase C tiebreaker (stages 6-7):**
> *You're in the middle work. Are you still meeting the tests and finding allies — or have you begun to sense what's at the heart of it, the thing you've been approaching all along?*
- Stage 6: tests and allies, still on the path
- Stage 7: approaching the cave, the core work is near

**Phase D tiebreaker (stages 8-9):**
> *The hard part. Are you in the ordeal itself — inside the loss, the dissolution, the hardest moment — or are you holding something on the other side of it, trying to understand what came through?*
- Stage 8: inside the ordeal
- Stage 9: holding the reward, integrating what came through

**Phase E tiebreaker (stages 10-12):**
> *You're returning. Is the path back still bumpy — old life pulling, new self not yet settled? Or have you been tested and held through it? Or are you finding yourself as a guide for others?*
- Stage 10: road back, still integrating
- Stage 11: tested and held, near the threshold
- Stage 12: offering to others

### Q1 Step 5 — Render the result

**ASCII arc first**, with the user's phase marked:

```
  Departure        Initiation            Return
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────
  [ A ] ──── [ B ] ──── [ C ] ──── [ D ] ──── [ E ]
 Ordinary   Threshold  Tests &    Ordeal &    Return
  World               Inmost Cave  Reward
```

Place a `●` below the user's phase. Then the stage name and a 2-3 sentence description grounded in what the user actually said. Held as hypothesis.

End with the confidence label and CTA toward Q2:
> *This is a rough placement — a first location, not a verdict. When you're ready, the deeper reflection (Q2) looks at the allies, the ordeal, and what's beginning to come back with you.*

### Q1 Step 6 — Write the Q1 reading

Prepend a Q1 reading note below the file header of `context-library/maps/heros-journey.md`:

```markdown
## Q1 Reading — YYYY-MM-DD

**Confidence:** rough
**Phase:** [Letter and phase name]
**Stage:** [Stage number and name if tiebreaker was used; otherwise "within Phase [X]"]

[The 2-3 sentence reading]

---
```

If the file does not exist, create it with this section and a note that the full map is generated by `/maps heros-journey`.

### Q1 Step 7 — Offer to refresh the map (NUM-116)

After writing the Q1 reading, check whether a fuller Hero's Journey map already exists (map content beyond the reading sections — a `## Where you appear to be`, prose, or prior `/maps` output).

If it does, offer it as a natural next step:

> *"You already have a Hero's Journey map. Want me to refresh it with this placement to raise its confidence? I'll merge what you just answered with what's already there from your notes and conversations."*

If yes, run the heros-journey refresh (same logic as `/maps heros-journey`), merging the new Q1 reading with existing harvested notes and prior readings. If no map exists yet, skip silently.

## Q2 — Deeper dive: allies, the ordeal, the elixir (NUM-118)

**Gate:** Q2 needs Q1 first. If `heros-journey.md` has no `## Q1 Reading`, say: *"Q2 builds on the placement. Let's start with Q1 — it takes about five minutes."* and run Q1 instead.

Q2 goes into the relational and transformational dimensions of the chapter the user is in. It's reflective, not forced-choice — invite real answers and reflect a phrase back when you synthesise. Ask one at a time.

### Q2 Step 1 — Open

> *Let's go deeper into this chapter of the journey. Less about where you are on the arc, more about what's happening inside it — who's with you, what you're facing, and what's beginning to change.*
>
> *Six questions. Take them slowly. Free text — say as much or as little as is true.*

### Q2 Step 2 — The six questions

**1. Mentors and allies.** *Who has appeared in this chapter — a person, a teacher, a practice, an inner figure — and what have they offered you?*

**2. The ordeal.** *What's the hardest thing you're facing right now, or recently faced? The challenge that feels like the centre of it.*

**3. What's being released.** *What are you being asked to let go of — an identity, a story, a relationship, a way of being? What's the death-and-rebirth threshold here?*

**4. Early signs of transformation.** *What's different about how you see yourself or the world, even slightly, compared to before this chapter began?*

**5. The emerging elixir.** *If you've come through any of it: what insight, gift, or new capacity are you carrying — something you might bring back to others or to your ordinary life?*

**6. What's still unresolved.** *What part of this chapter is still open — the question you can't answer yet?*

### Q2 Step 3 — Render the deepened reading

Synthesise into a short reading (4-6 sentences) that names: who is walking with them, the shape of the ordeal, what's being released, the early transformation, and the elixir if present. Mirror their actual language. Re-mark the arc if Q2 suggests they've moved a stage since Q1, and say so explicitly (*"Q1 placed you approaching the cave; what you've described here sounds like you're inside the ordeal now"*).

Close with one question worth sitting with, drawn from their "still unresolved" answer. Confidence label: **medium**.

### Q2 Step 4 — Write the Q2 reading

Prepend a `## Q2 Reading — YYYY-MM-DD` section to `heros-journey.md` (above the Q1 reading) with: **Confidence: medium**, the (possibly updated) phase/stage, the 4-6 sentence synthesis, and a short bullet list capturing allies, ordeal, what's being released, and the elixir.

### Q2 Step 5 — Offer to refresh the map (NUM-116)

Same as Q1 Step 7: if a fuller map exists, offer to refresh it, merging Q2 with Q1 and any harvested notes. The merged map should raise the confidence in its header.

## Q3 — Settled reading (20-30 min)

The longest reflection: a settled placement on the 12-stage arc, woven with the user's own words. 20 questions across six sections — structured (~60% of signal: multiple-choice + Likert) plus open-ended (~40%). Present one at a time, no commentary between. **Stage tags (S1-S12) on options are for your scoring only — never show them.** If Q1/Q2 exist, show the latest placement before starting. Standalone-capable. The terminal handles pause/resume naturally.

### Q3 Section 1 — Settling in (2 open-ended)

**1.** *Take a breath. In your own words: where do you find yourself in your life right now?* (hint: what's alive, what's heavy, what's quiet)
**2.** *If you had to name what your life is asking of you right now, what would you say?*

### Q3 Section 2 — Where you stand (4 multiple-choice)

**3. The most accurate metaphor for where you are:** Standing in a familiar room *(S1)* / Hearing a knock at the door *(S2)* / Pretending I didn't hear the knock *(S3)* / Studying a map before the trip *(S4)* / Walking out and not looking back *(S5)* / On a long road, learning who walks with me *(S6)* / Standing at the mouth of a cave *(S7)* / Inside the cave, in the dark *(S8)* / Walking out with something in my hand *(S9)* / Coming back to the village *(S10)* / Tested at the gate, and let through *(S11)* / Sitting by the fire, telling the story *(S12)*
**4. Your relationship to your old self (2-5 years ago):** I am that person, more or less *(S1)* / I sense the gap but I haven't moved much *(S2-3)* / I'm beginning to see them as separate from who I'm becoming *(S4)* / I've stepped past them; there's grief and relief *(S5-6)* / I'm meeting old patterns of theirs I thought I'd left *(S7)* / Something of them is dying right now *(S8)* / They feel distant; someone newer is here *(S9-10)* / I integrated what they carried *(S11-12)*
**5. Where the journey lives in your body right now:** I don't really notice my body *(S1-3)* / Tension I'm just starting to feel *(S4-5)* / Energy moving in places it didn't before *(S6-7)* / Heaviness, exhaustion, or grief *(S8)* / Lightness or unfamiliar spaciousness *(S9-10)* / Settled; body and life align *(S11-12)*
**6. What you do with most of your time, honestly:** Living the life I built, not asking too much of it *(S1)* / Going through the motions while something stirs *(S2-3)* / Studying, learning, building the practice *(S4-5)* / Practising; the work has its own claim on my time *(S6)* / Preparing for something I sense is coming *(S7)* / Holding what's hard *(S8)* / Reorganising my life around what I learned *(S9-10)* / Living simply, available to others *(S11-12)*

### Q3 Section 3 — Inner work depth (3 Likert + 2 open-ended)

**7. Likert (1-5):** *I have a practice or teacher I return to even when it's uncomfortable.* (high → S5+)
**8. Likert (1-5):** *I can name patterns in myself I couldn't see a year ago.* (high → S6+)
**9. Likert (1-5):** *Something I once relied on for identity has been taken from me.* (high → S8)
**10.** *Open-ended: What's the most honest thing you can say about your inner work right now? What's working, what's stuck, what's surprising you?*
**11.** *Open-ended: Describe a moment in the last year when you felt most yourself, and one when you felt most lost.* (the distance between them reveals the stage)

### Q3 Section 4 — Shadow, loss, ego (3 MC + 1 open-ended)

**12. When you think of your shadow (the disowned parts of yourself):** That language doesn't really land *(S1-2)* / I've heard it but haven't met mine *(S3-4)* / I've started naming a few *(S5-6)* / I'm sitting with one I've avoided for years *(S7)* / I'm being undone by one *(S8)* / I've integrated something that used to run me *(S9-10)* / My shadow is a familiar companion now *(S11-12)*
**13. In the last 18 months, the most accurate description of loss:** No major loss *(S1)* / Small endings I've been avoiding *(S2-3)* / I've let go of things I thought defined me *(S5-6)* / I've lost something I didn't think I could survive losing *(S8)* / The losses turned into openings *(S9-10)* / Loss feels like part of how life moves now *(S11-12)*
**14. When the hardest thing in your life is mentioned, you:** Don't think about it much *(S1)* / Avoid it when possible *(S3)* / Try to understand it *(S4-6)* / Sit with it before reacting *(S7)* / Let it teach me, even when it levels me *(S8-9)* / Witness it as part of a longer movement *(S10-12)*
**15.** *Open-ended: Name something you've lost, given up, or been stripped of, and what's grown in its place, if anything.*

### Q3 Section 5 — Allies and community (1 MC + 1 open-ended)

**16. Other people on the path:** I'm mostly alone with this *(S1-3)* / I have a few teachers or guides I lean on *(S4-5)* / I have peers walking similar terrain *(S6-7)* / I've felt the existential aloneness even with company around *(S8)* / I find myself naturally listening to people earlier on *(S10-11)* / Holding space for others has become part of my life *(S12)*
**17.** *Open-ended: Who are your teachers, allies, and inner critics right now? People, books, traditions, or voices in your head.*

### Q3 Section 6 — Integration and service (2 MC + 1 open-ended)

**18. The relationship between insight and your daily life:** Most of my life is unexamined *(S1-2)* / I have insights but they don't change much *(S3-4)* / I'm slowly translating insight into how I live *(S5-7)* / The work is rearranging what I do, eat, who I see *(S8-10)* / Insight and life have collapsed into the same thing *(S11-12)*
**19. When you think about offering what you've learned to others:** I have nothing to offer *(S1-3)* / Maybe one day, not now *(S4-7)* / I'm hesitant, I don't trust I've earned it *(S8-9)* / I'm beginning, in small ways *(S10)* / It's becoming part of who I am *(S11-12)*
**20.** *Open-ended (closing): If a future version of you, five years older and wiser, sat across from you right now, what would they say to you? And what would you say back?*

### Q3 Step 7 — Resolve and render

- Score structured questions to their mapped stages (range mappings split evenly), ~60% of the weight; the open-ended responses contribute the remaining ~40% (read affect, agency, self-language, loss/growth, mentorship). Resolve a single settled stage with a **tight halo on only the one adjacent stage** (the tightest of the three questionnaires).
- Render a personalised reading: sharp pin at the resolved stage, the stage name, a 4-6 sentence description woven with the user's own phrases, then three short paragraphs — **"What's true here," "What this stage is asking of you," "What comes next."** Confidence: **settled**.

### Q3 Step 8 — Write the Q3 reading and close

- Prepend a `## Q3 Reading — YYYY-MM-DD` section (above prior readings) to `heros-journey.md` with: **Confidence: settled**, the stage + tight halo, the woven reading, and 2-3 quotable phrases.
- Offer to refresh the Hero's Journey map (merging Q3 with prior readings + harvested notes; raises confidence).
- Seed 2-3 reflection prompts as conversation starters.

## Tone reminders

- Wise, sincere, grounded. Witness before wisdom.
- No em dashes. No "unlock," "transform," "breakthrough," "holistic."
- Numina lexicon: threshold, witness, pattern, shadow.
- A placement on the arc is a mirror, not a destination.
- The user may be in multiple phases at once. Name it when the data shows it.
